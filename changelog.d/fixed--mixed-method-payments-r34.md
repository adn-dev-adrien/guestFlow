- **Split payments, round-34 fixes** (spec `mixed-method-payments.md` rules 42-49, 2026-11-09). A price cut after a split payment that left a reste or a geste no longer raises a false trop-perçu: the paid bucket keeps what it received, and the cut lowers the reste, then the geste. Lowering a split-paid complement on the fiche no longer asks to delete a refund that does not exist. A split payment matched at the bank can no longer be taken back, and a payment whose plain receipt is already matched can no longer be split, so no bank line is left orphaned. On a cancelled stay, the caisse-interne share of a split solde is refundable in the caisse interne. The « Résumé tarifaire » reads « Perçu en partie » while a reste is due; an empty last line at 0 no longer blocks « Encaisser »; a correction left open on an undone payment no longer hides the new one's buttons. Every boot re-checks the split payments (after a rollback then a roll-forward); the VAT regime switch lists the months of a split payment.
- plugin:sas Each opening of the check-in/check-out dialog starts with one method, so a check-out with nothing to collect after a split one completes; « Plusieurs moyens » is no longer offered to reception, and a crafted split is refused in French.
