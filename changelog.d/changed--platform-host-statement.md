- **Platform stays are booked from the host statement** (spec `platform-host-statement-and-commission-vat.md`, 2026-11-26). The « Paiement plateforme » block takes the host side of the platform's statement in two figures — « Location (relevé) » and « Options (relevé) » — and never the guest total: the tourist tax and the fees the guest pays the platform are out of it. The option lines of the fiche follow « Options (relevé) » by themselves (a ménage left offered that the statement bills becomes paid, and the block says so). The « Taxe de séjour » box reads the statement by the platform's mode: informational where the platform keeps the tax, « versée avec le virement » where it reverses it — then GuestFlow's own tax stays the right one for `46710000` and the office declaration, and the gap with the platform's figure goes into the accommodation. « Calculer la commission » is host amount + tax paid with the transfer − transfer − acompte commission; under the block the server words the closing line, the tax gap, the commission's booking, a warning when the commission rate is more than one point off the platform's, and the few refusals. Gîtes de France, Airbnb and Booking intakes write the statement from their source (SDK 2.37.0: `ctx.core.reservations.touristTaxMode`, and `platformCommission` takes the platform's key). Airbnb moves to « nous reverse » after the release (an operator setting, not part of this change).
