- **« Déclarée » waits for a stay paid and over** (specs/tourist-tax-declared-checkbox.md rule 8, D16): a new tick, main row or added guests' row, is refused with 409 unless the stay is paid and its end date is past; the page greys the checkbox and says so. Withdraws D14's « a tax not yet collected can be declared », the plan row stays where it is. Stored ticks are not rewritten; unticking stays possible.
- **A plan pins the tourist tax it collected** (specs/tourist-tax-matches-the-office-calculation.md rule 19, D17): a plan fully paid, or a paid installment holding a tax share, freezes the tax as a paid solde does, so a rate move no longer asks the guest for more on an unrelated save. A party change still re-prices.
- **« Taxe de séjour en complément » cannot go on over a tax already collected** (specs/force-item-to-complement.md rule 20): 409 `TOURIST_TAX_ALREADY_COLLECTED` and a caption on the fiche; it billed the tax twice and rewrote a booked entry.
- **An added tax grown after the tick has its own row** (specs/platform-added-guests-tourist-tax.md rule 29): the declared row keeps what it told the commune, the difference is offered for declaration.
