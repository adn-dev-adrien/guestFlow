- **A forged mail can no longer freeze the server.** The mailbox reader skips any part over 256 KB and turns HTML into text in linear time (the image pattern was quadratic: 160 KB took 4 s), the Gîtes de France supplements are read chunk by chunk instead of with a backtracking pattern, and the Airbnb parser caps line and message lengths. An unauthenticated mail that does not parse is now stored as `ignored` (out of every list) instead of being re-read and re-parsed at every pass for 30 days.
