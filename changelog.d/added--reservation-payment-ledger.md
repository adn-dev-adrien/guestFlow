- **Payments as a status ledger on the reservation fiche** (specs/reservation-payment-ledger.md): one row per money movement — acompte, solde (or « Versement <plateforme> »), complements, notes en séjour, refunds and trop-perçu, caution received and returned — each with its amount, its state in words (« À percevoir avant le 23/08 », « En retard depuis… », « Perçu le 12/08 · lien de paiement »), one action and « Reste à percevoir » / « Tout est perçu » at the bottom (the same figure as the Finance « Suivi opérationnel »). The server builds every row.
- **Acompte and solde are settable from their row**: the acompte between 0 and the accommodation, the solde between the tourist tax and the pre-arrival total (the split moves, not the price); once the acompte is perceived, a different solde is a different accommodation price, found by re-running the whole pricing engine, to the nearest reachable cent, never below the acompte received. The consequence shows before « Valider »; an invalid amount is refused in French under the field.
- « Marquer perçu » opens a small form under the row (date of the day, « Caisse interne » on the complements only); « Marquer reçu » on a platform payout asks for the transfer, shows the gap with the expected net and proposes the commission. Undoing a payment asks for confirmation, naming the date and the accounting entry.
