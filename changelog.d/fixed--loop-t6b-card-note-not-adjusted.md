- **A note paid online can no longer be adjusted from the fiche** (`specs/guest-app.md` rule 113 a): « Modifier la note » refuses a new amount or « Caisse interne » on an app or shop card order, as « Reporter au départ » already did; only its date can change, and the fiche no longer draws those actions on such a note. Before, lowering it asked the guest again for money paid online, and « Caisse interne » took card money off the books.
