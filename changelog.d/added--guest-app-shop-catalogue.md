- **Guest app « Mon séjour », slice B (server)** (spec `guest-app.md` rules 22-27, 27bis). The sell switch of an option or a resource lives in the plugin (`GET /api/guest-app/items`, `GET/PUT /api/guest-app/items/:kind/:id`, a non-sellable price type refused with `NOT_SELLABLE`), and `GET /public/v1/guest/shop` serves the stay's lodging catalogue: per-lodging prices and labels, « Inclus », free units (own-channel stays) and free minutes left, « Déjà inclus dans votre séjour », the breakfast's mornings, categories, open exactly while the fiche takes mid-stay extras. The bath tile now follows the shop, and the gate tile follows the `gate-access` plugin. SDK 2.22.0 also exposes `publicCatalogProjection` (the option / resource projections, moved out of `website-booking`, which re-exports them unchanged), `midStayExtrasWindow`, and the pricing engine's `applyFreeUnitsToLine`, `getApplicableOptions`, `getApplicableResources`.
