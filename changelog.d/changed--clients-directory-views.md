- **Clients: a directory that reads at a glance** (specs `clients-upcoming-past-directory.md` rules 11-22, `clients.md` rule 15). « À venir » opens first; « Passés » and a new « Sans séjour » view (stay-less clients, with « Nettoyer les clients ») sit beside it, each with its count. Rows are grouped by when (En séjour, 7 / 30 prochains jours, Plus tard; years for the past), say where and when the guest comes (« Arrive dans 9 j », « Parti il y a 3 sem. »), show the platform and the number of stays, and the e-mail and phone are links. A search looks in the three views and offers the one that matches. One « Trier » select (date de séjour / nom) replaces the column headers, so sorting also works on a phone. The per-row delete icon is gone: deletion goes through the client sheet. `GET /api/clients/directory` takes `view` and answers `groups`, `counts`, `orphanCount` and `elsewhere` (`items` is kept for one release).
