- The gift-voucher plugin's settings (amount bounds, validity, VAT rate, price ideas) move into one `lodging` campaign « Bon cadeau séjour » (`gift_voucher_campaigns`, slug `sejour`): on sale when the voucher page address was set, otherwise a draft. `campaignId`, `contents` (default `{"kind":"lodging"}`) and `vatLines` are added to `gift_vouchers` and `gift_voucher_orders`; every existing voucher and order points to the campaign. No row is deleted; the migration is idempotent. The five settings are no longer declared by the plugin (the page « Bons cadeaux » edits them on the campaign from now on).
- Visual library and refunds: `gift_voucher_visuals`, `gift_voucher_visual_versions` and `gift_voucher_refunds` are created; `gift_vouchers` gains `visualVersions`, `cancelReason` and `cancelForced`. The terms a voucher order accepted (`gift_voucher_orders.termsVersionId` / `termsAcceptedAt`) are copied into the core's `terms_acceptances` (activity `lodging`, subject `voucherOrder`, channel `site`) when that table exists; the columns stay, unread and no longer written. The gift-voucher plugin no longer declares its mail template settings (`mailSubjectFr`, `mailBodyFr`, `mailSubjectEn`, `mailBodyEn`) nor `bookingUrl`; stored values stay in `plugin_settings`, unread. No row is deleted.
