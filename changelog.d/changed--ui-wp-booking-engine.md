- **Website booking engine — WordPress plugin 1.16.0** (UI/UX review 2026-10-18, decisions 2, 3, 4 and 16; specs/wp-booking-widget-redesign.md rules 29–33, specs/wordpress-plugin.md rules 28–29):
  - The chosen stay always reads as selected: hover styles only for a mouse, so a tapped departure no longer stays washed out on a phone — even under the legacy `gf-booking.php` still on the Solio Pi; past days, taken nights and free nights look different, with a legend. When the two months on screen hold no bookable arrival (taken nights, minimum stay, arrival days), « Prochaine disponibilité : mercredi 31 mars 2027 » offers to jump there.
  - Refusals land on the field: every missing or malformed contact field is marked under itself at once, the first one gets the focus, in the page's language; the server's refusals are mapped to the same fields. The engine announces each refusal as a `gf:refused` event so the Solio drawer can show the screen that holds the field (repo copy of the mu-plugin updated, not deployed).
  - On a phone (coarse pointer or narrow viewport, block under 900 px), the total and the button sit in a bar at the bottom; the button follows the step (« Choisir les dates », « Continuer », then the submit). The bar holds even in a theme that clips the page's overflow. On a wide block the summary follows the scroll in a right-hand column with its own button. The two times get a row of their own.
  - One accent colour drives the whole engine (the site's, else the theme's primary, else Maison fir green), darkened where it carries white text so it reaches 4.5 : 1; the stale MUI blue default and the calendar block's blue days are gone, and the site's own `.gf-btn` / `.gf-section` / `.gf-cta` are no longer touched.
