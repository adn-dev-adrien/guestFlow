- **Plugins — phase 1: the plugin SDK, and five plugins moved out of the core** (spec `plugins-phase-1-sdk.md`, 2026-09-28). Météo-France, school holidays, Google Agenda, tariff recipes and the Sowel gate access now live in their own folders (`server/src/plugins/<id>`, `client/src/plugins/<id>`) and register into the core: routes (same URLs), tables, settings, jobs, reservation events, email variables, SAS data and screen slots. Nothing changes for the operator. New on **Paramètres › Plugins**: uninstalling one of these five can **erase its data** (« Effacer aussi ses données », the list of what goes, « Désinstaller et effacer »); prices, payments and the tariff change journal are never touched — a recipe's seasons simply become « Manuelle ». The keypad code of the property now belongs to the SAS: it shows in the arrival SAS even without Sowel, and its field in Établissement follows the SAS plugin. Each plugin's screens are downloaded by the browser only when used. +37 server tests, +18 client tests, +1 E2E.
