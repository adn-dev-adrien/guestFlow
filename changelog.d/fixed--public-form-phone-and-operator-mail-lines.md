- **Public booking form**: the phone is validated at the boundary (`phoneInvalid`, 8–15 digits, `+` and separators only) and the e-mail must be a single address, so a line break in a field can no longer forge a line of the owner's notification e-mail. Every value interpolated in the plain-text operator e-mails (site devis, digest, iCal reservation, conflict) has its control characters and line separators replaced by a space (website-booking 1.8.4).
