- **Option and resource writes are validated on the server** (spec `options-resources-ux.md` rules 15, 16, 21). An option with an empty name, a negative or non-numeric per-property price (formerly skipped silently) or a percentage above 100 is refused with `422 VALIDATION_FAILED` and per-field details, as is a resource with an empty name, a fractional quantity, a negative price or a closing time before its opening time (formerly `400`). The overrides, defaults and bath-mat quantities of properties outside an option's scope are dropped on save by the server instead of the browser.
