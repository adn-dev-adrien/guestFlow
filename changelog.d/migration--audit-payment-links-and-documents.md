- **Payment links and property documents: additive columns, one move at boot.** `payment_links` keeps `qontoPaymentLinkId` / `qontoPaymentId` next to `providerLinkId` / `providerPaymentId` (added and copied from the old names on upgrade, kept equal by triggers); nothing is lost. Property documents already uploaded are moved from `uploads/` to `private-documents/` (beside it) and their `documents.filePath` rewritten to `private/<name>`; a missing file is logged and its row left alone. This move is not undone by a rollback (self-update rule 39).
