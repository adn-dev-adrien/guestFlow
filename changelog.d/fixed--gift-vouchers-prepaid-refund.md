- **Gift vouchers — a prepaid part is never refunded in money.** The refund register of a stay no longer counts what a prepaid instrument (a gift voucher) paid as money to give back: a cancelled stay paid by voucher and card offers only the card money, the cancellation indemnity is taken from the card first, and a stay paid only by voucher owes nothing in money (`specs/mixed-method-payments.md` rule 64). A code refused on a booking request is now counted as a failed try (the refusal used to roll the try back with the request), and the voucher PDF names the right missing setting (the booking page address).
