- **A save no longer rewrites a stay that was not edited** (spec `reservation-save-keeps-pricing.md`, audit 2026-10-28). « Enregistrer » on an untouched fiche kept re-pricing it with today's engine: paid stays moved by a few euros, snapshots and custom option ids were rewritten, the history blamed the operator. Now the money, the lines and the snapshots stay put unless a pricing input changed; Enregistrer is disabled on a clean form.
- **One way to price a stored stay.** Installment plans, restamped buckets, the trop-perçu baseline, the contrib capture and the tourist-tax declaration replay the stay exactly as its fiche saves it (tariff snapshot, deposit opt-out and overrides, commissions, platform settings, mid-stay state).
- **Money collected above the stay total is reported.** A collected arrival complement left above what the stay now asks (a line moved out of it) is a trop-perçu, not an anonymous « Complément d'arrivée » line.
- **The fiche warns** about a platform commission far above the channel's rate and about a tourist tax stored differently from what is now computed (« Recalculer »).
