- **Vigilance Météo-France now requires Arrivée et départ guidés** (`weather-alerts` 1.3.0, spec `plugin-dependencies.md` rule 20). A space that runs the weather plugin without the SAS plugin sees it « Nécessite Arrivée et départ guidés » (installed, not loaded) after the update; its settings and data are kept. Installing the SAS plugin from the Plugins page registers the weather plugin again at once, without a restart. No schema change.
