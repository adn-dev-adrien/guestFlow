- **The site's session options are the « Options proposées » list** (spec `site-outside-resource-booking.md` §3.H, 2026-11-26, connector 1.26.0, SDK 2.36.0, hourly-resources 1.12.0, gift-vouchers 1.9.0). « Ajouter à la séance » on the website offers the resource's own suggested-articles list, in the operator's order: no second list, nothing to migrate. The tab « Proposés à l'arrivée » of Options et ressources is renamed « Options proposées » (the old link still opens it, and the settings search finds both names); each card says where its list shows (« Sur le site » for an activity sold online, « À l'arrivée seulement » otherwise) and each row shows its unit price. « Ajouter des articles » opens a search and a checklist grouped by category that stays open, so several articles are ticked in one pass; an article the engine does not sell outside a stay is listed greyed with its reason, and one already listed and now refused is drawn in warning (« … Retirer pour enregistrer. »). The save of a list for an outside customer refuses it (`PUT /api/settings/suggested-articles/:kind/:id` → 400 `ARTICLE_NEEDS_STAY`); `GET /api/suggested-articles/catalogue` takes `context=<kind>:<id>` and `all=1`. On the site each option now carries its unit and the engine's price for each quantity, computed by the same function as a voucher's add-ons (`utils/addOnPricing.js`), and the limit reads « N au maximum par séance ».
