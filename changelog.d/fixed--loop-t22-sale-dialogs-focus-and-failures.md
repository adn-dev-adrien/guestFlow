- **Sale dialogs keep the focus, the typed text and a way out** (specs `plugins-phase-1-sdk.md` rule 54, `outside-booking-sas.md` rule 54, `pricing-components.md` rule 117, readings of 2026-11-18). The billing address of the « Facture » block can no longer be typed into while the invoice question is pending: a refusal, « Caisse interne » or a silent answer used to remove the field being typed in, losing the text and dropping the focus on the page. When a server answer, « Réessayer » or a collected line removes the control that holds the focus, the focus now stays in the dialog, on the nearest block still shown. The check-in's « Séjour » step and the check-out's extinguisher page say « Montant indisponible » with « Réessayer » when the preview fails, instead of « — » for ever, and the stay's settlement buttons wait for an amount. « Vente hors séjour » offers « Réessayer » when its total or its articles could not be loaded.
- **Nothing moves when the answer comes** (same readings). « Vente hors séjour » opens as a full-height sheet from a tablet up, so its « Total » row, its VAT line and its buttons stay where they are when the total arrives (before: up to 101 px); an article not priced yet shows « — » in its place; the outside visit's summary puts several VAT rates on one line; a booking paid now reads « Enregistrer et facturer » as soon as the « Facture » box is shown ticked. On a refusal or a failure some content may still move: that is accepted.
- **The check-in / check-out wizard keeps the keyboard in place** (spec `arrival-departure-sas.md` §3.0, reading of 2026-11-18). Every page change, forward or back, puts the focus on the new page's title: it no longer falls on the page, and it no longer stays on a footer button that the next page reuses for another answer (« Suivant » becoming « Valider et terminer »).
