- **Bank reconciliation — split payments, grouped payouts, automatic confirmation, bank line on « Échéances » (online-payment plugin 1.4.0, SDK 2.9.0; lot 2 of `specs/reconciliation-assisted-matching.md`).** A guest who pays an échéance (or the whole stay) in two or three transfers carrying their name is proposed as one proposal over several credits. Among equal installments only the earliest due one is proposed. A grouped platform payout now also combines stays whose payout the fiche does not carry yet (gross minus the platform's commission, 1 % tolerance per estimate, one payout run of 14 days): one estimate → « probable », two or more → « à vérifier » with « Répartir et confirmer » — shares are typed, previewed, and refused until they make the credit to the cent (`SHARES_DO_NOT_SUM`). After each read of the account (8-hour job, « Actualiser ») the « sûr » single exact matches confirm themselves — paid flag and date only, never a payout or an amount — with an « auto » badge, an history entry « Rapprochement bancaire automatique » and « Défaire » (which also discards the pair); the setting « Confirmer automatiquement les correspondances sûres » turns it off. The home's « Échéances » card shows « Virement trouvé : … » with « Confirmer », or « Rien sur le compte », through the new generic SDK hook `ctx.paymentDeadlineDecorator` (inert without a plugin). `late` receipts no longer count in « Écarts de règlement » (the « Échéances » card already shows them).
