- **Reservations — creation no longer fails after a cancellation** (spec `reservation-number-and-search.md` rules 13-14, 2026-10-18). A cancelled stay keeps its number, but the generator skipped it: once the latest number of the month belonged to a cancelled stay, every new reservation, devis conversion and iCal import failed with HTTP 500 until the month changed, and the failed fiche creation left a numberless stay blocking the dates and exported in the iCal feed. The generator now counts every numbered row (numeric max), and a creation writes its row and lines in one transaction. +9 server tests.
