- **A fiche save no longer re-dates a stay's first lines.** A save stamped every unstamped line of the stay with its own date, including the lines written when the stay was booked; a receipt dated between the booking and that save (a transfer ticked with its own date) was then frozen without those lines, and its options money cut over no options line. Only the lines the save itself wrote take its date; the others keep the booking's (`specs/pricing-components.md` rule 100).
- **A guest-app card link pays only the order it was issued for.** After the guest app was erased and reinstalled, an old open link could pay a new order that had taken its id. The order id counter now also restarts above the orders an open link names, and a link whose order points to another link sells nothing and owes its money back (`specs/plugin-removal-resilience.md` rule 23).
