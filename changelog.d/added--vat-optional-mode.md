- **« Sans TVA »: a dated VAT regime** (`specs/vat-optional-mode.md`). Paramètres › TVA & exercice › « Changer de régime… »: an installation that is not subject to VAT (neither a company nor with an accountant) runs without any VAT, and can change regime on a date — reservations made from…, stays starting from…, or a correction of every reservation. A preview dialog announces what changes (reservations, tourist tax, open payment links, accounting months) before confirming; the history is permanent and a change not yet in use can be cancelled.
- Without VAT, the prices the client pays do not move: the PDF quote, the pricing summary, the payment links, the financial follow-up and the accounting export lose their VAT lines, and the chosen legal mention (art. 293 B or art. 261 D 4° of the CGI, in French and English) is printed instead. The proportional tourist tax follows the price paid; a frozen or declared tax never changes.
- The regime is a core setting, independent of the accounting plugin; each reservation keeps the regime under which it was sold. Plugin SDK 2.10.0 (`coreModule('vatRegimeModel')`), accounting export 1.0.16.
- A regime change recalculates and saves the proportional tourist tax of the stays concerned (frozen or declared tax excepted) through a targeted write (only the tax and the amounts that depend on it change, the rest of the fiche stays identical); the preview announces exactly what will be saved, and open payment links whose amount changes are abandoned. A change that rewrites collected money or accounting months asks for a confirmation whatever its scope; a change dated in the future can be cancelled until its date comes.
- The VAT rate (Paramètres › TVA & exercice) is now the rate of the regime period in force: changing it recalculates the reservations of that period, not those of an earlier one. With a single period (every installation before its first regime change) nothing moves, to the cent.
