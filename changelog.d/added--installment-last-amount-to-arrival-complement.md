- Installment plan: the last installment's amount can be typed too. When no installment adjusts any more the plan is closed, and what it does not cover (« Reste de l'échéancier ») is paid with the arrival complement at once — on the fiche, in the SAS arrival step (the unpaid installments, then the complement with that line) and in the J-2 / arrival emails — booked on the accommodation like the auto-gap. A typed amount that would put the plan above the stay is refused; a price rise goes to the complement, a drop lowers it (and, beyond the plan and before any payment, frees the last installment). « Ajuster automatiquement » on the last row re-opens the plan; the card ends with « Reste à régler à l'arrivée ».
- Installment plan: a plan can be created or resized on a stay whose acompte is already collected (cash included). It splits what is left of the pre-arrival amount, the acompte keeps its amount, date and accounting entry and shows as a paid row on the card; a collected solde still refuses it.
