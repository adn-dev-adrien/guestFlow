  prices, the « a week is four nights » discount curve and the rules that carve the year (April,
  May-to-October, the July and August shoulders around the summer core, the year-end block, and the
  réveillon carved out of it) now live in one declarative document, so 2027 and every year after are
  derived instead of painted by hand. Public-holiday long weekends go up one rank — capped at Haute —

  target** it lacked — the own-channel price less the 5 % engine fee — so every platform's price is
  grossed up from what the Domaine actually collects rather than from a price that already contained
  the margin. Prices are unchanged except where the grid contradicted itself: **Très haute saison's
  curve is repaired** (its stored tiers priced a week at 1 794,75 € while its own 15th-night tier
  implied 2 150 €, and its 6th night at 21,50 €), 1 January joins the year-end block as 1 January of
  the following year already did, April starts its low season on the 1st rather than on the first
  Saturday, and Très haute's base rounds 537,50 € → 538 € so the direct row can reproduce it to the
  euro. A new **Nouvel An** season covers the nights of 30 and 31 December; **its rate is provisional**
  and those two nights are not to be published on a channel until it is settled. +6 server tests. The

  then.
