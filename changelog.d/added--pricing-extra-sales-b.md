- **Sell a product on an existing stay, one detail everywhere (pricing refonte, PR B)**: the stay now offers « ajouter un produit » (quote, then sale at the accepted price), moves an unpaid line between « à payer à l’arrivée » and « à payer au départ », and discounts or offers a line; a paid line cannot be lowered without confirmation and the excess is then owed back. The detail of every line (amount, source, moment, status, VAT) comes from one server payload. « Hébergement » and « Taxe de séjour » are lines of the stay, kept current at each save.
