- **The platform statement can be entered after the payout.** On a platform reservation, clicking the amount of the payout row now opens « Montant total payé par le client » and the commissions even when the payout is already received or the stay is over (locked fiche), without unlocking it. The correction is written at once, re-splits the received transfer between turnover and commission without moving the money, shows the net against the transfer before « Valider » (a gap is accepted with a warning), refuses any statement that would also change the tourist tax, the complement or the options, and appears in the history as « Relevé plateforme corrigé ». Every finished stay listed by « À vérifier » under « Séjours plateforme terminés sans « Paiement plateforme » » can now be cleared from its fiche. (`specs/platform-statement-after-payout.md`)
