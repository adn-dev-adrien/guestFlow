- **Sale outside a stay — the money given back leaves the books** (`specs/outside-booking-sas.md` rule 48, stabilisation loop t1 B-1). « Marquer remboursé » on a paid line offered, removed or cancelled now records the refund on that line, and the journal books an avoir of the sale on the refund day (revenue and VAT reversed, internal till excluded); before, the journal kept the line's revenue and VAT forever. Months already booked do not move.
