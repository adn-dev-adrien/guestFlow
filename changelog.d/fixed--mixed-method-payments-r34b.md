- **Split payments over several buckets, round-34 verification** (spec `mixed-method-payments.md` rules 50-53, 2026-11-09). The money received by one payment covering several buckets (the single arrival payment, the check-in and check-out SAS, the complement carrying the end of stay) is read as one sum: a later price cut lowers the reste or the geste first and no longer raises a false trop-perçu. Un-ticking only one bucket of such a payment, or switching one of its buckets to or from the caisse interne, is refused (« Paiement en plusieurs moyens : annulez le paiement unique en entier. » / « … passer par « Modifier ». ») instead of turning caisse-interne money into revenue or forgetting the reste. A geste on a split payment now comes off the accommodation in the refund register, as in the books, so a cancelled stay refunded in full no longer leaves tourist tax owed. A split end of stay no longer counts its departure lines (cleaning) as part of the price in the trop-perçu.
