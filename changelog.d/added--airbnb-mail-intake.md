- **Saisie Airbnb** (spec `airbnb-mail-intake.md`, plugin `airbnb-intake`, installed inactive). The « Réservation confirmée » mail Airbnb sends to the mailbox GuestFlow sends from fills the matching iCal fiche: the guest on a client of their own, the party, the confirmation code, the guest's message in the notes, and « Paiement plateforme » — brut, the tourist tax Airbnb keeps, the payout, and a commission equal to Airbnb's host fee. A fiche already edited gets an « Appliquer / Ignorer » banner instead; every banner links to the reservation on Airbnb, where the phone and e-mail are. To use it: activate « Saisie Airbnb » in Paramètres › Modules, paste each property's Airbnb listing link in Paramètres › Intégrations, then « Tester la connexion ».
