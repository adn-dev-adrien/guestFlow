- **Outside bookings — « Fiche passage », the preparation card and the prepaid lines of the arrival** (`specs/gift-voucher-campaigns.md` rules 35, 36, 53, hourly-resources 1.9.0). `GET /api/resource-bookings/:id/fiche` (administrator) answers the client (name, e-mail, phone, linked client), the session, each line of the sale with what paid it (the words of the instrument that paid in advance, « CB », « Espèces »…), the payments, the refund lines, the terms accepted (activity, version, date, channel), the arrival or the no-show and the journal newest first. Every event now writes a line of `standalone_sale_history`: created (who, channel), slot held, paid, prepaid tender applied, terms accepted (`ctx.terms.accept` with `subjectType: 'outsideBooking'`, its `onAccepted` writes the line), moved, arrival, option « non pris », no-show. The planning events of an outside booking carry `options` (every option of the sale, whoever paid it) and `badges` (the label of each prepaid tender); the reception reads both. The visit payload gains `prepaidSections` (the lines a prepaid tender paid, titled and marked with the words their instrument gave, counted as paid) and each such line a `prepaid` block; `POST /api/resource-bookings/:id/visit/lines/:lineId/not-taken` marks an element paid in advance as not taken (recorded, journaled, never refunded, the instrument told through its generic `unused` call), and such a line can be neither offered nor removed (`LINE_PREPAID`). A generic booking offer (`ctx.resourceBookingOffer`): `freeSlots`, `quote`, `hold` / `release` (15 minutes), `bookOutside` (booking, sale and tenders in one transaction), with the `resource-holds-expire` job. Screens: « Passage » (`/passages/:id`, two columns from `md`, history below on a phone), the planning card's badge, options to prepare and « Ouvrir la fiche », and the arrival dialog's section of lines paid in advance with « Non pris » and « Ouvrir la fiche ».
