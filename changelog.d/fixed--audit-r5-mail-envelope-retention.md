- **Mail intakes (SDK 2.17.0):** a pass no longer keeps the sender-written envelopes of every unread unauthenticated mail in memory (it kept about 200 MB for 6 000 forged mails with 32 KB subjects); only small records are kept, and the Message-ID used as the row key and the subject are capped at 998 characters. The internal counters of `airbnb-intake` and `gites-de-france-intake` (`lastRunAt`, `unreadMails`, `unreadStreak`) are now `internal` settings: refused by `PUT /api/plugins/:id/settings` and hidden from `GET`. `airbnb-intake` 1.1.5 and `gites-de-france-intake` 1.4.7 ask for SDK 2.17.
