- **A website quote can no longer be paid at a stale price** (spec `public-online-payment.md` rules 14-15, audit 2026-10-28). Re-pricing a devis (operator save or the guest's request sent again) now retires its open payment links and deactivates them at the provider. A link paid for another amount than the quote asks (re-priced quote, or a full link minted before the tourist tax joined the amount) books the amount really received, leaves the shortfall due and alerts the admin instead of ticking the stay fully paid. Two concurrent `pay` calls mint one link; a second paid link of the same devis is a refund alert, not a second booking. The retirement leaves a history row, the save answers `retiredPaymentLinks`, a link paid before its retirement is converted and booked, and a stale link paid after a manual tick is no longer reported as a double payment.
