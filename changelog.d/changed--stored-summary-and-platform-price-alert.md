- **Pricing summary: the saved amounts look like the summary, and a platform price far from the grid is flagged** (spec `reservation-payment-ledger.md` rules 50-64). When the amounts saved on a fiche differ from today's calculation, the « Résumé tarifaire » now shows them in the usual card (options, resources, nightly detail, tourist-tax case, cascade, échéances) instead of a reduced one, with no notice about the calculation; « Actualiser tarifs » stays the one way to re-price. A single alert « Prix {plateforme} à vérifier » appears on an upcoming platform stay whose paid price is more than 15 % below or 25 % above (and at least 30 € away from) the « Prix plateformes » grid of its channel, with a « Voir la grille » link. `POST /api/reservations/calculate-price` returns `platformPriceCheck`; `storedSummary` is shaped like the quote.
