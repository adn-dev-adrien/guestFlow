- **« Validation en deux étapes »: the activation as every site does it** (specs/hosting-h2-account-security.md rules 38–53, replaces the « Second code » card): « Mon compte » shows a status and « Activer », which opens one dialog in three steps — password, authenticator app, backup codes. A computer shows the QR code with the key folded behind « Saisie manuelle de la clé »; a phone gets « Ouvrir dans l’appli d’authentification » and « Copier la clé » (the QR code folded). The entry carries « GuestFlow » and « company · email » in the app. The code is sent at its 6th digit and a wrong one says the tries left (then a clock hint); an activation expires after 15 minutes and « Annuler » leaves nothing behind. The email code is a fallback link under the app. « Terminé » waits until the backup codes are copied, downloaded or ticked as saved. Turned on, the card says the method, its date and the codes left (warning from 3), and « Changer d’appli » swaps the app without ever leaving the account unprotected. The dashboard card opens the dialog directly. The name is the same everywhere (card, dashboard, journal, Paramètres › Utilisateurs); the settings search still finds « Second code » and « 2FA ».
