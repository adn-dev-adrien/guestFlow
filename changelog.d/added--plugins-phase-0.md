- **Plugins — phase 0: the Plugins page, and what is not installed disappears** (spec `plugins-phase-0-foundation.md`, 2026-09-28). Twelve optional features become built-in plugins, driven from **Paramètres › Plugins** (tabs « Installés » / « Disponibles », search, Activer / Désactiver, Désinstaller in two clicks): hourly resources, linen, website booking, Sowel gate access, Neat, online payment (Qonto), Google Agenda, accounting export, guided arrival/departure (SAS), tariff recipes, school holidays, Météo-France. An inactive plugin leaves no entry point — menu, settings, dashboard alerts, reservation page, SAS steps, planning cards — its routes answer 404 `PLUGIN_INACTIVE` and its background jobs stop. Deactivating keeps every piece of data; money already recorded never changes. Deactivation is refused while a Qonto link is open or a reception-only / accountant-only account is active. +25 server tests, +20 client tests, +5 E2E.
