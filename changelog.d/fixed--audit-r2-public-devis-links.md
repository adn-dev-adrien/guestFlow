- Payment links of a quote converted into a stay now follow the stay: cancelling it abandons them, re-pricing the fiche retires the stale ones, and a payment on a cancelled stay is flagged for refund instead of ticking it paid. An acompte link and a full link of one record can no longer both stay payable; a payment landing while a link is being replaced is never overwritten, a retired link paid after its dates were taken still converts the quote, and an unreadable provider no longer deactivates a link that may be paid.
- A devis link paid after its stay was re-priced is checked against the stay: the trop-perçu shows on the fiche and the admin alert states the real direction and amount. Links of a converted devis are retired when it converts, when its stay changes, and by a one-time migration for those already stale. A full link is retired once the acompte is paid, and only the duplicated acompte part is refunded when both were paid. A refused or failed mint no longer cancels the other valid link, and the public status of a cancelled stay answers cancelled.
- WordPress plugin 1.18.1: the payment return page stops polling and reads « Cette réservation est annulée. » when the stay was cancelled.
