- **Establishment closures in the public iCal feed** (spec `ical-export-closures.md`, 2026-09-20). A property's `.ics` export now carries the closures that apply to it — its own plus the global ones — so Booking, Airbnb, Gîtes de France and GreenGo stop selling a period declared closed instead of having the incoming booking silently dropped by the sync guard. Closures that are already over are left out; the event reads `Fermeture — <label>`, carries no guest data, and keeps the closure's stored dates (start inclusive, end exclusive). +10 server tests.
