- **A platform stay of the `clientGrossAmount` era no longer books its commission as VAT** (specs/accounting-books-the-money-collected.md rule 12, accounting-export plugin 1.0.9). Ticking the solde of such a stay (Gîtes de France #13: 706 € net, 907,20 € paid by the guest) captured per-line contribs from the net solde; the export used them against a CA grossed up to 907,20 € and put the 201,20 € gap on the VAT line (`44571100 C265,38` on 641,82 € of revenue). A grossed-up entry now ignores its contribs and is pro-rated from the stored money: `70600000 C824,73 | 44571100 C82,47`. No month already collected changes on the Solio base.
