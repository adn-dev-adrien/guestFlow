- **Plugins**: twelve optional features become plugins driven from Paramètres › Plugins; what is not installed disappears from the menus. Plugins ship as signed packages from a registry and are installed, updated and rolled back from the Plugins page (an update restarts the instance, 7-day rollback). An inactive plugin's routes answer 404 `PLUGIN_INACTIVE`; deactivation is refused while a Qonto link is open or a reception-only or accountant-only account exists; activation and deactivation take requirements and dependants along under one confirmation, uninstall never cascades, a dependency cycle is refused (`REQUIREMENT_CYCLE`). Without `hourly-resources` nothing is sold by the hour; erasing it drops paid external bookings after a warning.
- **A new instance starts neutral**: the start assistant (`/demarrage`) sets company, first property and recommended plugins; neutral seeds replace the former Solio defaults.
- **Guest app « Mon séjour »** (plugin `guest-app`): one link per stay, a booklet studio, a shop with stock, delivery or pickup, bookable hourly slots, card orders and a PWA. Orders paid « sur la note » or at 0 € are written at once on the fiche as mid-stay extras; a card order paid late whose slots are gone shows « Payée · à rembourser ». Stock sales are never refused for staff or the website (stock may read −2), the app answers `OUT_OF_STOCK`.
- **Hourly resources** (plugin `hourly-resources`): a booking outside a stay shows its price line by line, takes a payment and an invoice issued in Qonto; resource cards show evening and external rates.
- **Laundry days** (plugin `linen` 1.2.0): several laundry trips a week and a turnaround of 1 to 14 days, applied from tomorrow; `GET /api/planning/laundry` and `/api/planning/linen-inventory` are removed.
- **SDK 2.x**: `ctx.onActivate`, price-line contributors, payment providers, invoice issuers, attention subjects; guarded in CI by a census of the mounted routes and a bundle budget.
