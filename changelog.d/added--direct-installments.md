- **Pay a direct reservation in several times** (spec `direct-installments.md`, 2026-10-27). The fiche of a direct reservation (own channels, `isDirectChannel`) gets an « Échéancier »: 2 to 12 installments, the first on the day it is created, the last at the usual solde date, the others evenly spaced; every date is editable while unpaid and every amount but the last unpaid one can be typed. The plan replaces the acompte and the solde (the arrival complement and the caution are untouched). Euros are whole, the last installment carries the cents. Before the first payment every change of the total recomputes the amounts; after it they are frozen — a rise is spread over the unpaid installments, a drop changes nothing and shows « Échéancier au-dessus du séjour ». Each installment is requested with its own Qonto link at its exact amount, through the new manual email « Demande de règlement d'une échéance » (FR + EN): one « à valider » entry per installment from its due date, or « Envoyer la demande » from the fiche. A paid link marks its installment (webhook and polling); a payment received another way is marked from the fiche with its date and mode. An installment unpaid three days after its due date reads « En retard » and joins the dashboard's payment-deadline card. A link replaced because its amount changed is now deactivated at the provider before the new one is minted. Each paid installment is its own accounting encaissement, its tourist tax shared in proportion. No automatic reminder and no cancellation for non-payment. +85 server tests, +17 client tests.
