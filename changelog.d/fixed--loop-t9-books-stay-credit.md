- **A line sold unpaid after a paid solde line was offered or discounted is paid by that money first** (`specs/pricing-components.md` rule 121, loop t9 B9-2): the sale API, the fiche and a deleted refund now end the same — the stay never asks for the new line while it still owes the freed money back. Once the arrival complement is collected, the refund of a solde line offered is proposed on « Prestations complémentaires » (70600010), not on the accommodation.
- **A solde line whose trop-perçu was refunded cannot be put back to billed** (rule 108 reading, B9-4): un-offering or removing its discount answers « Ligne remboursée : la revendre comme une nouvelle ligne. », as for a note-paid line; the fiche no longer offers the gesture. Before, the guest kept the line and the money.
- **Platform stays: the collected arrival complement's freed money pays what is still due, like a note** (rule 116 reading, B9-3), and offering a line that credit paid owes the credit back again. A caisse-interne refund of complement cash is no note money (B9-1): the rest of a mixed excess is proposed by transfer.
- **Two lines of one article keep their own routing inside the stay** (rule 85 reading, B9-5): the paid solde's beer no longer moves to the complement and is no longer asked again at departure when the same article was sold at the arrival.
