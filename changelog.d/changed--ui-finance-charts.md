- **Suivi financier, charts readable at a glance** (specs/finance-dashboard-redesign.md rules 43-46, specs/booking-pace.md rules 14-15; UI/UX review of 2026-10-18). The « faits marquants » no longer cut their text at 900 px; « meilleur mois » is chosen among the months already over, and before any the leading month is named « en tête des réservations ». « Revenu par mois » writes each month's total above its column, marks the current month and abbreviates months on a wide screen. Every chart colour is a theme token with checked contrast (« à venir » in a deeper honey, last year's bar outlined).
- **« Réservations à venir, à date » on a phone** stays a chart: the columns scroll under a fixed axis, set on the current month, and one tap (or one click) opens a month's curve with its figures in the header (no clipped tooltip on a phone). Month labels are reachable from the keyboard. The « — » row of écarts waits for a first comparable month.
- **Start of an exercise**: the cumulative curve of the banner stays, shorter, until the first month is over.
