- **Guest email texts on one page, in send order** (specs/guest-email-texts-made-simple.md): Paramètres › « Textes des emails » groups, email by email (confirmation, deposit/balance, J-7, J-2, day after, November/January, cancellation), the subject, the sentences, the mentions and the property hooks. Variables are inserted with chips and shown by their French label. The full email is rendered by the server beside the text while typing, in French or English, on the next stay or a sample one, with « M'envoyer un test ». An « À compléter » list says what would render badly, and the dashboard shows « Emails incomplets » before an arrival.
- **Arrival facts per property**: address, map link, how to get in, key-box code and Wi-Fi (code and password encrypted) compose the J-2 of a new instance.
- **One file for every text**: CSV export, and an import that shows its changes before saving and refuses a bad row with its line number; « Traduire les textes manquants » when the platform has a translation key (`GUESTFLOW_TRANSLATE_API_KEY`).
- **Start assistant**: a step « Vos emails » before « C'est prêt ».
