- **Settings rationalization** (spec `settings-rationalization.md`, 2026-09-21). The « Paramètres » submenu is reordered by family, one short page per concern (Établissement with the devis, Plateformes, TVA & exercice, Emails & notifications, Intégrations, Utilisateurs, Système), each with its own Save; the 13-card « Générale » page is gone. « Clients » becomes a top-level entry and « Mon compte » sits above « Se déconnecter ». A new « Plateformes » page holds every per-platform setting (colour, commission, acompte, tourist tax, payout); the property page and the pricing card show them read-only. The property page is split into six tabs kept in the URL, with a dot on unsaved or refused tabs, server-side validation (beds vs capacity, deposit %, tax %), and a Documents tab whose add form sits behind « Ajouter un document ». The sending address, SMTP login, sender name and notification recipient are derived from the contact email unless overridden. The J-7 hook per property moves to the J-7 template dialog; the commission / cancellation-indemnity VAT moves to Plan comptable; « Tarifs facturables » becomes a tab of Options & ressources.
- **Past reservations are unlocked per fiche**: an admin clicks « Déverrouiller cette fiche » in the lock banner; the unlock lasts until the page is left. The global switch is gone.
- **Guest emails are sent automatically per template only**: the « Envoyer les emails clients sans ma validation » switch is removed; a template in « Automatique » leaves on its own, one in « Manuel » waits for validation.
