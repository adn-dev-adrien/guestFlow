- **One payment, several methods** (spec `mixed-method-payments.md`, 2026-11-09). A payment recorded by hand (acompte, solde, complément, fin de séjour, paiement unique, note en séjour, échéance) can be split over 1-4 lines — CB, espèces, chèque, virement, chèques-vacances, autre — behind a discreet « Plusieurs moyens » in the payment form; cash goes to the caisse interne by default. Lines under the amount due ask « Reste à percevoir » or « Geste commercial »; a split payment is corrected with « Modifier », its reste collected later with « Marquer payé ». An amount raised after the payment becomes a reste à percevoir; an amount lowered below it is shown as a trop-perçu. The books, « Déjà payé », the turnover and the bank reconciliation count only the part on the books. SDK 2.24.0.
- plugin:accounting-export A split payment is booked settlement by settlement, at each date, for its share on the books; its geste commercial is a rabais.
- plugin:online-payment The bank reconciliation expects only the card, cheque and transfer lines of a split payment.
- plugin:sas « Plusieurs moyens » records a split payment at the check-out and on the single arrival payment.
