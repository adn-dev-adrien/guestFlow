- **Bank reconciliation — review fixes (`specs/reconciliation-assisted-matching.md` §3.5).** A credit settled more than 3 days before the expected payout is no longer proposed (it let another stay's payout become a « sûr » match and be auto-confirmed); a grouped payout never beats an exact single stay and always waits for the operator; « Défaire » now takes an auto-confirmed installment back; a penalty is offered on a partial cover and completes it; the fiche's « Vous percevez » is net of penalties; a payout the fiche does not carry yet takes one credit at most; a platform receipt cannot be over-allocated through « Rattacher ». UI: the credit dialog no longer crashes on close, « Actualiser » keeps the filter, the desktop table shows auto badges, splits and groups, the home card only offers one click on « sûr » proposals, the penalty is declarable from the payout row of a locked fiche, and the statement editor no longer writes a stale commission back after a penalty.
- **Bank reconciliation — verification round.** The early side of a payout window is the stay (no credit before check-in; Lodgify and Stripe like a platform paying at booking time), « sûr » and automatic confirmation need a credit from checkout on and within 14 days after the due date, a group that explains the credit as well makes a single stay « à vérifier », card operations are never platform payouts, a confirmation never rewrites a recorded payout or its commission, a declaration completes a fiche only on an exact cover (and every read sweeps the ones the event missed), « Défaire » on an installment warns that its payment link stays closed, and the automatic-confirmation banner counts what « Voir » lists.
