- **Options & ressources, read by category and edited in a side panel** (spec `options-resources-ux.md`, 2026-10-28). The flat 7-column table becomes one foldable section per category (« Sans catégorie » first, the fiche's order) with a search, a property filter and category chips; a row shows the name, the traits that make an option special (offered / included per property, internal, planning card, insurance at 0), and the price it really sells at — « 30 € – 80 € · selon logement », the base price no longer shown when every property overrides it. The editor is a 560 px side panel (a full-height sheet on a phone) in sections: Général, Logements et prix (one table: Proposée · Prix · Inclus · Offert per property), Planning, Visibilité, then the type-specific blocks; every previous setting stays reachable. Archiving moves into the row's « ⋯ » menu with a confirmation; the system options say why they cannot be archived. Resources become cards that show their stock and, through the hourly plugin, the evening and external rates and the schedule. `/options` and `/resources` redirect to their tab. New generic components `SidePanel`, `GroupedList`, `SearchField`; `PricedItemsPage` is removed. New endpoints `GET /api/options/catalogue-view` and `GET /api/resources/catalogue-view`.
