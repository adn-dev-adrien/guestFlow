- **Saving never rewrites what was not edited**: « Enregistrer » is disabled on an untouched fiche; a fiche opened before an import, mail intake or another tab changed the stay is refused on save (« Fiche modifiée entre-temps », reload or keep the draft). A save no longer re-prices an untouched stay (paid stays used to move by a few euros); sold night prices are kept only while the arrival is unchanged; the extra-guest supplement no longer follows the live season price; free-unit options (welcome pack) keep their value on every save (removing the option or lowering its quantity no longer re-bills it, 300 → 380 €).
- **Money changes to know**: a guest added after the booking is now billed the supplement (e.g. 0 → 31 €); the acompte deadline is cleared when the acompte is 0 €; the cot supplement is stored at creation; a platform commission is clamped to its échéance; a platform « Compté comme vente directe » is a direct sale everywhere (existing stays keep their schedule); « Total perçu » counts a rise after payment once (600 paid, raised to 700, reads 700); a collected complement above the stay total is a trop-perçu with « Rembourser » on direct channels. A growth after solde and arrival complement are paid is claimed at end of stay; a cancelled plan gives back everything it collected.
- **New refusals**: a save bringing a paid stay below what was collected is 409 `OVERPAID` until confirmed (the fiche then offers the refund, split by poste); a known e-mail or phone is 409 `CLIENT_EXISTS` (`GET /api/clients/duplicate`; « Créer un nouveau client » writes nothing until the stay is saved); an hourly session overlap is 409 `SESSIONS_OVERLAP`; a missing client is 400 `CLIENT_REQUIRED`; an option or nordic-bath date outside the stay is « Date hors du séjour. »; a served-persons count of 0 is 400 `INVALID_CARD_PERSONS`; a non-integer linen quantity is 400 `INVALID_QUANTITY`; editing a received compensation is 409 `COMPENSATION_LOCKED`; the cancellation insurance cannot be deleted; converting a devis onto nights sold meanwhile is 409 `DATES_UNAVAILABLE`, a devis created or moved onto closed nights is 409, « Transformer en devis » is 409 `DATES_HELD` or `CLOSED_DATES`; a check-out at 17:00 or later the day a closure starts (check-in at 10:00 or earlier the day it ends) is refused; a resource booking must end after it starts.
- **Cancellation**: only money really collected can be refunded (a refund above it, or an indemnity share in caisse interne, is refused); a refund made before the cancellation is not kept again (acompte 274, 100 refunded, cancelled → 174 kept); provider commissions are requalified in the avoir and indemnity; the cancel dialog shows the sum the guest is told is kept and the notice `{{retainedDepositAmount}}` states the sum actually kept; the seeded notice no longer opens with « Faute de règlement du solde ». A refund can be deleted from a cancelled stay only if the cancellation never read it; « Acompte conservé » cannot be deleted. Refunds on a live stay above what was collected are refused (`REFUND_EXCEEDS_COLLECTED`, `REFUND_EXCEEDS_LINE_COLLECTED`); a trop-perçu cannot be refunded twice; un-ticking a payment, an arrival undo, a reconciliation « Défaire », « Sans acompte » or a lowered complement is refused (409 `REFUND_BLOCKS_UNTICK`) while a refund needs that money.
- **Deleting** a stay, devis, client or property is refused when it holds money (including a settled mid-stay note or a caution not yet returned), history or a paid or invoiced resource booking; open links of a deleted row are deactivated at the provider first, and a link that cannot be deactivated refuses the delete. A caution held on a cancelled stay can be returned (« Restituer »).
- **Availability**: a new stay's own night blocks count against closures, cots and per-stay resources respect the stock (409 past stock), a check-in at 10:01 to 10:59 no longer blocks the previous night, changing only the times re-runs the overlap check.
- **Devis**: a devis whose acompte would fall after its solde now owes ONE payment on its validity date (open acompte links are withdrawn and the devis re-stored at boot); dated and expired on the operator's day; a quote for a stay starting today or tomorrow is valid; a converted devis keeps its promised deadline and cannot be deleted while its stay exists; a devis cannot be moved onto closed or held nights; every deleted devis retires its number (never reused).
