- **Saving never rewrites what was not edited**: « Enregistrer » on an untouched fiche changes nothing; a fiche opened before an import or a bank reconciliation no longer overwrites the background change, and no false « Fiche modifiée entre-temps » appears.
- **Money already collected never moves**: a tourist tax that rises after payment, a reduced stay, a removed line or a refunded trop-perçu no longer rewrites entries already booked; a refund is offered, counted and deletable once on a cancelled stay (including one absorbed by the solde).
- **Cancellation**: the cancel dialog shows the sum the guest is told is kept; refunds made before or after a cancellation reduce or keep the indemnity correctly; the cancellation notice states the kept sum; a stay paid through installments reverses each bucket.
- **Dates and numbers are validated everywhere**: impossible dates, reversed or zero-night stays, negative or fractional parties, bad option quantities and wrong JSON types are refused with a message instead of crashing or storing a guess; typed counts read true everywhere.
- **Deleting a stay, devis, client or property is guarded** when it holds history, money or a payment link; a deletion that fails after the operator moved on is reported.
- **Availability**: a new stay's own night blocks count against closures, converting a devis checks the dates, cots and per-stay resources respect the stock, changing only the times re-runs the overlap check.
- **Bed counts, client choice and dirty-form guards**: an explicit 0 is kept; a client typed on an unsaved fiche counts as an edit and does not follow the page to the next fiche; every way out of a dirty page asks first.
- **Devis**: dated and expired on the operator's day, a quote for a stay starting today or tomorrow is valid, a devis never asks for its acompte after its solde, a converted devis keeps the deadline it promised, and a devis cannot be moved onto closed or held nights.
- **Caution** follows the fiche's rules in the arrival and departure sas; a complement already collected is not asked again.
