- **SAS — share the gate access** (spec `gate-access-sowel-connector.md` rule 24b). The « Portail » step gains a « Partager » button under the QR: it opens the phone's native share sheet (Messages, WhatsApp, mail…) with the same link the QR carries, key included, so guests renting together can send the access to one another. Without a share sheet (desktop) it becomes « Copier le lien ». 4 client tests.
