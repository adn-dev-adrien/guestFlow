- **Google Vacation Rentals feed** (new plugin `google-vacation-rentals`): for every enabled property, the website's own quote priced for Google (tax-included totals, availability, minimum and maximum stay, changeover days, closures). Simulation by default — nothing is sent — until Google opens a partner account.
- **Website booking requests resist abuse** (plugin `website-booking`): an internal proof of work solved by the browser (enforced only once the site runs WordPress plugin 1.18.0; until then Paramètres › Intégrations › Site says « Protection anti-robot inactive »); at most 3 new quotes per e-mail address per 24 h (a 4th is refused); the same e-mail, property and dates update an untouched draft in place. Operator mails are grouped (the first of a 15-minute window at once, the rest in one summary) and the home flags more than 20 requests per hour. **Requests never opened, sent, edited, paid or converted are deleted after 30 days, together with their CGV acceptance and the client created for them** (numbers are never reused).
- **Public API, additive**: the availability answer carries `nextAvailableDate`, and a refused contact field names the field and the reason in the request's language.
- **Website set up in two pastes**: Paramètres › Intégrations › « Site internet (WordPress) » shows the API URL and the key to paste into WordPress.
