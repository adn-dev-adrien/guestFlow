- **Google Vacation Rentals feed** (new plugin `google-vacation-rentals`): for every enabled property it computes, with the website's own quote, the tax-included total of each stay of the horizon, availability, minimum and maximum stay, changeover days and closures, and sends the difference to Google. Simulation by default (nothing is sent) until Google opens a partner account.
- **Website booking requests resist abuse** (plugin `website-booking`): an internal proof of work solved by the browser, at most 3 new quotes per e-mail address per 24 h, and the same e-mail, property and dates update an untouched draft in place instead of piling up.
- **Public API, additive**: the availability answer carries `nextAvailableDate` (first bookable arrival), and a refused contact field names the field and the reason in the request's language.
- **Website set up in two pastes**: Paramètres › Intégrations › « Site internet (WordPress) » shows the API URL and the key to paste into WordPress.
