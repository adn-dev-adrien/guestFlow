- **Payment links expire**: every link minted from this version is valid 36 hours (30 minutes for a guest-app card order), and acompte, solde, séjour and échéance links also lapse at 00:00 Paris on the arrival day (a link minted that day keeps its 36 hours; complement links untouched); a link is reused only while it has 24 hours left. The default payment e-mails state the date until which the link works (a template edited by hand keeps its text: add {{paymentLinkValidUntil}} to it), and the acompte reminder carries a fresh link. The WordPress plugin must be 1.19.0 to show « lien expiré ».
- **Paid dates are held**: a reservation holds its nights once it carries money or comes from a platform. A request whose dates another stay took loses its link and shows on the « Dates prises » card. The guest notice « Dates plus disponibles » is **sent automatically** — the only exception to « no guest mail without validation » (its mode is locked, 409 `SEND_MODE_LOCKED`) — once per stay, after the provider's read made one hour after the link's deactivation found it unpaid; nothing is sent when the template is disabled. New refusals: 409 « Dates déjà prises… : aucun lien créé »; a devis cannot be created or moved onto held nights (409).
- **A closure is refused over a stay or an open payment request** (409 `RESERVATION_OVERLAP`, `OPEN_PAYMENT_REQUEST`), and « Retirer le lien » withdraws a link by hand. No link is created on a closed night (409 `CLOSED_DATES`). A payment received on a link withdrawn by hand or for taken dates blocks new requests of that stay (409 `UNSETTLED_WITHDRAWN_PAYMENT`) until the operator uses « Marquer remboursé » or « Enregistrer sur le séjour ».
- **Bank reconciliation (Qonto)**: the credits of the account are read every 8 hours (and on « Actualiser ») and matched to the expected payments, confirmed one by one or with « Tout confirmer ». Unambiguous « sûr » matches are confirmed automatically after each read (paid flag and date only, « auto » badge; « Défaire » also discards the pair); the setting « Confirmer automatiquement les correspondances sûres » turns it off. Split payouts over several stays need typed shares summing to the credit (422 `SHARES_DO_NOT_SUM`); the bank line shows on « Échéances ». A platform payout is proposed only inside the stay's payout window: from the stay's arrival (up to 400 days before the expected date for a platform that pays at booking) to 30 days after the expected date; with no arrival date the early bound is 3 days before the expected date.
- **Platform penalties** are declared on the stay (« Déclarer une pénalité », or from a reconciliation gap) and booked on account 658000 « Pénalités plateformes » by default, without VAT — **ask the accountant to confirm the account** (editable in the Plan comptable). The payout row, « encaissé » and cash flow are net of penalties.
