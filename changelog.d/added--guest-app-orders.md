- **Guest app « Mon séjour », slice D (server)** (spec `guest-app.md` rules 24-38, 41-45). The guest orders from the stay's shop: `POST /public/v1/guest/orders/quote` prices the cart with the fiche's own engine (the same save played without writing, so free units, free minutes, the stay's price lock and per-lodging prices apply as on the fiche), each hourly session re-checked against the resource planning and the other sessions of the order, the breakfast's mornings and hour, the delivery time of the staff-prepared items and the stay's 0 € allowance. `POST /public/v1/guest/orders` takes it « sur la note » or at 0 € (confirmed and written on the fiche at once as mid-stay extras, in one transaction with the order, history « Commande client #N »), or by card: a 30-minute `complement` payment link for exactly the order's basket (VAT lines at the stay's rate, back to `/commande/<id>` on the guest host), the bath slots held meanwhile; paid, the order is committed and its money recorded as a settled mid-stay note carrying the provider's reference, so the end-of-stay complement owes nothing for it. Unpaid after 30 minutes it expires; the guest can abandon it; a payment that lands late still commits it when its slots are free, else the order is « Payée · à rembourser » and the fiche's ledger shows « Commande client à rembourser ». `GET /public/v1/guest/orders` and `/orders/:id` follow it (chips of rule 38, the provider read on demand). Staff side: the « Commandes clients » push on every confirmed or paid order, `GET /api/guest-app/orders` (« À préparer » / « Toutes »), `POST /api/guest-app/orders/:id/delivered`, and `GET /api/guest-app/planning/order-cards` for the planning's « Commande client » cards (admin and reception). An option of the planning other than the breakfast can no longer be flagged « Vendu dans l'app ». SDK 2.22.0 adds the generic `ctx.paymentBasket` (its owner's refund words, else « Paiement en ligne à rembourser »), `ctx.core.payments` and `coreModule('midStayOrderCommit')` (a plugin's order written as mid-stay extras, named and historised in the caller's words); the plugin limits its own orders (`GUEST_ORDER_RATELIMIT_*`, 10 orders / hour / session). +56 server tests.
