- **Mollie rolling reserve — a paid card link is two bank tranches** (spec `mollie-rolling-reserve-and-incoming-payments.md`, 2026-11-16). The payment link a guest pays by card reaches the Qonto account in two credits: the immediate share and the reserve Mollie keeps (20 % for 90 days by default; settings « Retenue Mollie », « Durée de la retenue », « Retenue appliquée depuis »). The split is computed once, stored in `payment_link_splits`, and never changes what the guest owes or the fiche's paid flags. The payments ledger of `GET /api/reservations/:id` gains `bankTranches[]` on a paid row and a `schedule` recap.
- **Reconciliation — Mollie credits replace the acquirer rule** (rules 8-13). A paid link yields two receipts keyed `<ledgerKey>#immediat` and `<ledgerKey>#retenue`; a credit from « Stichting Mollie Payments », whatever its operation type, is matched against the open tranches (one → « sûr », a grouped credit → « probable »), short of Mollie's estimated fees at most. Confirming one never writes the fiche. Every reconciled tranche carries the Qonto transaction id (`bank_transactions.qontoReference`), the Mollie settlement reference and the Mollie payment id. A reserve past its window raises « Retenue Mollie non reçue » on the home page.
- **Optional « Compte Mollie »** (rules 14-17): an advanced access token (read-only, stored encrypted as a plugin secret) lets the reconciliation read the settlement behind a credit, its payments and its costs, so a grouped credit is « sûr » payment by payment. `POST /api/plugins/online-payment/mollie/test` reports the cause in words (profile-restricted token, refused token). A Mollie failure never blocks the reconciliation.
- **Suivi financier — « Versements à venir »** (rules 21-27): `GET /api/finance/incoming[?recent=1]` and `tiles.incoming` on the dashboard list every sum still to arrive on the bank (Mollie reserve, Mollie in transit, instalments, platform payouts), grouped by client, with server-built state labels; `?recent=1` lists the last 30 days' receipts. Readable by the accountant role.
- **Accounting export — Mollie journal** (rules 28-31): journal `MO`, clearing account `46720000` (a paid link debits it and credits the client; a reconciled credit debits the bank `51200000` and credits it; settlement costs debit `62780000`), the switch « Exporter les versements Mollie reçus », the clearing balance at month end and the unattached Mollie credits on `GET /api/accounting/sales`.
- **Plugin SDK 2.29.0**: `ctx.ledgerBankTranches`, `ctx.incomingPayments`, `ctx.journalEntries`; the `journalEntries` core module. `online-payment` 1.8.0 and `accounting-export` 1.3.0 require it.
