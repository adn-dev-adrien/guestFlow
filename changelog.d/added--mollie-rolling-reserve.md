- **Mollie rolling reserve — a paid card link is two bank tranches** (spec `mollie-rolling-reserve-and-incoming-payments.md`, 2026-11-16). The payment link a guest pays by card reaches the Qonto account in two credits: the immediate share and the reserve Mollie keeps (20 % for 90 days by default; settings « Retenue Mollie », « Durée de la retenue », « Retenue appliquée depuis »). The split is computed once, stored in `mollie_payment_splits`, and never changes what the guest owes or the fiche's paid flags. The payments ledger of `GET /api/reservations/:id` gains `bankTranches[]` on a paid row and a `schedule` recap.
- **Reconciliation — Mollie credits replace the acquirer rule** (rules 8-13). A paid link yields two receipts keyed `<ledgerKey>#immediat` and `<ledgerKey>#retenue`; a credit from « Stichting Mollie Payments », whatever its operation type, is matched against the open tranches (one → « sûr », a grouped credit → « probable »), short of Mollie's estimated fees at most. Confirming one never writes the fiche. Every reconciled tranche carries the Qonto transaction id (`bank_transactions.qontoReference`), the Mollie settlement reference and the Mollie payment id. A reserve past its window raises « Retenue Mollie non reçue » on the home page.
- **Optional « Compte Mollie »** (rules 14-17): an advanced access token (read-only, stored encrypted as a plugin secret) lets the reconciliation read the settlement behind a credit — its exact composition from the balance transactions (each payment's immediate share, each reserve release by its payment id), its fees (cancelled today by Mollie's « Fee Discount ») and the real reserve held — so a grouped credit is « sûr » tranche by tranche; the card shows what Mollie still holds (`pendingAmount`). The bank's `REF T…` reference is matched to Mollie's form without the `T`. `POST /api/plugins/online-payment/mollie/test` reports the cause in words (profile-restricted token, refused token). A Mollie failure never blocks the reconciliation.
- **Suivi financier — « Versements à venir »** (rules 21-27): `GET /api/finance/incoming[?recent=1]` and `tiles.incoming` on the dashboard list every sum still to arrive on the bank (Mollie reserve, Mollie in transit, instalments, platform payouts), grouped by client, with server-built state labels; `?recent=1` lists the last 30 days' receipts. Readable by the accountant role.
- **Accounting export — Mollie journal** (rules 28-31): journal `MO`, clearing account `46720000` (a paid link debits it and credits the client; a reconciled credit debits the bank `51200000` and credits it; settlement costs debit `62780000`), the switch « Exporter les versements Mollie reçus », the clearing balance at month end and the unattached Mollie credits on `GET /api/accounting/sales`.
- **Plugin SDK 2.29.0**: `ctx.ledgerBankTranches`, `ctx.incomingPayments`, `ctx.journalEntries`; the `journalEntries` core module. `online-payment` 1.8.0 and `accounting-export` 1.3.0 require it.
- **Card payments taken with the Qonto app go through Mollie too** (rule 1, second half): a Qonto invoice paid by card (paired with its Mollie payment by the invoice number, gross and date exact), and — from the new setting « CB sur place via Mollie depuis » — a card payment recorded on a fiche instalment, on the CB line of a split payment or on a sale outside a stay. They get the same two tranches, reconciliation, ledger lines, « Versements à venir » (a sale outside a stay groups under its client or its label) and Mollie journal entries; such a sale leaves the day's card batch.
- **Reads never write, and a failing settlement never stops the others** (review 2026-11-16): the splits are made when a payment is seen paid, by the read job and by the reconciliation page — not by the fiche, the quote preview or the dashboard; the fiche reads its own tranches only. One Mollie reference Mollie cannot give is kept with a 24-hour delay and the rest of the settlements are read; a settlement holding a payment GuestFlow could not name is read again, and the refunds of payments whose reserve is held are refreshed. A settlement read from Mollie whose composition does not match the open tranches is never « sûr » by amount. A tranche past its window is « en retard » whether or not a proposal waits (ledger, « Versements à venir », reconciliation item); the fiche's recap line tells the immediate share from the reserve; `GET /api/payments/reconciliation/reserve-attention` serves the home alert (one line per fiche, oldest expected date); `tiles.incoming.nextDateLabel`; the Mollie journal books the fee gap of a credit confirmed short, nets refunds and shows the costs of unknown payments apart.
