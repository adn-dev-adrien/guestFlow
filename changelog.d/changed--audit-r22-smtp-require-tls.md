- **SMTP: STARTTLS is required with a login** (spec `admin-account-management.md` rule 16, audit R22 E22-2). On a transport without implicit TLS, a login now requires the upgrade: instance SMTP, platform sender and console mailer. A server with no TLS on 587 now fails with `SMTP_TEST_FAILED` instead of sending the password and the mail in clear. A relay without a login keeps working.
