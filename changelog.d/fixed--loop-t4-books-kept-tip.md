- **A sale outside a stay keeps its books, its invoice and its « À rembourser » rows in step with its money** (stabilisation loop t4, B4-1 to B4-6): a paid line offered, removed or cancelled whose row is closed « rien à rembourser » is now money kept as a tip, like a kept link surplus — its revenue and VAT leave the journal on the close day and the tip is booked, so the journal states exactly what the Qonto invoice states; a closed row is no longer subtracted forever from what the sale owes (a line made payable again is asked again, removing a line owes what its money no longer pays); a cancelled sale gives back the lines no other row was opened for first; the invoice is refused « Caisse interne » only while the sale still holds caisse-interne money; the preview of a sale shows the lines the unallocated money will pay and the rows at the amounts the save leaves them.
- **The Qonto invoice of a sale states the VAT the books declare** (B4-4): Qonto computes the VAT item by item; the items of each VAT class and rate are now sent so their VAT is the one cut once on the group, as in the journal (two beers at 6,50 € : 2,17 € of VAT, no longer 2,16 €).
