- **Hourly resources — degressive pricing by hour rank** (`specs/gift-voucher-campaigns.md` rules 43–45). A resource shown on the planning card may carry a table « à partir de la Nᵉ heure, −x % ». Each slice of a session takes the rate of its band (day or evening, guest or outside), then the discount of its hour's rank. Free minutes take the first ranks. It applies everywhere the resource is priced: the reservation fiche, the SAS, the guest app and outside visits. With an empty table, prices are unchanged.
