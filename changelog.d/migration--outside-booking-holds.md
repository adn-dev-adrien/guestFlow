- Outside bookings: `resource_booking_holds` (a slot held for 15 minutes while a remainder is being paid: `resourceId`, `date`, `startTime`, `endTime`, `ref`, `expiresAt`, `releasedAt`) and `resource_visit_line_marks` (the elements a prepaid tender paid that the client did not take) are created, empty. No row is changed or deleted; the migration is idempotent.
