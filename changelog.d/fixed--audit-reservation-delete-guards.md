- **Deleting a stay, devis, client or property is guarded everywhere** (spec `payment-schedule-and-cancellation.md` rules 49-52). `DELETE /api/reservations/:id` refuses cancelled stays, duplicates set aside and devis (409), and any stay carrying money — including money left on a devis converted into it (collected bucket, paid link, refund, penalty, compensation): « Un paiement est enregistré sur ce séjour : l'annuler plutôt que le supprimer. ». The forced client delete and the property delete refuse the same way; a devis carrying money is kept; an iCal cancellation approval cancels and keeps a stay carrying money instead of deleting it. Open payment links of a deleted row (and of its converted devis) are deactivated at the provider first; a link that cannot be deactivated refuses the delete. Open links pointing at a missing reservation are logged at boot.
