- **Deleting a stay is guarded** (spec `payment-schedule-and-cancellation.md` rules 49-51). `DELETE /api/reservations/:id` refuses cancelled stays, duplicates set aside and devis (409), and any stay carrying money (collected bucket, paid link, refund, penalty, compensation). Open payment links of a deleted stay or devis are deactivated at the provider first; a link that cannot be deactivated refuses the delete. Open links pointing at a missing reservation are logged at boot.
