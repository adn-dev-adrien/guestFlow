- **Calendars — the logement calendar shows every stay « Tous » shows** (spec `calendar-property-parity.md`, 2026-10-28). Both read one `GET /api/calendar`; a platform « Désactivé » for a logement no longer hides its stays anywhere (the fiche's date-conflict hints included). The logement calendar scrolls like « Tous » (sticky month titles, each month loaded once, « Aujourd'hui » the only navigation; no more month arrows) and, on a phone, is a day agenda with coloured ribbons instead of a week pager. Guest names are readable again: one ribbon per week across the stay, never clipped.
- **Calendars — solid, distinct colours; closures on their logement's row.** One hue per platform with a text colour the server picks for contrast (past stays fade to a tint); a closure is a hatched bar in its logement's lane with a stay's half-day edges, on both calendars.
