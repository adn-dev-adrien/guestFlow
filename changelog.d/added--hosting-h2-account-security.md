- **Accounts: forgotten password, two-step login, support access with consent** (specs/hosting-h2-account-security.md). « Mot de passe oublié ? » sends a one-hour, single-use link by email (same answer whatever the address, 3 requests per address and 20 per IP per hour). « Mon compte » turns on a second code — authenticator app (QR code) or email code — with 10 backup codes, « Faire confiance à cet appareil 30 jours » and a 15-minute lock after 5 wrong codes; an admin can turn another user's off, recorded in the account's new security journal; a dashboard card invites admins without one. On a managed instance, the console asks for support access, an admin authorises it (1 h, 24 h or 7 days) or refuses, the console opens the space through a signed 2-minute link as « Support GuestFlow » under a red bar, and Paramètres › Accès du support lists every access and what it did. The TOTP helpers are now shared by the instance and the console. `reset-admin.js --disable-2fa`.
