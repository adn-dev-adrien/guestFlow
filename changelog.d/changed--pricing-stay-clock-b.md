- **Stay clock decides « started » (pricing refonte, PR B)**: a stay counts as started once its check-in time has passed (Europe/Paris), not when the check-in box or the arrival SAS is ticked. The extras sold from then on are routed to the end-of-stay complement; the arrival mark only changes what the fiche displays. The reception SAS window, the end-of-stay collection and the mid-stay window read the same clock (no change of behaviour there). Timed options now read a `HH:MM:SS` time like the clock does.
