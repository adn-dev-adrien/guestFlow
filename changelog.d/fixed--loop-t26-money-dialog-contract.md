- Money dialogs (counter sale, outside visit arrival, hourly booking, SAS arrival and departure recaps, « Nouvelle note », platform penalty, payout split, credit link, the fiche's « Encaisser en une fois » and ledger payment forms) now share one contract module (`client/src/hooks/useMoneyCommit.jsx`, SDK 2.32.0): the payment lines the operator typed stay as typed when the due changes, after a failed recompute and when their block comes back — the default card line of the whole due follows the new due until it is typed; a click on a commit button keeps the focus in the dialog while it is busy (no longer on the page or the dialog itself); a refusal of the server (4xx) shows its reason without « Réessayer », which stays for a server that did not answer (5xx, 408, 429). Nothing about the amounts changes: they are the server's (`specs/plugins-phase-1-sdk.md` rule 58).
- A generative test harness drives every money dialog through thousands of generated runs (operator gestures, server answers held, failed, refused or answered out of order) and checks the six invariants of rule 58 after every step; a failing run prints its seed and a shrunk list of steps.
- Money previews no longer ask again a draft that came back to the one already on its way or already answered (« Plus 15 minutes » then « Moins 15 minutes »): for one render the screen read the earlier answer as current and then as pending, so the visit's and the booking's invoice question could leave for a draft about to be asked over, and the booking's save could send a price just put back in question. A draft back to a failed one is still asked afresh.
