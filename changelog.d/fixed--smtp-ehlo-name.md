- **Outgoing mail greets the SMTP server with the sender's domain** (specs/admin-account-management.md rule 21): nodemailer used to announce itself as `[127.0.0.1]`, which the receiving side records in the `Received` header and spam filters read as a sender that does not know its own name. Every transport now passes the domain of its From address (`domainesolio.com`, or the platform's noreply domain).
