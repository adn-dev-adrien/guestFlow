- **Week and weekend bath vouchers** (spec `gift-voucher-week-weekend-and-print.md`, 2026-11-27, connector 1.27.0, SDK 2.38.0, hourly-resources 1.13.0, gift-vouchers 1.10.0). The outside grid of an hourly resource gets a weekend set (Friday from the evening switch, Saturday, Sunday, public holidays and the evening before one); a bath campaign sells the week, the weekend or both and the giver chooses inside it, the period frozen on the voucher (`contents.resource.period`, « · semaine » / « · week-end » in its title). The validity is picked from 3, 6, 12, 18 or 24 months, on a campaign, on « Nouveau bon » and for a hand-made voucher. Held by new suites on the server (the period redemption, the booking-pricing invariant harness with a negative control per invariant, the back, print and migration suites) and on the client.
- **Redeeming by value on any slot.** Every slot is offered to a bath voucher and tagged against its value: « Inclus », « + x € » (a named supplement: soirée, week-end, soirée week-end, or créneau when neither explains it), « Inclus · boisson offerte » for a cheaper slot (a 0 € line from the option set in the plugin settings, the gap traced as « Écart de créneau »), « Inclus · 2 h + boisson offerte » for an evening-weekend voucher on a free day-week slot (a 0 € « Heure offerte » line; « Inclus · 2 h » — tag `twoHours` — when no drink option is set). The slot is listed with its doubled end, the offered hour survives a remainder paid online, the added time is offered only within what fits after it, and the confirmation refuses (`RECAP_CHANGED`) rather than silently dropping the hour if the slot changed since the recap; when the recipient adds time its price shows next to the gift (« + 45 € · boisson offerte »).
- **Redrawn card backs, price on the merchant card and print sheets.** Both backs are redrawn on white (the website draws the same back; a voucher's back is drawn at its own smaller sizes so « Valable jusqu'au », the contents with their period and the message always print, the contents' first item losing its name before its period); GuestFlow and the WordPress connector update in either order (`/offer` keeps sending the 4.7.0 back under `back.layout` / `back.texts`, the new one under `back.design`; a public request without `period` gets the cheapest). The merchant card's front carries « à partir de » and the campaign's lowest price in the corner chosen per campaign; « Imprimer… » lays 1, 2, 4 or 8 cards per A4 sheet two-sided, or 1, 2 or 4 pairs to fold (fronts cropped like the single card, each picture embedded once, crop marks 3 mm from the edge at every cut line, backs turned half a turn with « De haut en bas »), with a live preview and the server's sentence (`print-layout` and `print.pdf` endpoints for a campaign and a voucher).
