- **Plugin `online-payment` migrations `reconciliation_v3` and `reconciliation_v3b`** — additive: `mollie_payment_splits` (a split per source: payment link, Qonto invoice or card payment recorded on site), `mollie_settlements`, `mollie_settlement_payments`, `mollie_balance_transactions` (the primary balance read from Mollie), `mollie_settlement_failures` (a settlement Mollie could not give, kept with the day it may be asked again), `mollie_payment_splits.refundCheckedAt` (rotation of the refund refresh), `bank_transactions.qontoReference` (filled on the next read). The reconciliation lines and discards recorded on the bare ledger key of a paid payment link — and of a complement deferred to the check-out, `checkoutComplement` — are rewritten once to `<ledgerKey>#immediat` (idempotent, nothing lost; a discard that already has its twin is dropped; production holds at most one). Every paid link is split the first time it is seen, from « Retenue appliquée depuis » (default: the reconciliation start date). `reconciliation_v3b` reaches this schema from ANY state: a development or shadow database that ran a draft of `reconciliation_v3` has its two settlement tables rebuilt (they hold only what Mollie can say again; the next read fills them) and its `payment_link_splits` copied into `mollie_payment_splits`; a database already on the final shape is untouched.
