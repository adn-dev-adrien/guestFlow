- **A guest whose dates were taken is told, once** (spec `payment-link-lifecycle.md` rules 35, 43-45, 47, 64-66, 2026-10-31). When the payment link of a direct devis or unpaid reservation is withdrawn because another stay took its nights, and once the provider has read that link unpaid one hour after its deactivation (no payment paid or still in flight), GuestFlow mails the guest « Dates plus disponibles » (FR / EN, warm and short, no link, no amount), once per stay (the devis and the reservation it became count as one): the single exception, granted by Adrien on 2026-10-30, to « no guest e-mail without validation » (one named constant `AUTO_SEND_EXCEPTIONS`, read by its sender alone; the template stays Manual in the database and `autoSendPolicy` still refuses it). The notice answers that withdrawal only, judged once: nothing is sent when the stay that took the nights no longer holds them (even if another stay books them later), for a platform stay, when the nights went to a stay of the same client, for a lapsed link, a started stay, « Retirer le lien », a link paid meanwhile, or the stays withdrawn at the upgrade. A send interrupted after SMTP accepted it reads « envoi incertain » and is never repeated. Template off, no address, no English version or no SMTP: nothing leaves. The operators get a push « Dates prises — {client} » (client prévenu / non prévenu, with the reason) and the home a « Dates prises » card (the stay that took the dates as a link, the reason when not told, « Ouvrir », « Vu »). In Emails › Modèles its mode reads « Automatique (exception) » and cannot be changed (409 `SEND_MODE_LOCKED`); the history says « Envoyé automatiquement (dates prises) ». The acompte reminder on taken dates is no longer proposed in « Emails à envoyer », and its send and preview are refused « Dates déjà prises par une autre réservation : relance non envoyée. » (traced once in the history, and proposed again once the nights are free). Sent by a core per-minute pass, never from a GET. New component `DatesTakenAlert`. +44 server tests, +8 client tests.
