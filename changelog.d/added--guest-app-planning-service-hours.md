- **Guest app « Mon séjour », slice C (server)** (spec `guest-app.md` rules 28-31, 39-42). `GET /public/v1/guest/shop/resources/:id/slots` lists the bookable slots of an hourly resource (free or evening rate only, by day, priced per lodging), read from the resource planning by the scheduler `hourly-resources` declares: other stays' sessions, outside bookings, held orders, turnover, capacity, heat-up and retention, closed days; a slot that needs the resource lit from cold is offered only when its ignition falls in the service hours. The service hours are the plugin's (`GET/PUT /api/guest-app/service-hours`, shared or per lodging: up to three ranges a weekday, exceptions by date, the all-properties establishment closures applied); `POST /public/v1/guest/shop/delivery-slots` gives a cart's delivery times (30-min steps, the longest preparation lead inside one service range, check-in to check-out, « Dès que possible » while open), and the shop payload gains `service` (open now, next opening, « Le service reprend demain à 8 h — … ») and « Plus disponible pour votre séjour » on a staff-prepared item with no delivery time left. SDK 2.26.0 adds `ctx.resourceScheduler(provider)` with `ctx.core.resourceScheduler()`, and `ctx.resourceHolds(fn)` with `ctx.core.resourceHolds(range)`.
