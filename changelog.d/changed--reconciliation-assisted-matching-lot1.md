- **Bank reconciliation — linking dialog, filters and « Tout confirmer » (online-payment plugin 1.3.1, SDK 2.8.0; lot 1 of `specs/reconciliation-assisted-matching.md`).** The fiche's « Rattacher des virements » lists the credits that can pay it: the plausible ones first (settled within 15 days of an échéance and within 5 € of it, or carrying the client's name), every other available credit folded and paged — the old cut at the 40 newest credits is gone — with a server-side search by name, date (`12/03`) or amount (`596,58`). Several credits can be ticked at once; the server previews the allocation (oldest credit onto the earliest due échéance, an installment plan in its own order), a manual split is validated field by field, and « Rattacher » writes everything or nothing. The page's counters now filter the list (kept in the URL, chip + Échap to leave), and « Tout confirmer » confirms the view's proposals in one batch, each in its own transaction, « à vérifier » left out unless ticked. From a credit, « Rattacher à des fiches » ties it to several échéances. New routes under `/api/payments/reconciliation`: `reservations/:id/credits`, `allocation-preview`, `links/batch`, `confirm-all`; `candidates` and `GET /` take `transactionId`/`filter`. The fiche's plugin block no longer carries the credits. New generic client component `FilterChip` (exported by the client SDK).
