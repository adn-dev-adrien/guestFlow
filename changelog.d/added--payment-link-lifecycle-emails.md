- **Payment e-mails state until when their link is valid, and the acompte reminder carries a fresh link** (spec `payment-link-lifecycle.md` rules 26-30, 35, 36, 2026-10-30). New template variable `{{paymentLinkValidUntil}}` (« Fin de validité du lien »), written in the guest's language and Paris time: « Ce lien est valable jusqu'au lundi 2 novembre à 18 h 00. » / « This link is valid until Monday 2 November at 6:00 pm. », on the line after the link of the acompte, solde, full-payment and installment requests and of the acompte reminder. The acompte reminder no longer re-offers whatever link is open: when it is sent, it reuses the open link only while it has 24 hours left and otherwise mints a fresh one; the request templates sent from the e-mail dialog do the same. Their preview prints « (lien de paiement créé à l'envoi) » when only the send will create the link, and the send fills the previewed text with the link it resolved (an outdated link in the preview is replaced, a placeholder never leaves); a request without any link to carry is refused, and a send with no address or no SMTP is refused before a link is created. Every template stays Manual: nothing is sent without the operator. The public payment status answers `expired` when the request's link lapsed unpaid (while a payment made just before the deadline may still land, it keeps answering « en attente »), and the WordPress plugin 1.19.0 shows « Ce lien de paiement a expiré. Refaire la réservation depuis la page du logement. » (English included); an older plugin ends on « Paiement en attente » as before. +74 server tests, +3 client tests.
