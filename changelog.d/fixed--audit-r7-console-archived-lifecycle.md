- Console: an archived customer is never served again by mistake. « Réessayer » on a failed restart, process restart or rename is no longer offered (and is refused) once the customer is archived; a « Restaurer à une date » that reaches the customer after a deprovision closed it is refused instead of restarting the instance; a rename or a restore sent while a deprovision runs is refused (« Fermeture en cours. ») instead of moving the closed space to a new address or replacing the database just exported; a second deprovision sent while one runs no longer exports and mails a second time; closing a space while its address change is still waiting is refused (« Changement d’adresse à terminer d’abord. »), and erasing a customer removes the route of every old address it still holds, so an old address never serves the next customer's space (specs/hosting-h1-fleet-runtime.md rule 38).
