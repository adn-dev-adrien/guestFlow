- **Accounting — a stay paid, then reduced, no longer books a negative tourist-tax credit** (spec `accounting-books-the-money-collected.md` rule 11, 2026-10-18). The contribs captured when the solde was paid (by hand or online) described the bigger stay; once the stay was saved with fewer guests or less breakfast they overflowed the entry and the export put the gap on the 46710000 line (`C-88,80`). Such an entry is now booked pro-rata of the current lines, as master did for online payments. Accounting-export plugin 1.0.1.
- **Reservations — a trop-perçu is reported and offered as a refund** (spec `reservation-refunds.md` rule 34). A save that brings a paid stay below what was collected is refused with 409 `OVERPAID` until confirmed; the fiche then opens the refund dialog on the amount, and the Remboursements block shows « Trop-perçu : X € » with « Rembourser », live while the fiche is edited — including the excess a paid acompte and solde keep once the stay is reduced.
- **Hourly resources — no more double-booked sessions on the fiche** (spec `plugins-phase-3c-hourly-resources.md` rules 30-31). A save whose sessions use a resource more times at once than it exists is refused with 409 `SESSIONS_OVERLAP`; « Ajouter une séance » proposes the first free slot of the stay, from the check-in on arrival day, and greys out the slots outside the stay. Hourly-resources plugin 1.0.2.
- **Clients — no more duplicates, no client left by an abandoned fiche** (spec `clients.md` rules 12-13). A client whose email or phone is already known is refused with 409 `CLIENT_EXISTS` (the Clients page offers « Créer quand même »; the fiche offers « Utiliser ce client »). On a new reservation or devis, « Créer un nouveau client » writes nothing until the stay is saved: `POST /reservations` and `POST /devis` take `newClient` and write both in one transaction. New `GET /api/clients/duplicate`.
- **Clients — the city autocomplete works in production** (spec `security-hardening.md` rule 12). The production CSP allowed `connect-src 'self'` only, which blocked the commune lookup (`https://geo.api.gouv.fr`) of the fiche and the Clients page; that exact origin is now allowed, and nothing else.
- **Reservation screens — small fixes of the review** (spec `review-2026-10-18-reservations.md`; `reservation-price-arithmetic.md` rule 10). No « (NaN nuit) » on an empty fiche; amount fields show `7,20`, not `7.2`; the devis PDF strikes a price only when it is higher; the SAS reads « Étape N » without a total that grew with each upsell; « Petit déjeuner · 3 matins » instead of « ×3 »; impersonal texts on the client form, the meal covers and the Qonto error; the fiche is one column below 1200 px with the centred client name from 1200 px, and a long property name no longer widens the fiche or the calendar. SAS plugin 1.0.1, online-payment plugin 1.0.1.
