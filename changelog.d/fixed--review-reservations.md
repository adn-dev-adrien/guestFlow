- **Accounting — a stay paid, then reduced, no longer books a negative tourist-tax credit** (spec `accounting-books-the-money-collected.md` rule 11, 2026-10-18). The contribs captured when the solde was paid (by hand or online) described the bigger stay; once the stay was saved with fewer guests or less breakfast they overflowed the entry and the export put the gap on the 46710000 line (`C-88,80`). Such an entry is now booked pro-rata of the current lines, as master did for online payments. Accounting-export plugin 1.0.1.
- **Reservations — a trop-perçu is reported and offered as a refund** (spec `reservation-refunds.md` rule 34). A save that brings a paid stay below what was collected is refused with 409 `OVERPAID` until confirmed; the fiche then opens the refund dialog on the amount, and the Remboursements block shows « Trop-perçu : X € » with « Rembourser ».
