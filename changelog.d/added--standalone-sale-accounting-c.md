- **Sale outside a stay — accounting** (`specs/pricing-components.md` rule 63, `specs/outside-booking-sas.md` rules 30, 32, 43). Sales outside a stay enter the journal on the day they are paid, per product class and per VAT rate (internal till and offered lines excluded), and in the card batch, once only. New « Compte des boissons » setting of the « Export comptable » plugin: without it, an export whose period contains a drink is refused (409 `DRINKS_ACCOUNT_REQUIRED`). Months already exported do not move.
