- **A devis never asks for its acompte after its solde** (D23, specs `deposit-blocks-the-dates.md` rule 19 and `payment-schedule-and-cancellation.md` rules 5 and 56, audits R33-M-M1 = R33-DB-M1, R33-DB-L1). A devis with an acompte for a stay 31 to `30 + validity` days out had its solde due before its acompte, on the PDF and on the reservation it was converted into (dashboard « Solde à demander » then « À annuler » before the acompte fell due). Such a devis now owes one payment, due on its validity date: its live quote, save, read, PDF, e-mails, payment link, the site's quote, « Transformer en devis », the conversion and the converted stay's later saves all agree. Reservations keep rule 47.
- **The live quote of a new devis is the devis its first save stores** (audit R33-M-L1): « Nouveau devis » now shows the deadlines « Enregistrer » writes.
- **A page load overtaken by another no longer fills the fiche** (audit R33-C-L1): a devis re-read landing after « Accepté » had opened the new reservation left the devis's data on it.
