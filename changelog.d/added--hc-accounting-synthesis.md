- plugin:accounting-export **Accounting synthesis — server and API** (spec `accounting-synthesis.md`, 2026-11-05). `GET /api/accounting/synthesis` answers, for a month, a calendar quarter, the fiscal year or a custom window (≤ 750 days, the Suivi financier's refusals), the tiles of the future « Synthèse » tab of « Comptabilité » with their last-year comparison (the dashboard's rule): revenue HT/TTC of the stays, collected and number of payments, tourist tax we remit (the « Taxe de séjour » extraction, month by month), VAT to declare by rate (or the franchise/exemption mention). Plus the VAT return by rate and by revenue account read from the very rows the CSV writes (CSV byte-identical, pinned by a golden file), the checks strip (balanced entries, VAT coherence entry by entry to the cent, finished stays not settled, last CSV export), collections by means (platform payout, payment link, marked perçu) and by settlement type with shares adding up to 100, and the collected series with last year's running total. Each panel names its source (stays vs collections). The CSV download now stamps the last export. SDK 2.22.0 exposes `financeModel`, `financeDashboardModel`, `financeWindow`, `fiscalYear`, `yearOverYear`; the dashboard's N-1 range is now one function (`lastYearRange`), its payload unchanged.
