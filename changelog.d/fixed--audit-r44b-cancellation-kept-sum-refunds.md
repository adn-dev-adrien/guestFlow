- **The sum a cancellation notice states reads every refund right** (`specs/payment-schedule-and-cancellation.md` rules 61 and 62, verification R44 L1-L2). A caisse-interne acompte kept by a cancellation now gives back only what the caisse refunds leave over once every other caisse money (a mixed solde's caisse share, a complement paid in the caisse) has absorbed them; a notice sent after a refund made since the cancel states the sum kept less what that refund took from it, never below 0. The email editor labels `{{retainedDepositAmount}}` / `hasRetainedDeposit` « Somme conservée » / « Si somme conservée ».
- A caisse refund made before a cancellation, and counted in the sum kept, can no longer be deleted from the cancelled fiche.
