- **plugin:sas — the arrival « Séjour à régler » is the amount the commit marks paid** (`specs/pricing-components.md` rule 113): when unticking « Offert » on a paid line raises the unpaid balance, the stay step and the recap show the raised balance, and a commit sent with a recap read before that change is refused (409 `STAY_DUE_CHANGED`) and read again. Before, the SAS could mark paid 45 € more than it had shown.
- **plugin:sas — offering a paid line at the arrival SAS credits the unpaid balance like the fiche's line action** (rule 114): no refund to confirm when the balance still due absorbs it. Before, the same gesture at the same hour left 45 € owed back while the full balance was still asked.
- **Deleting a refund on the trop-perçu gives its money back as credit first** (rule 115): what the stay still asks (open complement, end-of-stay remainder, balance, deposit) absorbs it before anything is owed back again, so « refund deleted, then a line sold » and « line sold, then refund deleted » end the same.
