- **Bank reconciliation**: a linking dialog, filters on the counters and « Tout confirmer » for the proposals of the view; the start date must be a real day within today ± 5 years; a newly picked account is read from 13 months back.
- **Payment links**: a plugin provides the payment provider (Qonto is the first); the core keeps what a payment means. A direct payment link is refused for a platform stay (« Réservation plateforme : aucun lien de paiement. »; before, « Demande de solde » on a platform fiche mailed the guest a real link). A request on the arrival day mints a fresh link instead of reusing one that lapses minutes later; a bucket collected at arrival retires its open link at once.
- **Settings are bounded**: acompte delay 0 to 30 days, solde and annulation 0 to 60; out-of-range values are refused by the server (a negative delay used to be saved as 7); the per-platform commission endpoint refuses a negative or excessive rate instead of clamping. A stored out-of-bounds delay stays until edited; 0 reads « à régler à la réservation ».
- **New refusals** around payments: 409 `LINKS_LAST_LOOK_PENDING` (removing online-payment while a lapsed link's last check is pending), `DEVIS_HOLDS_LINK` (deleting a converted devis that holds the open link), `LINK_ALREADY_PAID`, `LINK_PAYMENT_IN_FLIGHT`, `ALREADY_PAID` (no link for an échéance already paid); 400 `REFUND_BEFORE_PAYMENT`.
