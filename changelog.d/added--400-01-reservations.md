- **Pay a direct reservation in several times**: the fiche of a direct reservation gets an « Échéancier » of 2 to 12 installments, the first due the day it is created. Each installment has its own Qonto payment link and bank match; there is **no automatic reminder** and no automatic cancellation for non-payment: the request goes by the manual mail « Demande de règlement d'une échéance » (one « à valider » entry per installment from its due date, never settable to automatic). The plan replaces acompte and solde (arrival complement and caution untouched); an installment is always its own accounting encaissement.
- **One payment, several methods**: a payment recorded by hand (acompte, solde, complement, end of stay, single payment, mid-stay note) takes 1 to 4 lines among CB, espèces, chèque, virement, chèques-vacances and autre; cash goes to the caisse interne by default. The books, « Déjà payé », turnover and bank reconciliation count only the part on the books; a lowered amount shows as trop-perçu.
- **Payments as a status ledger on the fiche**: one row per money movement (acompte, solde or « Versement <plateforme> », complements, refunds) with state, link, undo and detail. Acompte and solde are settable from their row; once the acompte is perceived, a different solde re-prices the whole stay (nearest reachable cent, never below the acompte received) and the consequence is shown before « Valider ».
- **Restore an archived option**: a folded « Archivées » section restores an archived option (`POST /api/options/:id/restore`; per-property defaults dropped by the archive are not brought back).
- **Duplicates set aside** (`superseded`) leave every calendar, list, availability check and the books; an admin can « Rétablir » one, and iCal never resurrects it.
- **« Prix {plateforme} à vérifier »**: an alert flags an upcoming platform stay whose paid price is more than 15 % below or 25 % above (and 30 € or more away from) the channel's « Prix plateformes » grid. The fiche shows stored amounts, not today's calculation, until modified.
