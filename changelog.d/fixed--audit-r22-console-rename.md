- **Console: a rename can no longer lose an instance** (`specs/hosting-h1-fleet-runtime.md` rule 45). After a failed address change, a second one was accepted and recorded as done on a directory nobody had moved: a later deprovision skipped the export and left the unit running, the erasure left the database, and the next customer of that address opened it. A rename now waits for the one before it (409 `RENAME_PENDING`, button disabled with its reason), a retry starts from the slug the instance really left even after two renames on one day, a rename finding no directory for a live instance fails, and a creation stops on a database already at its address. A customer stopped there cannot be closed on that database (409 `ADDRESS_HELD`) and leaves for a free address without moving it or redirecting it.
