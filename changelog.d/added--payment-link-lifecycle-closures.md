- **A closure is refused over an open payment request, and « Retirer le lien » withdraws a link by hand** (spec `payment-link-lifecycle.md` rules 40-42, 49, 2026-10-30). In the closure dialog, as soon as the logement and both dates are set, the server says what the closure would meet: a red line « Réservation sur ces dates : fermeture impossible. » listing every stay, an amber line « Demande de paiement en cours sur ces dates. » listing each devis whose payment link is still open (amount, end of validity), and an amber line « Devis en cours sur ces dates. » for a devis without one; each row has « Ouvrir » (new tab on desktop, in place on a phone), and the lines are asked again when the operator comes back to the tab. Save stays enabled: the server refuses a reservation (409 `RESERVATION_OVERLAP`, now with its list) and an open request (new 409 `OPEN_PAYMENT_REQUEST`, « … la retirer avant d'enregistrer la fermeture. »), never a devis without a link. On the fiche and the devis, the ledger row of an open link offers « Retirer le lien » behind the confirmation « Retirer ce lien ? Il ne pourra plus être payé. »: the link is withdrawn (`cancelReason = 'operator'`), deactivated at the provider (the poll retries when that fails), and the row reads « Lien retiré le {jour} » with « Renvoyer la demande »; a link the guest has just paid is refused and its payment booked at once (also later, by the poll, when it was paid before a withdrawal made while the provider was disconnected), a second open link of the same row gets its own button, and cancelled or set-aside stays never offer it. A stay with at least one night under a closure gets no payment link: the mint and the request or reminder e-mail are refused 409 `CLOSED_DATES` « Logement fermé sur ces dates : aucun lien créé. » (spec rule 55). New endpoints `GET /api/establishment-closures/conflicts` and `POST /api/payments/links/:id/withdraw`; new generic component `ConflictList`. Closures written by a tariff recipe are not concerned. +41 server tests, +17 client tests.
