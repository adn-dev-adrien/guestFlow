- **Payment links**: a full link charges acompte + solde with the tourist tax on its own 0 % VAT line and no arrival-complement line (it used to charge the final price and tick the whole solde). **A link paid at another amount than the quote asks — re-priced quote, older full link, a full link paid after an acompte appeared — is booked at the amount really received** (acompte and solde together: 250 € on a 90 + 217,20 stay leaves 57,20 owed), the shortfall stays due, the « autre montant » alert is raised and the confirmation is still sent; an acompte link paid after the solde was collected is booked on top and the excess shows as a trop-perçu to refund. A retired complement link paid late is recorded like « Marquer perçu ». An acompte link paid after the stay was switched to « Sans acompte » no longer loses the money (« Acompte réactivé : payé par un lien retiré », solde lowered by the same amount). A payment on a link whose stay was deleted alerts the admin.
- **Online payments are split across the stay's lines like a hand-ticked payment** (before, the bucket was only ticked). A link GuestFlow abandons (stay cancelled, amount changed) is deactivated at Qonto and retried at each check if Qonto is unreachable; a payment that still lands on the link of a **cancelled** stay is not booked and the admin is told to refund it (when the amount changed, the money received is booked, see above); a link settled another way is retired, and one retired because its bucket was ticked by hand, then found paid, becomes the payment itself. Reminding a stay no longer creates a Qonto link while online payment is off; a platform stay never gets a direct payment link.
- **Qonto**: one token refresh at a time, bounded requests (a hung request no longer blocks the poll), a failed webhook check stays shown. **An instance already switched to production needs ONE new Qonto consent** so the webhook is subscribed on the right account.
- **Bank reconciliation** follows the payments ledger for every non-direct platform, refuses unknown stays and credits, and « sûr » matches need a credit from checkout on and within 14 days after the due date.
- **Installment plan guards**: while a payment waits to be settled or a link deactivation is unconfirmed, a plan cannot be made or resized (409 `UNSETTLED_WITHDRAWN_PAYMENT`, `LINK_DEACTIVATION_PENDING`, `LINK_LAST_LOOK_PENDING`); beside a plan only an échéance is paid (409 `INSTALLMENT_PLAN_IN_PLACE`); a payment received after a withdrawal shows as « Paiement après retrait » in « À traiter ». Every payment flag must be true/false/1/0 (400 `INVALID_PAYMENT_FLAG`).
- **Split payments** (several methods): price cuts, refunds and cancellations after a split payment no longer leave a reste, a phantom indemnity or a settlement behind.
- **Held dates**: the first boot after the install, failures while withdrawing a link and a link minted during a withdrawal no longer leave a stay payable. A website devis re-priced (operator save or guest resend) retires its open links at the provider; two concurrent pay calls mint one link; a payment on closed nights books the stay unconfirmed and alerts the admin.
