- **Online payments announced** (spec `online-payment-notices.md`, 2026-11-26). Every online payment recorded on a stay — acompte, solde, full payment, échéance — tells the operator: a push on the new « Paiements en ligne reçus » channel (own toggle in « Notifications push »), an e-mail to the notification address, and a « Paiements reçus » subject on the home listing the last 24 hours, each line opening its fiche. A payment that already raises an alert (paid after cancellation, double payment, amount mismatch) keeps that alert only; a payment ticked by hand is not announced.
- **« Solde reçu » e-mail.** A balance paid online (or the last échéance of a schedule) sends the guest a receipt: the new default template `balance_received`, FR and EN, ships `auto` — the third named exception to the manual-by-default e-mail policy, granted by the operator; it can be switched to `manual` on the Emails page, then it waits in « Emails à envoyer ».
- **Devis converted by hand confirms the stay.** Converting a devis from its fiche now hands the new stay to the confirmation e-mail, whose own mode decides (sent at once when `auto`, otherwise queued). Converting twice, or a devis already confirmed by a payment, sends nothing more.
