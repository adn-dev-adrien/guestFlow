- **Suivi financier** — a window the server refuses is no longer a dead end: a custom date with a 5-digit year, 30 February or a missing date says « Deux dates à choisir. » before any request, and every refusal (month outside the exercise, unknown period or logement) keeps the period selector (and « Tous les logements ») under the error. A link the device's day refused now loads once the server's day accepts it instead of spinning (audit R20-C1, C3).
- **Fiche de réservation** — a date with a 5- or 6-digit year or an impossible day says « Date de séjour invalide. » under the dates and asks no quote, occupied dates, availability or welcome pack for it; the fiche's reads re-run when the server's day lifts a refusal (audit R20-C2, C3).
- **Emails › Simulation** — « Du » opens on the local day, not the UTC one (audit R20-C4).
