- **« Transformer en devis » refused on held or closed nights** (`specs/payment-link-lifecycle.md` rule 46, reading of 2026-11-01, audit R11-2). `POST /api/devis/from-reservation/:id` now applies the checks of a devis creation: 409 `DATES_HELD` when the stay's nights are held (by the stay itself — a platform stay, a direct stay with money — or by another one) and 409 `CLOSED_DATES` when a closure covers them. The devis was otherwise born on held nights, marked « Dates prises » by its own source and unpayable. The fiche shows the refusal in the « Devis non enregistré » dialog, with the stays or the closure to open.
- **Public payment: visitor wording when the nights are lost during the provider round trip** (rule 54, reading of 2026-11-01, audit R11 public L-1). Nights taken or closed while the provider answered are refused 409 `DATES_UNAVAILABLE` « These dates are no longer available. » in the visitor's language, instead of the operator sentence « … aucun lien créé ».
- **Rollback safety of « Dates plus disponibles »** (`specs/self-update-and-releases.md` rule 43, reading of 2026-11-01, audit R11 console N1). The template is seeded on the `datesTaken` anchor, so a rollback to v3.19.0 never offers it for every stay on its arrival day; a generic test now guards every event-triggered or exception template against the `start` anchor.
