- **Sale outside a stay — the core** (`specs/pricing-components.md` rules 53 and 74). A catalogue article marked « vendable hors séjour » is sold to a client without a stay: priced by the same families, the outside customer's VAT per class (drinks, catering, options, resources), lines frozen at the sale, remaining balance and payment allocation in order of sale. The client is « Passage » unless the e-mail address is identical to an existing client's or « Enregistrer comme client » is used. New routes `POST /api/standalone-sales/quote` and `POST /api/standalone-sales`, SDK 2.31.0.
