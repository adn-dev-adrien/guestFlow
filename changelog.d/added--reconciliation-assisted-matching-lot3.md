- **Platform penalties (online-payment plugin 1.4.1, accounting-export plugin 1.0.15, SDK 2.10.0; lot 3 of `specs/reconciliation-assisted-matching.md`).** An amount a platform keeps on a payout besides its commission and the tourist tax is declared on the stay (« Déclarer une pénalité » in the « Paiement plateforme » block, or from a reconciliation gap, prefilled with the gap). The server asks whether the payout typed on the fiche is the statement figure or the bank amount: in the second case the penalty is added back to the payout so the commission drops by the same amount instead of swallowing the penalty. Guards in words (direct or cancelled stay, amount, motif, date, penalties above the payout, negative commission); a warning when a month already transmitted changes; « Supprimer » restores the payout only if it was not edited since. The payout row of the ledger shows a line per penalty; « total de séjour », « encaissé », « à encaisser », a late payout and the cash-flow line are net of the penalties, commissions stay the commission alone. The reconciliation expects `payout − acompte net − penalties` and writes `credit + acompte net + penalties`, so the gap clears once declared. Accounting: one entry per penalty, dated on the penalty, debit the « Pénalités plateformes » account (**`658000` by default, editable in the Plan comptable — to be confirmed with the accountant**), credit the client's account, no VAT. New routes `…/reservations/:id/platform-penalties[/preview|/:penaltyId]`; new core module `platformPenaltiesModel` exposed to plugins.
