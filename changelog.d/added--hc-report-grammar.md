- Report building blocks for the upcoming finance reports (specs/report-grammar.md): KPI tiles with a definition button, a secondary line and a year-over-year pill giving last year's value (« — vs N-1 » plus one notice when there is nothing to compare), titled report panels, tab counts, a line chart with series named at their end, a legend that switches series on and off and a « ⋯ » menu to read the chart as a table or copy its data, aligned daily and cumulative charts sharing one cursor, and a segmented share bar above its table.
- Report charts load with the first chart shown rather than with every page, keep their axis on round steps (0–100 % for a rate), say when a period is empty, and keep long series names and definition bubbles readable on a phone.
- Report charts keep their top graduation close to the highest value and their month labels evenly spaced; a definition bubble closes when the page scrolls; a failing accounting check is titled by what fails (« Écart de TVA », « Écritures déséquilibrées », « Séjours non soldés »).
- Chart axes hug a negative day as closely as the highest value, long series names stay whole on wide screens, and the Suivi financier curve spaces its months evenly at every width.
