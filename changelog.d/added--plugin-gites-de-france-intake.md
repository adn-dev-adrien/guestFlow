- **Plugin « Saisie Gîtes de France »** (`gites-de-france-intake`): every 3 hours (1–24 h, setting) the plugin reads, read-only, the « nouvelle réservation » mails of the centrale (`resa@gdf07.com`) in the mailbox GuestFlow already sends from, and fills the matching iCal fiche — guest, party, dossier number, ménage paid to the platform, beds and « Paiement plateforme » (brut = rent + options + tax, the centrale's booking fees left out). The mail only gives the rent net of commission: the gross rent is solved from the platform's commission %, and when two rents fit (one case in ten) the fiche shows both with their commission, one click to choose. A fiche already edited by hand gets a proposal, never a write; unreadable mails and bookings still without a fiche after a day show on the dashboard. Nothing is read until a property is tied to its gîte number (Paramètres › Intégrations). Spec: `specs/gites-de-france-mail-intake.md`.
- **Plugin SDK**: `ctx.core.reservations.update` (a plugin saves a reservation through the fiche's own path — validation, pricing, history, event — never on a started stay), `ctx.core.reservations.suggestBeds`, `ctx.core.clients.findOrCreate`, `ctx.core.settings.mailbox()`, and the `reservation.banners` slot at the top of the fiche. The fiche's save handler is now `performUpdate`, callable without Express; HTTP behaviour unchanged.
