- Console: a failed address change no longer lets other actions reach the wrong place. « Restaurer à une date » is refused until the address change is finished, and a blocked « Réessayer » keeps the instance running. A fleet update skips that customer (« Changement d’adresse à terminer d’abord. ») instead of stopping for everyone, and the plugin push and the catalogue read its space at the address that still answers. An address with a folder on it is refused before a rename starts. A customer refused at creation because another customer's database sits at its address no longer writes a licence there, reads or orders an erasure of its modules, opens its support access or files its accounts under its own name. An archived customer gets no support sign-in link (specs/hosting-h1-fleet-runtime.md rules 46-51).
