- **Test suites no longer go red with the calendar, again** (audit R8 M-1, `server-test-isolation.md` rule 6). It had reached 711–798 s of its 15-minute cap, and the release ran server and client one after the other under 20 minutes; the release now runs the server suite and the three client shards side by side, and packages after all of them.
