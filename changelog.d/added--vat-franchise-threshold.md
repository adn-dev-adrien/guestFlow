- **Franchise threshold** (`specs/vat-optional-mode.md` rules 35 to 44). Under "Sans TVA · art. 293 B", Settings › TVA & exercice gains a "Seuil de franchise (€)" field, empty by default: GuestFlow ships no legal amount, the operator types its own. The Suivi financier then shows a card: the turnover collected during the calendar year (each payment on its date, platform commission and penalty added back, tourist tax and caisse interne left out), the projection to 31 December from the reservations in progress, a gauge, the level (under the threshold, threshold close at 80 %, projection beyond, exceeded) and last year as a caption. From "Seuil proche", a "Seuil de franchise" line joins "À traiter" on the home. Read-only; invisible for a VAT-registered installation.
