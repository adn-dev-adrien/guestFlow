- **Arrival of a client without a stay — the day's small sale** (`specs/outside-booking-sas.md` rules 1 to 34, `specs/resource-booking-pricing-and-invoicing.md` rule 52, `specs/reception-role-checkin-only.md` rule 17, `specs/payment-link-lifecycle.md` rule 69; « Ressources à l'heure » plugin 1.8.0). On the server, the arrival of a resource booking outside a stay becomes a sale: the session (with its free time), the articles sold at the counter, « Offrir » on each line, a partial payment that leaves a « reste à percevoir », a payment link for the remainder, « Client absent ». All amounts come from the server. The planning card shows « À encaisser », « Reste … », « Payé » or « Absent ». Reception opens and reopens the day's arrival until 4 a.m. the next day; a paid line, a refund and « Client absent » stay with the administrator. The Qonto invoice includes every paid line once the remainder is settled. A session already paid on the booking keeps its payment and its log; if drinks or articles are sold at the arrival, a single Qonto invoice covers the whole visit, once everything is settled.
