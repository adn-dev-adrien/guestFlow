- **A date typo no longer freezes or crashes the server** (audit R19; specs/finance-dashboard-redesign.md rule 51, specs/establishment-closures.md rules 18-20): a « Personnalisée » finance window is at most 750 days (« Période trop longue : 750 jours au plus. », refused before any request), and its cumulative curve always stops on the window's last day — « Au » typed as 9999-12-31 used to loop until the process ran out of memory. A closure must be two real days at most two years apart (« Date invalide. », « Fermeture trop longue : 2 ans au plus. »), and closures are expanded only over the nights asked, so a long closure no longer costs every public availability read, the fiche's occupied dates or the Google Vacation Rentals feed (plugin 1.0.3) about 3 s. The occupied-dates endpoint takes a window of at most 750 real days.
