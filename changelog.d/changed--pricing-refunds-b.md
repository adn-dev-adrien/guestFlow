- **Refunds, one trop-perçu formula (pricing refonte, PR B)**: the excess a guest paid is now one figure for every stay: the money received (allocations) minus what the stay costs, minus only the refunds opened on the trop-perçu. A commercial gesture on a free line no longer lowers it, the live figure no longer counts the drift of today's engine price, and what the end of stay collected for the departure's own sales is not an excess. The un-tick of a payment and the correction of a split payment share one guard. The cancellation stores the sum it kept and the refunds its avoir netted. Money received in a way that must go back (a link paid twice, a basket not honoured, a payment on a withdrawn link, a paid line lowered) is kept in a register of refunds to make, each with a « Marquer remboursé » action; « Carte » is a means of refund with the provider's reference.
