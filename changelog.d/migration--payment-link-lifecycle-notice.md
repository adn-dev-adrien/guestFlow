At the first boot, every stay already holding a link withdrawn for taken dates without a `dates_taken_notices` row gets one, `not-sent` / `upgrade`: it shows on the « Dates prises » card as « client non prévenu » and never triggers a guest e-mail. The default template `dates_unavailable_notice` is seeded (stored Manual). Nothing is deleted. `payment_links` gains `takenByReservationId`, `unpaidReadAt` and `noticeJudgedAt` (nullable; links withdrawn before keep them empty and are never mailed), and `dates_taken_notices` is rebuilt once to allow the `sending` status, rows and ids kept.
