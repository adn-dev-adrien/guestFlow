- **Gift vouchers — campaigns, composed vouchers and one-shot vouchers** (`specs/gift-voucher-campaigns.md` rules 2–6, 9, 11–12, 19, 41, 46, 52). A voucher is sold from a campaign: the free amount of a stay (`lodging`) or a content composed from the catalogue (`resource`) — one hourly resource in the day or evening band with its included minutes, included options, add-ons and extra time offered to the buyer. Every price is read from the pricing engine for the outside customer (the band's external rate, the resource's degressive tiers by hour rank — extra time continues the ranks —, each option at its catalogue price, the VAT of each line's class) and never stored on a campaign; a campaign the engine cannot price is not offered. `offerService` is the one path of the sale: the site's controllers (key guard, proof of work, per-visitor limits, terms version) and the operator's (session, outcomes) only wrap it. New routes: `GET /public/v1/gift-vouchers/offer` now lists the campaigns (the lodging payload keeps its place), `POST /public/v1/gift-vouchers/offer/price`; admin `GET /api/gift-vouchers/catalogue`, campaigns CRUD and duplicate, `POST /campaigns/preview-price`, `GET /offer`, `POST /offer/price`; `POST /api/gift-vouchers` takes `slug` or a one-shot `composition`, `extraMinutes`, `addOns` and `outcome: paid | offered | link` (« Offert » records no sale and no VAT; the link is a site order mailed on the call). Nothing exists before the bank confirms but the order, which is priced again when its payment link is minted. A voucher pays only for its kind (`lodging` or its own resource): the check lives in the plugin's `apply`, which receives the consumer's kind from the core (`consumer`, default `lodging`). Core, generic: `standaloneSale.quote` accepts a dateless hourly session `{ band, minutes, rankOffsetMinutes }` priced by the hourly family (`sell` still refuses it); the `articles` core module exposes `makeArticle` and `liveFamilies` (SDK 2.33.0).
- **Gift vouchers — visual library, printed cards, mail, cancellation, recipes** (`specs/gift-voucher-campaigns.md` rules 7, 10, 20–22, 33–34, 37, 39, 47–50, 54). `GET/POST/PUT/DELETE /api/gift-vouchers/visuals[/:id]` (+ `GET /:id/image`): JPEG or PNG in the A6 portrait ratio within 2 %, at least 1240 × 1748 px, stored as received under the plugin's data folder; replacing the image adds a version, a voucher freezes the version it was issued with (`visualVersions`), delete is refused while an on-sale campaign uses the visual, and the daily `visuals-purge` job removes a version file once no valid voucher carries it. The voucher PDF is an A6 two-sided card with a 3 mm bleed (front = the frozen visual, never resampled; back drawn with the logo, the QR code to `/<linkBase>/utiliser#bon=<code>`, the contents, the code, the validity, the company name, town and website, and the legal mention along the right edge; no amount on a resource voucher); `GET /api/gift-vouchers/campaigns/:id/merchant-card.pdf` serves the printer's card (crop marks, code box of 24 mm, four guide cells) and locks the campaign's slug. The mail carries the code, the contents, an invoice-style recap read from the lines and VAT lines frozen on the voucher and the PDF attached (`ctx.mail.send` attachments); the mail template settings and `bookingUrl` are gone, `linkBase` (default `bon-cadeau`) is new. `GET /:id/pdf` and `POST /:id/resend` (`{ email? }`) refuse a voucher used, expired or cancelled; a resend to another address is written to the history. `POST /:id/cancel { force?, reason? }` follows the 14-day withdrawal (`CANCEL_FORCE_REQUIRED` after it), releases the holds and opens a `gift_voucher_refunds` line, `POST /:id/refunds/:rid/settle` marks it settled; the books carry it as an avoir dated the cancellation day. A site order's acceptance of the terms is recorded through `ctx.terms.accept` (`voucherOrder`, activity of the campaign, channel `site`; `payment-link` for the operator's link at payment), the public order refuses `TERMS_NOT_ACCEPTED` / `TERMS_OUTDATED`, and a resource campaign is not offered while its terms are unpublished. `GET /campaigns/:id/recipe.zip` and `POST /campaigns/import` export and import a composed campaign as `campaign.json` plus images, articles named never priced, all-or-nothing. Core, generic: the accounting journal mirrors a plugin's `role: 'refund'` sale that carries a single VAT rate (customer credited, revenue and VAT debited); `fflate` 0.8.3 becomes a direct server dependency (zip archives of recipes).
- **Gift vouchers — « Mon bain », the recipient's use of a composed voucher** (`specs/gift-voucher-campaigns.md` rules 12–15, 23–32, 44–45, 51). Public, behind the key guard, the code always in a POST body: `POST /public/v1/gift-vouchers/use` (contents, names, validity, add-ons and extra time on offer, `horizonUntil`; a `lodging` code answers `{ kind: 'lodging' }`), `/use/slots` (`{ code, from, to, extraMinutes? }` → days of slots tagged `in` or `supplement` with `supplementCents`; an evening voucher is offered evening starts only), `/use/quote` (`{ code, start: 'YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM', extraMinutes?, addOns? }` → included, supplement, extras, `dueCents`, VAT, all the engine's), `/use/confirm` (quote + `email`, `phone`, `name?`, `termsVersionId`, `returnPath?`). The first read and the confirmation ask for the signed proof of work; a wrong code counts against the same per-visitor limits as the check. Nothing is booked, held or created before the bank confirms the payment, but a 15-minute hold: nothing due → the booking and the settle in one transaction; a remainder due → the slot and the voucher held 15 minutes and a 15-minute payment link (`{ payUrl, heldUntil }`). The bank's word (`ctx.paymentBasket` source `giftVoucherUse`) books and settles in one transaction; a payment confirmed after the lapse on a slot taken meanwhile is flagged « à rembourser » on its link (the core's refund line) and leaves the voucher intact. The `uses-lapse` job (every minute) frees the slot and the voucher of a use past its 15 minutes. The voucher pays the lines of the sale through a prepaid tender (`standaloneSale.takePrepaid`), the card the remainder; the recipient gets an automatic confirmation mail (rule 31). A voucher's `ctx.prepaid` gains an optional `unused` call: an option marked « non pris » is traced on the voucher (`gift_voucher_unused`, « Écart non utilisé » in its detail). Core, generic (SDK 2.33.0): `ctx.resourceBookingOffer` / `ctx.core.resourceBookingOffer()`, `prepaidSources.unusedFor` and `wordingOf`, `standaloneSale.takePrepaid`, `paidByOf`, `recordHistory`, `historyOf`; a dateless session quote may name its `start` (a slice of a slot really placed).
- **Gift vouchers — the back-office screens** (`specs/gift-voucher-campaigns.md` rules 2–10, 16–18, 41–42, 46–50, 54, 56–60, §4.2). New pages under « Bons cadeaux », one tab each: « Campagnes » (`/bons-cadeaux/campagnes`: cards on `xs`, table on `md+`, state, kind, validity and contents summarised by the server, « Importer une recette » listing the server's per-field refusals) and its editor (`/bons-cadeaux/campagnes/:id`: general, composition picked in the catalogue with its tariff modes and degressivity, add-ons, extra time, the lodging amount bounds, VAT and ideas moved here from the settings, the two visual pickers with an inline upload, the permanent links, the merchant card; on `md+` the price the server computes for the composition being edited, with « dont TVA » per rate), and « Visuels » (`/bons-cadeaux/visuels`: thumbnails, campaigns and valid vouchers per visual, add, rename, replace, delete). « Nouveau bon » is a full page (`/bons-cadeaux/nouveau`) in the order of the giver's tunnel — a campaign on sale or « Sur mesure », the extra time and add-ons, the server's recap, then « Payé », « Offert » or « Envoyer un lien de paiement » (e-mail mandatory) — and replaces the dialog. The voucher list gains « Campagne », « Contenu » and « Utilisé le » (a link to the fiche of the passage) with filters by campaign and kind; the detail downloads the voucher, resends the mail to another address, cancels (a confirmation naming the amount to refund within 14 days, a forcing dialog with a mandatory reason after) and marks the refund as made. The settings page keeps only `pageUrl` and the new `linkBase`. Server, additive: a campaign carries `summary` and `links` (`/<linkBase>/offrir/<slug>`, `/<linkBase>/utiliser`), the catalogue modes carry `degressiveText`, `GET /offer` (admin) carries `paymentMethods`, the voucher list carries `contentsText`, `usedOn`, `bookingId`, takes `campaignId` and `kind` filters and answers the `campaigns` to filter by, the voucher detail carries `contentLines` and `refundCents`. Client: `ImageDropField` joins the SDK.
