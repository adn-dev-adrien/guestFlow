- **Gift vouchers — campaigns, composed vouchers and one-shot vouchers** (`specs/gift-voucher-campaigns.md` rules 2–6, 9, 11–12, 19, 41, 46, 52). A voucher is sold from a campaign: the free amount of a stay (`lodging`) or a content composed from the catalogue (`resource`) — one hourly resource in the day or evening band with its included minutes, included options, add-ons and extra time offered to the buyer. Every price is read from the pricing engine for the outside customer (the band's external rate, the resource's degressive tiers by hour rank — extra time continues the ranks —, each option at its catalogue price, the VAT of each line's class) and never stored on a campaign; a campaign the engine cannot price is not offered. `offerService` is the one path of the sale: the site's controllers (key guard, proof of work, per-visitor limits, terms version) and the operator's (session, outcomes) only wrap it. New routes: `GET /public/v1/gift-vouchers/offer` now lists the campaigns (the lodging payload keeps its place), `POST /public/v1/gift-vouchers/offer/price`; admin `GET /api/gift-vouchers/catalogue`, campaigns CRUD and duplicate, `POST /campaigns/preview-price`, `GET /offer`, `POST /offer/price`; `POST /api/gift-vouchers` takes `slug` or a one-shot `composition`, `extraMinutes`, `addOns` and `outcome: paid | offered | link` (« Offert » records no sale and no VAT; the link is a site order mailed on the call). Nothing exists before the bank confirms but the order, which is priced again when its payment link is minted. A voucher pays only for its kind (`lodging` or its own resource): the check lives in the plugin's `apply`, which receives the consumer's kind from the core (`consumer`, default `lodging`). Core, generic: `standaloneSale.quote` accepts a dateless hourly session `{ band, minutes, rankOffsetMinutes }` priced by the hourly family (`sell` still refuses it); the `articles` core module exposes `makeArticle` and `liveFamilies` (SDK 2.33.0).
