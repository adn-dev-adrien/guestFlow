- **Plugins — phase 3c: hourly resources move out of the core** (spec `plugins-phase-3c-hourly-resources.md`). The core gains two plugin contracts: a **price-line contributor** (a plugin prices a resource line inside the engine's loop; a sold line still goes through its locked snapshot) and a **SAS commit contract** (a plugin step validates before the commit, bills lines tagged `sasLineKey` recomputed on every commit, writes inside the transaction). The bath's pricing, external bookings, scheduling, planning cards, SAS step, resource fields, session picker and Calendrier › Ressources become the `hourly-resources` plugin; new client slots `calendar.menu`, `resources.fields`, `reservation.resourceLine`, timed `planning.days` entries and collecting SAS steps. Without the plugin nothing is sold by the hour: the resources leave the catalogue, the property and the site, an addition answers 422, and a sold line stays frozen and read-only with its sessions. Hours, slots and the free hour now apply to `per_hour` resources only.
