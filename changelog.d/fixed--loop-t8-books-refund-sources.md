- A refund of a stay's own trop-perçu (a solde line discounted before the arrival) is no longer read as note money already refunded: a note line offered afterwards keeps its credit, can be un-offered, and a cash note still refunds through the caisse interne (pricing-components rule 113, loop t8 B8-2).
- A note line discounted refunds its excess on « Prestations complémentaires » (70600010), as an offered one does, instead of « Hébergement » (rule 111 reading, loop t8 B8-1).
- On a platform stay whose arrival complement was collected before the arrival, offering, discounting or lowering a line of it now asks to confirm the trop-perçu and keeps it owed back on the line's poste; the money no longer vanishes (rule 114, loop t8 B8-5, also on master). Removing such a line is refused (offer it instead).
- A line lowered before the arrival on a collected complement no longer makes every later sale of the stay fail with « Une prestation vendue à l'arrivée ne peut être ni retirée ni réduite. » (loop t8 B8-6).
