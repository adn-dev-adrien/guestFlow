- **Textes des emails: writing one sentence at a time** (specs/guest-email-texts-made-simple.md rules 37–41, UI/UX review of 2026-10-18). Variables are offered on demand — « + Variable » or the `[` key opens a list filtered as you type — instead of a wall of chips under every field. A mistyped variable is refused under its own field with the closest variable as a one-tap « Remplacer », and the preview keeps the last valid email instead of disappearing. One language at a time: FR | EN at the top, the English field with the French above it, and the server's « N à traduire » beside the switch. A click on a sentence of the preview opens its field (beside it on a computer, in a sheet on a phone); the preview is tagged inside its rolled-back transaction only, and a test guarantees that no tag reaches an email.
- **One name per email, in the order of the stay** (rule 40): « Accès et arrivée », « Préparation du séjour », « Merci et avis »… replace « J-2 », « Séquence — J-2 arrivée » on Emails › Modèles, Textes des emails, the send dialogs and the exported file. Emails › Modèles is a timeline grouped by moment; « Manuel » and « Modèle livré » are no longer repeated on every row; a shipped email can be switched off but no longer deleted (« ⋯ » holds the editor, the texts, the mode — switching to automatic asks first — and, for an email of one's own, « Supprimer »).
- **« Quand ? » in one sentence** (rule 39): « Envoyé 2 jours avant l'arrivée », edited as « Envoyé [n] jours [avant / après / le jour même de] l'arrivée », refused live beyond 90 days, with the day it would leave for the next stay. The sequence emails, whose days the sequence computes, show their moment as a fixed sentence.
