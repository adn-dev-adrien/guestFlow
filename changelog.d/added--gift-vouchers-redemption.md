- **Gift vouchers — paying a booking with a voucher** (`specs/gift-vouchers.md` rules 36-42). The booking engine of the website gains a « Bon cadeau » field: the code is checked in the browser before any call, the server holds the voucher's amount for the quote (never more than the stay, one winner when two requests race for the last cents) and returns the amount applied and « À payer aujourd'hui ». The voucher pays the acompte first, then the solde; the card link is minted for what the chosen mode still asks, and when nothing is due the quote becomes a reservation at once, with no link. The voucher is a means of payment, not a discount: the stay keeps its price, tourist tax and commission, and carries the amount as money received « Bon cadeau », its own line in the fiche's payments. The hold is given back when the quote is deleted or lapses unpaid. The fiche gains « Payer par bon cadeau » (undoable) and, on a cancelled stay, « Recréditer le bon » for the voucher part (the indemnity is taken from the card first); an expired voucher that receives a refund is extended by 3 months. Core, generic (SDK 2.33.0): `ctx.prepaid` and the modules `prepaidSources` / `prepaidReceipts` — a prepaid tender line is received money, never expected at the bank nor booked as an encaissement (`specs/mixed-method-payments.md` rules 59-62).
