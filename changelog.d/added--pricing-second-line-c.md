- **A second sale of the same article is added as a line of its own** (« une deuxième vente du même article s'ajoute en ligne »; spec `pricing-components.md` rules 13 and 85). Selling an article the stay already sells (fiche « Ajouter un produit », SAS, shop) writes a separate line with its own frozen price, source, moment and status instead of completing the first one. Offering, paying or moving targets one line; the fiche and the SAS group an article's lines under one title (« Bière locale — 3 × 6,50 € (2 lignes) ») and the e-mails name it once. A session added to an already paid line makes a new line; the free units and minutes of an article are counted once. Existing stays (one row per article) are unchanged.
