- **The « Suivi financier », the accounting journal and payment links read the sold lines — for stays booked after this version is installed.** The « Suivi financier » computes the pre-tax amount line by line and still shows what the stay earns, drawn from the receipts as today; each receipt is spread over the sold lines and its VAT computed once per class and per rate, like credit notes; a new payment link names the lines it collects (« Solde séjour — Ménage »), each at its rate, for the same amount. Figures that move: none on stays already booked — their amounts, their next links and the months already declared stay those of today (production copy of 11/11: no difference). On a stay booked afterwards, the pre-tax amount in the « Suivi financier » may differ by one cent, and so may the VAT of a receipt (a single rounding per rate).
