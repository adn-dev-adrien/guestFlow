- plugin:hourly-resources An outside session booked at the planning (« séance hors séjour ») is priced by the hourly engine with its real date, week or weekend, for any start and duration the planning allows — off the slot grid it was billed at the guests' flat day rate (a Sunday 13:00–14:30 outside booking cost 45 €). An outside band with no first-hour price is refused with a sentence on every path (planning, site, vouchers, « Mon bain »), never priced 0 €. A booking that carries a lodging keeps the week grid and its free hour exactly as before.
