- **The departure SAS recap is priced by the server** (specs/sas-departure-recap-server-side.md): the end-of-stay lines, « Sous-total arrivée », « Total à percevoir », « Rien à percevoir » and whether a settlement is asked now come from a new preview (`POST /api/reservations/:id/sas/departure/preview`), computed by the very function the check-out commit runs. The dialog sends intentions only (missing linen by item, the ménage as a yes/no, the extinguisher quantities, stored lines by a server-issued key) and renders the answer; the figure the operator collects is the figure the books keep.
- The missing linen and the end-of-stay ménage are priced by the server from « Facturables » and the property (they were stored at the amount the browser had loaded), and the lines already stored on the fiche are kept by the server instead of being sent back.
- The operator's « Ajustement » line is no longer shown as an offerable line at check-out: an end-of-stay or arrival total adjusted on the fiche shows « Montant ajusté sur la fiche (calcul : … ») under the total, and offering a recalled arrival line leaves the adjusted amount.
- A check-out recorded on a total other than the one the recap showed is announced in the dialog and noted in the fiche's history. The plugin SDK moves to 2.1.0 (additions only: `planDepartureSas`, `resolveSasDepartureItems`, `buildDepartureSeed`, the `departureSasPlan` module).
