- **iCal — a cancelled stay no longer hands its fiche to a new booking on nameless feeds** (audit 2026-10-27, `ical-summary-fallback-cross-uid.md` rule 7). The same-summary re-claim is skipped when the summary names nobody (Airbnb « Reserved », Gîtes de France « BOOKED »): the new stay is created and the old one goes to the cancellation approval. A locked fiche no longer swallows the new booking behind a misleading date-drift alert.
- **iCal — two platforms' nameless stays on the same dates are no longer merged** (`ical-import-hardening.md` rule 15): both are created and the second is flagged in conflict.
- **iCal — the Booking-style « CLOSED - Not available » feed is a setting of the source** (`lodgify-decommission.md` rule 16): `booking-com`, `BookingTente` and any other spelling keep their stays; switch in the URL editor of « Plateformes & iCal ». Migration: new nullable column `ical_sources.feedMarksStaysClosed`, one-shot `ical_feed_marks_stays_closed_v1` ticks every existing « booking… » source.
- **iCal — the closure guard only drops the closure's own echo** (`lodgify-decommission.md` rule 17): a new booking partly over a closure is created and flagged, a stay moved onto a closure follows its feed (flagged) or raises its date-drift alert when locked.
- **iCal — a stay already cancelled in GuestFlow is never proposed for deletion** (`ical-cancellation-approval.md` rule 13): no alert when its feed drops it, and approving an older alert keeps the row and its refunds (`outcome = 'already_cancelled'`).
- **Platforms — saving an iCal source keeps its platform's spelling** (`normalize-platform-names.md` rules 22-23): `GitesDeFrance` no longer becomes `Gitesdefrance` (second catalogue row at 0 % commission, GdF intake at 0 %). The boot de-duplication now groups by slug and merges such pairs into the most-referenced row (never renaming an established spelling), settings and references — cancellation compensations included.
- **iCal parser** (`ical-import-hardening.md` rule 16): `BEGIN:VEVENT` with trailing blanks, `DURATION` without `DTEND`, and a named stay whose description says « non disponible » / « blocked » are now read correctly. The export format (`DTSTART:YYYYMMDD`) is deliberately unchanged.
