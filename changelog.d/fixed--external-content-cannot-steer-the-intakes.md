- **Mail intakes (Airbnb, Gîtes de France, Booking)** no longer let the text a guest types steer what is written. Airbnb: only the structured part of the mail after « Envoyer un message à » is read, the confirmation code must match the reservation link and the arrival the subject, and a repeated key header refuses the mail (airbnb-intake 1.1.1). Gîtes de France: every field is read on its own labelled line, dates must be real and the stay at most 120 nights (gites-de-france-intake 1.4.1). Booking: a cancelled fiche or another platform's fiche is never matched, several candidates are refused, and the e-mail must be a single address (booking-intake 1.3.6). The « Voir sur Airbnb » link accepts only exact Airbnb hosts. A cancelled fiche is no longer matched by the Gîtes de France intake either.
