- **A template without its English version is never sent to an English-speaking guest** (operator decision, specs/email-language-fr-en.md rule 9). The manual send is refused with « Version anglaise manquante pour ce modèle. », the send dialog says so and marks the template, Emails › Modèles badges it « EN manquant »; the automatic passes skip it and the history shows why. It used to leave in French with English dates and amounts. Every shipped template has its English version.
- **One amount format in every guest e-mail** (specs/email-language-fr-en.md rule 10): « 1 234,56 € » with the thousands grouped, « €1,234.56 » in English, and no « ,00 » on a whole amount — the caution reads « 500 € » like the « 45 € » of the stay texts.
