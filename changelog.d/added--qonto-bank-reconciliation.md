- **Bank reconciliation (online-payment plugin 1.2.0, SDK 1.6.0).** The credits of the Qonto account are read (every 8 hours, and « Actualiser ») and matched to the payments the fiches expect — platform payouts, payment-link payouts, guests' transfers and the on-site card batches — to surface the gaps (`specs/qonto-bank-reconciliation.md`). A « Rapprochement bancaire » card under the payments ledger of the fiche, a page under Suivi financier, a subject on the home and a settings card (account, start date, recognition patterns, fees). A proposal is never a decision: « Confirmer » marks the échéance received on the fiche at the date of the transfer through the core's own « Marquer perçu » (same guards and history), « Défaire » puts the fiche back, and nothing is ever changed on an échéance already marked received. New core modules exposed to plugins: `reservationLedger`, `reservationPaymentMark`, `platformDisplay`; the Qonto client gains `listTransactions`; a recette tool loads a Qonto CSV export into a shadow database (`server/scripts/bank-transactions-from-qonto-csv.js`).
- **Migration.** New plugin tables `bank_transactions`, `bank_reconciliation_lines`, `bank_reconciliation_fiche_writes`, `bank_reconciliation_discards`, `bank_transactions_outside` (migration `plugin:online-payment:reconciliation_v1`); no core table changes. « Effacer aussi ses données » drops them; the fiches already marked stay marked.
