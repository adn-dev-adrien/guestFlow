- plugin:guest-app **Demo mode** (spec `guest-app-demo-mode.md`). Studio › Réglages › « Lien de démo » gives one link (QR, « Copier le lien », « Ouvrir », « Régénérer le lien ») that opens the guest app on a fictitious stay, « Camille », 2 adults: any enabled lodging, before arrival, during the stay or after departure, switched from a « Démo » bar. The whole journey is tried (cart, bath slot, order, both payment choices, gate with a fictitious keypad code, simulated lights, real readings) and nothing is written: no reservation line, planning hold, payment link, stock count, push or e-mail. The simulated orders are kept in memory for the demo's life and shown in « Mes commandes » with a « Démo » chip. +40 server tests, +15 guest app tests, +4 studio tests.
