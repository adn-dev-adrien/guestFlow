- **A complement deferred to the check-out no longer asks again for an end of stay already collected** (specs/defer-arrival-complement-to-checkout.md rule 22, specs/reservation-payment-ledger.md rule 69, audit R28-C-M1): a stay left « Perçu en fin de séjour » over a 0 € (or paid) arrival complement drew « Complément de fin de séjour · À percevoir au départ » for money already counted in « Total perçu »; its « Marquer perçu » moved the end of stay's accounting entry to another month and « Annuler » erased the collection. The fiche merges the two complements only while the arrival one is still to collect, leaves a settled end of stay on its own paid card, and a mark of the complement never re-dates nor clears it. Once paid, the arrival complement and the end of stay each keep their own card and their own « Annuler ».
