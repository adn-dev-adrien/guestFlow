- **Mail intakes (Airbnb, Gîtes de France, Booking)** no longer trust a forged sender: a mail from an unverified sender is stored as a proposal « Expéditeur non vérifié », never auto-applied; the mailbox password is sent only over TLS. A guest's text can no longer steer the application; an Airbnb or Gîtes de France pass reads at most 200 unauthenticated mails (the newest half and the oldest half; the home shows « Mails Airbnb non lus : N (afflux). »; a mail with a good DKIM verdict is read whatever the cap), skips known Message-IDs and remembers non-intakes as ignored; a mail part over 256 kB is skipped and a forged Date header no longer blocks the intakes. « Appliquer » writes a fiche once and no longer erases the planned moments of a planning-card option. Mail for an unmapped gîte waits and is applied once mapped; a started stay is refused before a client is created. The four mail plugins now require SDK 2.15.
- **Website booking is tightened** (WordPress plugin 1.17.1 verifies the archive SHA-256): a phone must have 8 to 15 digits, only `+` and separators allowed, else 422 `VALIDATION_FAILED` (reason `phoneInvalid`); name, phone and e-mail are capped (100, 40, 254); a stay longer than the season maximum is 409 `MAX_NIGHTS`, off changeover days 409 `CHANGEOVER`; an arrival or departure time the house cannot take is 409 `STAY_TIMES_UNAVAILABLE` before any payment; only public-catalogue options are accepted; `/quote` and `/booking-requests` refuse a stay starting in the past, longer than 365 nights or ending more than 730 days ahead, and `/quote` refuses a party over capacity (409 `OVER_CAPACITY`); a wrong JSON type gets the documented 422 (no 500); the read-only key works again.
- **Closures and properties**: a malformed `propertyId` is 400 `INVALID_PROPERTY` (it used to close every property); `POST /api/properties` refuses an unknown `touristTaxMode`; a null no longer clears the access code or the Wi-Fi password.
- A Booking event wholly over held nights is created and flagged; a platform price check and date labels are read on the operator's day.
