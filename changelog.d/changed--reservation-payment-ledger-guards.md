- Recording a payment from the fiche is now checked by the server: no date in the future, no 0 € bucket (a converted quote carrying a 0 € acompte could be « paid »), the amount the operator saw must still be the stored one, and a caution is returned only after it was received and not before. « Enregistrer » no longer writes the caution state; only its row does. A past or ongoing fiche keeps its payment and caution actions.
- An open payment link whose amount the fiche changes is withdrawn at save (and deactivated at the provider); its row then offers « Renvoyer la demande ». If the guest pays the old link anyway, the payment is recorded on the fiche at its real amount. A link paid just before the save refuses the change.
- The live preview of the fiche now prices « Sans acompte » like the save does.
- The fiche shows the payments as stored (the same « Reste à percevoir » as Finance); the live figures appear only once the fiche is modified, never on a past one. A stay imported without a price reads « Prix à renseigner ». Receiving a platform payout records the transfer with the mark, without saving the whole fiche — past stays included — and a stay priced under an earlier rule records its payment with a warning instead of refusing it. Undoing a received acompte keeps its amount. A paid solde of a stay without acompte is no longer raised when the stay costs more: the difference is owed as the arrival complement.
- A payment already recorded can no longer be marked again (a second tab would have re-dated it and moved its accounting entry); the transfer and commission of a payout are only accepted on its reception, on a platform stay, and a commission above the billed amount is refused. « Virement reçu » is typed when the commission is unknown. A payment recorded on a stay whose amounts drifted is now split on its stored amounts in the export (never booked whole on the tourist-tax account). The sticky total bar shows the stored figures with the stored ledger; an undone payment proposes its date again when redone.
- « Encaisser en une fois » and the arrival check-in payment now split a payment whose amounts drifted on its stored amounts too, like the fiche and online payments: a solde is never booked whole on the tourist-tax account any more. The Comptabilité page warns about any entry of the month that is (« À vérifier avant l'envoi au cabinet »). On an unmodified fiche the pricing summary shows the stored figures, like the ledger and the total bar.
