A past or ongoing fiche keeps its payment and caution actions.
- An open payment link whose amount the fiche changes is withdrawn at save (and deactivated at the provider); its row then offers « Renvoyer la demande ». If the guest pays the old link anyway, the payment is recorded on the fiche at its real amount. A link paid just before the save refuses the change.
