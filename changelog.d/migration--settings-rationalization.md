- **Settings rationalization** (spec `settings-rationalization.md`). On startup: drops the dead `app_settings` columns (payment timings, `paymentLastMinuteDays`, `paymentFullPaymentDueDaysBefore`, the Google service-account pair, `neatStoreId`, `smtpPort`, `allowEditPastReservations`, `emailAutoSendEnabled`) and the `payment_methods` table. SMTP identity columns equal to their derived value are emptied — no effective value changes. When the auto-send switch was off (the production state), every email template is set to « Manuel » before the switch column goes, so no email starts leaving on its own; each one-shot step is recorded in `migrations`.
