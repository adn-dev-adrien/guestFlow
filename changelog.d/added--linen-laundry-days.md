- **Laundry days — several trips a week and a laundry turnaround** (spec `linen-laundry-days.md`, 2026-10-28). The `linen` plugin (1.2.0) takes a set of laundry weekdays and a turnaround (1 to 14 days) instead of one weekday: the Linge page edits both, the Planning shows a card on every laundry day with where each pick-up comes from (« déposé mar. 21 ») and what still waits at the laundry, and the stock projection keeps linen unavailable until it is ready. A change applies from tomorrow and the past keeps its rhythm. New `GET/PUT /api/laundry/schedule`, `POST /api/laundry/schedule/preview`, `GET /api/planning/laundry/days`; removed `GET /api/planning/laundry` and `GET /api/planning/linen-inventory`. The shortage alert says when the shortage ends (`lastShortageDate`, `laterShortage`).
