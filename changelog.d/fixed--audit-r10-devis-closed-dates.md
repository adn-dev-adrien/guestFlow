- **A devis can no longer be moved onto closed nights** (spec `payment-link-lifecycle.md` rule 55, audit R10-1, 2026-10-31). A devis holding an open payment link could be re-dated onto an establishment closure; its link stayed payable and a payment confirmed the guest on closed nights. Creating a devis, or changing its dates or logement, onto nights a closure covers is now refused (409 « Logement fermé sur ces dates : devis non enregistré. », with the closure to open in the dialog); a devis already there keeps its other edits. And should a devis link still be paid for closed nights, the stay is booked without the guest confirmation and the admin is told « Paiement reçu sur des dates fermées … déplacer le séjour ou le rembourser ». +9 server tests, +1 client test.
