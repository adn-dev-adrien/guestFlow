- Guest app « Mon séjour » opens at J-7 (specs/guest-app.md rules 65-89): the shop, the nordic bath and « Mes commandes » work before the arrival; an order taken before it is collected on the arrival complement (`midStayOrderCommit`), card or « Régler à l'arrivée », a card-paid order is recorded as an arrival-moment note the engine subtracts and is never collected again at the arrival SAS.
- The delivery and pickup times of an order are the stay's own days and the hours the guests are present and the shop is open; the nordic bath is bookable from the app inside the stay and the bath's own hours, with the heat-up counted from now, the other bookings of the bath, the free time at the day rate and the evening supplement billed once with the order; a crafted time or slot that is not offered is refused first (`DELIVERY_TIME_UNAVAILABLE`, `SLOT_TAKEN`, `SLOT_UNAVAILABLE`).
- The arrival SAS lists the stay's app orders in a collapsible step (articles, quantities, prices, delivery time, totals), lets an unpaid order be changed (quantity, article, bath session, the whole order; `POST /api/guest-app/orders/:id/amend`), and keeps a card-paid order read-only.
- A cancelled stay cancels its app orders (new status `cancelled`); a card-paid one is flagged « à rembourser » on its link. J-7 and J-2 e-mails gain the flags `hasStayAppShop`, `hasStayAppBath` and `hasStayAppShopOnly`; `specs/guest-app-livret/import.mjs` adds the J-7 paragraph (link and what the guests can already do), no send mode touched.
- Plugins: `guest-app` 1.1.0, `hourly-resources` 1.6.0 (SDK 2.26: `heatFrom`, `orderSupplement`, `ctx.core.payments.flagBasketRefund`).
