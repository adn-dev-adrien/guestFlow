- **Pricing — a sale reads back the stored extra guests and tourist tax** (`specs/pricing-components.md` rule 8). Selling, offering, removing or discounting a line no longer recalculates the extra-guest surcharge or the tourist tax: a tax or surcharge setting changed since then no longer moves the stay being sold on. A change to the stay recalculates them as before. Since rule 93 the stay moves by the line only, even when its stored values no longer match (rewritten without recalculation).
