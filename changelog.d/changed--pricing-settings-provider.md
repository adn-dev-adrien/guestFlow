- **Pricing settings provider** (spec `pricing-components.md` rule 50): `SettingsProvider.for(component, scope)` resolves article, then property, then global, then the engine's own default; a key's scope is the narrowest layer allowed to set it and a NULL column is a value. `vat`, `platformPrice` and `articles` are registered, and the pricing steps, the catalogue, the stay clock and the platform grid now read their parameters through it instead of the property row. No price moves.
