- **Dashboard — new reservations stay 24 hours** (spec `dashboard-ical-new-reservations.md`, amended 2026-10-06). The « Nouvelles réservations » card keeps each reservation for exactly 24 hours after it arrives, instead of clearing at UTC midnight (a booking imported in the evening used to vanish within hours). It now lists every origin — platform imports, paid website bookings (« Site ») and reservations entered in GuestFlow (their channel, e.g. « Direct ») — and refreshes every 5 minutes. Endpoint renamed `/api/dashboard/ical-new-today` → `/api/dashboard/new-reservations`, component `IcalNewReservationsAlert` → `NewReservationsAlert`. 10 server tests (was 7), 6 client tests (was 5).
