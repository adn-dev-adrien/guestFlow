- **The end-of-stay complement is collected after the check-out only**: « Marquer perçu », « Encaisser en une fois » and a bank reconciliation refuse it before the check-out — the departure SAS committed, or the last day reached without the SAS plugin (409 `END_OF_STAY_BEFORE_CHECKOUT`, « Complément de fin de séjour encaissable après le départ. »); « Tout solder » leaves it out.
- **An unlocked fiche keeps its final price**: « Déverrouiller cette fiche » on a started stay accepts only changes that leave the final price and the tourist tax unchanged (409 `UNLOCKED_PRICE_CHANGED`); on a platform that remits the tax, the withheld tax and a brut correction are entered on the locked fiche.
- **Dates are bounded**: a date more than 5 years ahead or back is refused (« Date trop lointaine / trop ancienne : 5 ans au plus. ») on stays, devis, iCal date changes, closures and the custom finance window, and the server now judges every operator-typed date (payment marks, installments, refunds, penalties, compensations, calendar notes, seasons, laundry, school holidays, resource bookings) — several took any text before. Bad stay dates answer 400 `INVALID_STAY_DATES`; a stay lasts at most 366 nights. Dates already stored are never re-judged.
- **An acompte paid with several methods is paid whole** (all on the books or all in the caisse interne); an installment is always on the books, never with a reste or a geste commercial. These are the amounts a cancellation keeps.
- **Recording a payment is checked by the server**: no future date, no 0 € bucket, the amount seen must still be the stored one, a caution is returned only after it was received, a payment already recorded cannot be marked again, a payout commission above the billed amount is refused. An open link whose amount the fiche changes is withdrawn at save; if the guest pays it anyway it is recorded at the amount really paid.
- **Raising the price of a fiche whose end-of-stay complement is collected** is refused (`MID_STAY_EXTRAS_CLOSED`) — always before arrival, and on a started locked fiche unless an unpaid payment can take the rise; « Percevoir en fin de séjour » is refused when it is already collected (409 `DEFERRAL_END_OF_STAY_SETTLED`).
- **Clients**: « À venir » opens first, « Passés » and « Sans séjour » (with « Nettoyer les clients ») beside it; the per-row delete icon is gone (delete from the client sheet); `GET /api/clients/directory` keeps `items` for one release.
- **Arrival SAS**: the breakfast offer price is computed by the server; in Calendrier › Ressources, a resource booking's total comes from the server.
- **Options & ressources** are read by category and edited in a side panel, with search, property filter and category chips.
