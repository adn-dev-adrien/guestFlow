- **Extras on a locked fiche** (spec `mid-stay-extras-on-locked-fiche.md`, 2026-10-26). Once a stay has started, options, resources and custom lines can be added and corrected without « Déverrouiller cette fiche », until the end-of-stay complement is collected; dates, guests, notes and deletion stay locked. Lines sold at arrival can be increased but neither removed nor reduced (`ARRIVAL_EXTRAS_FROZEN`), and a save after the collection is refused (`MID_STAY_EXTRAS_CLOSED`). `GET /api/reservations/:id` now returns the server-decided `editLock`, which replaces the client's own date comparison. +28 server tests, +11 client tests.
