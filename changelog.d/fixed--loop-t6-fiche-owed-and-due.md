- **A paid line offered on a platform stay can be refunded from the fiche** (`specs/pricing-components.md` rule 106 a): the money a note paid to the establishment and the operator confirmed as owed shows as the « Trop-perçu » row with « Rembourser », the refund dialog opens pre-filled with it, and the refund settles it. Before, the obligation existed but no screen of the fiche showed or paid it, and a refund on the trop-perçu was refused.
- **Cash note money is refunded from the caisse interne by default** (rule 106 b): when the excess is money a cash note took, the refund dialog defaults to « Caisse interne », so the default refund writes no credit note for money never booked.
- **The sale panel's « Reste à payer » reads what the stay still asks** (rule 106 c): its open acompte, solde, installments and complements, instead of every line minus what was paid on lines only — a fully paid stay no longer shows its whole price. On a platform stay it is the net still to collect, the same figure as the payment register's « Reste à percevoir ». Display only; no stored amount moves.
- **The « Déjà réglé » credit is listed in the fiche's sale panel** (rule 104 e) as a read-only line; at the departure it no longer offers « Offrir » (which answered « Ligne introuvable »), and an arrival SAS draft no longer lists a credit its total no longer takes.
- **A cancelled stay's refund dialog offers a card order paid before the arrival on its own line** (rule 106 d), no longer inside « Hébergement », and refunding it there closes the amount the stay owed back for it.
