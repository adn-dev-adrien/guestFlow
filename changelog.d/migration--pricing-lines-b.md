- **Pricing lines, stored data (pricing refonte, PR B)**: every line of a reservation (option, resource, free line) gets a stable number that a fiche save keeps, and new columns record when a line is collected and what was sold (article, family, label). Each stay gets its « Hébergement » and « Taxe de séjour » lines, and tables for refund obligations, refund nettings and payment allocations are added. Everything is derived from what is already stored: no amount is rewritten, the migration takes well under a second on the production copy, a second boot changes nothing, and the previous release keeps running on the migrated tables.
