- **Devis: an OTA devis no longer hands a direct deadline to its stay** (specs/deposit-blocks-the-dates.md rule 24): a stay converted from a devis on Booking (or another OTA) and then switched to Direct took the devis's validity date as its solde deadline, months before an identical direct stay. Only a direct devis's promise is kept now.
- **Devis: the stored schedule always matches the fiche and the PDF** (specs/deposit-blocks-the-dates.md rule 25): after a change of « Solde dû … jours avant », a devis outside the single-payment window kept its old solde date in the e-mail tokens and the conversion while the fiche and the PDF read the new one; a legacy devis with no validity date is stamped with the date it reads, so its conversion and its stay read one date. Re-stored at boot and after a logement save.
- **Devis numbers are never handed out twice** (specs/public-request-abuse-and-grouping.md rule 35): a devis deleted from its fiche, or with its client or logement, gave its number back to the next devis of the month. Every deleted devis now retires its number.
- **Refund register with a payment in several methods** (specs/mixed-method-payments.md rules 54 and 55): a geste that the accommodation cannot hold (a complement paid before the solde) comes off the other lines, so no line offers more than it collected; the lines of a split payment add up to the money received to the cent (a pool with a caisse-interne share read one cent short).
- **Console e-mails greet the mail server with the sender's domain** (specs/admin-account-management.md rule 21): login codes, export links, closing and renewal mails no longer say `EHLO [127.0.0.1]`.
