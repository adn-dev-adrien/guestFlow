- **Pricing — a sale moves the stay by its line, and by nothing else** (`specs/pricing-components.md` rule 93). Selling, offering, discounting, moving or removing a line now adds, changes or removes that line's amount only: the nights, the extra guests, the tourist tax, the platform price, the deposit, the balance and the other buckets keep their stored figures, even on a stay today's engine no longer reproduces (a platform's tax mode changed since, a price agreed by hand). The quote is exactly what is written. A line already sold is kept when its article is no longer offered at the lodging, and an hourly line keeps the hours it billed; a started stay no longer refuses every sale for that reason. A card order paid before the arrival is no longer asked again at the door.
