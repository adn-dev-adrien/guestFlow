- **Blank time on a stay update** (rule 23): it keeps the stored time instead of writing 15:00 / 10:00.
- **Reservation number refusals** (rule 24): over 64 characters or with control characters, the message names the field, and the fiche's field stops at 64. **A devis's money refusal** answers French text and its code, as a stay's does (rule 19).
