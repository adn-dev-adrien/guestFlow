- **Console (control plane), review fixes — billing:** « Prolonger » no longer leads to a second invoice: a customer has one unsettled invoice at a time, its reminders follow the customer's real end date, and its payment adds to the extended date. An invoice Qonto totals differently is held until « Réessayer la facture » instead of being numbered and cancelled every night; every Qonto creation call carries an idempotency key; an email leaves once even on two clicks; a payment recorded by hand counts once (`409 STALE` on a double click), also settles an invoice still in preparation, and gives way to a card payment that just landed.
- **Console, operator access:** a wrong password no longer locks the account (only wrong codes do), a TOTP code is accepted once, changing the second factor asks for the password, and backup codes are hashed like passwords.
- **Console, deprovisioning:** nothing is erased before « Processus et route arrêtés » is ticked, the export archive is deleted with its link (after 30 days, at reactivation, at erasure), and an unfinished rename blocks deprovisioning; until the directory is moved the login page sends users to the old address.
- **Console, catalogue:** prices and quotas are read by the server as typed (an emptied price or « 1,5 » units is refused) and the confirmation lists every price and quota the change touches.
- **First administrator script:** refuses a database at another version instead of migrating it.
