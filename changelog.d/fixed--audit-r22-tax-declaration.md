- **Added guests' tourist tax, declaration** (spec `platform-added-guests-tourist-tax.md` rules 5, 19-24, audit R22). Ticking a row on the day its complement was collected no longer splits it: the tick records whether the declared row held the addition. The added row reads the adults stored with its amount, a gap with nobody added says so and claims no adult, its own tick is cleared when the complement is unticked, and ticking a row that no longer exists answers 409. On a percentage property the comparison reads the occupants of the paid party, not the persons billed. The line is renamed « Complément de taxe de séjour » on the fiche, SAS recap, departure recall, J-2 email and declaration page. The declaration page reads the month again after a tick.
