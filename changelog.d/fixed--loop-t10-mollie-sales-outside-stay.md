- **Mollie — sales outside a stay** (spec `mollie-rolling-reserve-and-incoming-payments.md` rules 32-33, 2026-11-17). The payment link of an outside sale (no stay) now yields its two Mollie tranches on the reconciliation page, in « Versements à venir » and as a `link` entry of the Mollie journal; its split is stored with no reservation id under `sale:<sale key>:link-<id>`. A document paid by card on several days gets one split per card day: the invoice number keys the first card day only, so two card payments are no longer merged into one tranche.
- **Accounting export — Mollie entries** (rules 28-29). The synthesis reads the Mollie entries with the export's own settings (« Exporter les versements Mollie reçus » off now totals like the CSV); an anonymous outside sale paid through Mollie is labelled with the sale's label instead of « RÉSERVATION #null ».
