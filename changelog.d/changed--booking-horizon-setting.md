- How far ahead the public may book is now a setting instead of a constant. The lodging's « Horizon de réservation en ligne » (Paramètres › Établissement, 1 to 36 months) replaces the 730 days of the public booking API, and bounds the booking engine, its availability calendar and the next-availability search: **the site's horizon goes from 2 years to 12 months by default**. A date past it is refused with « Réservable jusqu'au … » and the availability answer carries `horizonUntil`, so the WordPress calendar greys out the days past it, stops its navigation at that month and offers month chips to jump to one (plugin 1.20.0). Each hourly resource has its own « Réservable jusqu'à » (3 months by default, 1 to 24) that the guest app's slots stop at. The back-office is never bounded.
