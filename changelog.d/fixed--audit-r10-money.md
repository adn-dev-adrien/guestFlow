- **Payments: an installment plan no longer hides a payment received after a link was withdrawn** (specs/payment-link-lifecycle.md rule 63, audit R10-M1): such a payment blocks every new request until it is settled (rule 56), but once a plan existed the fiche no longer showed « Marquer remboursé » / « Enregistrer sur le séjour », and the only way out was to delete the plan. The ledger now lists those payments on a row of their own (« Paiement après retrait », « À traiter ») with both actions, and a plan can no longer be made or resized while one waits: 409 `UNSETTLED_WITHDRAWN_PAYMENT`, and the échéancier card gives the same reason.
