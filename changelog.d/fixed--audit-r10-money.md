- **Payments: beside an installment plan, only an échéance can be paid, and a payment received after a link was withdrawn is never hidden** (specs/payment-link-lifecycle.md rule 63, audit R10-M1): such a payment blocks every new request until it is settled (rule 56), but once a plan existed the fiche no longer showed « Marquer remboursé » / « Enregistrer sur le séjour », and the only way out was to delete the plan. The ledger now lists those payments on a row of their own (« Paiement après retrait », « À traiter »). A plan can no longer be made or resized while an acompte / solde / séjour link may still be paid: a payment waiting to be settled (409 `UNSETTLED_WITHDRAWN_PAYMENT`) a deactivation not yet confirmed by the provider (409 `LINK_DEACTIVATION_PENDING`), or a lapsed link's last check still owed (409 `LINK_LAST_LOOK_PENDING`). The échéancier card gives the reason and the fiche re-reads its links after such a refusal. The stay is checked again after the provider answered, and the plan is made under the stay's payment-link lock, so a link minted at the same moment never lands beside it. An échéance paid after its withdrawal is recorded on that échéance; an acompte / solde / séjour payment beside a plan can only be marked refunded (`INSTALLMENT_PLAN_IN_PLACE`), so it is never booked on top of the échéances.
