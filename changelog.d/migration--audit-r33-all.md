- **Open devis re-stored as one payment** (specs `deposit-blocks-the-dates.md` rule 20): at every boot, and after every save of a logement for its devis, an open devis whose solde falls on or before its validity date but whose stored row still holds an acompte is re-written as the single payment (acompte 0, the pre-arrival total due on the validity date, one history entry), and its open payment links billing the old amounts are withdrawn (`amount-changed`). Converted devis are never touched. Idempotent; on the production copy of 2026-11-09 no row qualifies.
