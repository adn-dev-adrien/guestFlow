- **Google Vacation Rentals feed** (spec `google-vacation-rentals-feed.md`, 2026-10-26). New plugin `google-vacation-rentals`: for every enabled property it computes, with the website's own quote, the tax-included total of every stay of the horizon (arrival × length up to 14 nights × party size), each night's availability, minimum/maximum stay, changeover days and closures, and pushes the difference to Google's ARI endpoints. Simulation by default (the XML is written on the server, nothing is sent) until Google opens a partner account. Triggers: a reservation or iCal import (within a minute), a saved tariff (debounced 30 s), a nightly full re-send; a « Vérifier les prix » check compares what was sent with the website's quote. Settings tab under Intégrations. Core: the generic `pricing.changed` plugin event emitted by every pricing, option, option-default, closure and property write that can move a price; plugin SDK 2.4.0 (`ctx.events.emit`, `coreModule('directQuote')`, the website quote moved to `utils/directQuote.js` unchanged). WordPress plugin 1.17.0: the booking block opens with the `checkin`, `checkout` and `guests` of its address. +66 server tests, +6 client tests.
