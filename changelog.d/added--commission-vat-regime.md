- **Commissions follow the platform's VAT regime** (spec `platform-host-statement-and-commission-vat.md`, 2026-11-26). *Comptabilité › Plateformes* replaces the « TVA déductible » box by a « Régime de TVA » per platform — France (20 % deductible), UE (autoliquidation) or Sans TVA — and choosing one proposes its commission account (`62260500` / `62260300` / `62260600`, three roles of the plan; a typed account is never overwritten). The export self-assesses the VAT of an EU commission (two lines: `44566200` deductible, `44520000` due, switchable by `reverseChargeLines`), shows it in *Synthèse › TVA*, and writes a « Code TVA » in a new last column of the CSV (`TI` / `TN` / `TR` by rate of a sale, `INTRACOM` on an EU commission, editable in the plan; no other column moves). The Mollie journal's fee account defaults to the no-VAT commission account `62260600`. « Revenu brut » becomes « Montant hôte » on platform rows.
