- **Every price of an hourly resource of the planning is explicit and set in « Prix »** (spec `gift-voucher-week-weekend-and-print.md` §3.A). The guests' and outside grids (week and weekend, first hour and following hours) are entered in the section « Prix » of the resource panel, contributed by the `hourly-resources` plugin (new `resources.priceFields` slot, `ctx.resourceValidator`, SDK 2.38.0); « Horaires » keeps hours and planning settings. No price falls back to another: an outside first hour at 0 is not sold, an empty following-hours field no longer means « same as the first hour », « Prix par logement » keeps only « Heure offerte par logement » (a `per_hour` resource with no planning card keeps « Prix de base » and its per-lodging prices). `outsideOffer.quote` and the public session check price through the engine's standalone-sale quote, with the real date.
