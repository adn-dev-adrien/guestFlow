- **Console — « Réessayer » on a failed closing mail never sends a dead link** (audit R9 N1, specs `hosting-h1-fleet-runtime.md` rule 40 and `control-plane-plans-and-access.md` rule 45). The retry was offered on an archived customer and mailed the latest export link without looking at it: during an erasure it sent a link the erasure was about to kill, and after the 30 days it sent one already answering `410`, while the step read « Envoyé ». The retry is now refused with « Effacement en cours. » while an erasure is queued or running, runs inside the customer's lock, produces a fresh export when the link has expired or lost its archive, and fails with its reason when there is no database left. A retry queued behind the customer's lock does nothing when « Réactiver » or another tab's retry ran first, so an active customer never gets a fresh export nor a « a été fermé » mail. The mail writes the link's real last day (« jusqu’au JJ/MM/AAAA inclus ») instead of « pendant 30 jours ». +11 console tests.
