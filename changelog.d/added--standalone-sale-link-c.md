- **Sale outside a stay — payment link** (`specs/outside-booking-sas.md` rules 39 to 42, `specs/payment-link-lifecycle.md` rule 68). The remainder of a sale outside a stay can be paid through a Qonto link: amount computed by the server, valid 36 hours, a single open link per sale (reused or replaced), withdrawn with the sale. A link paid afterwards settles the sale in full; the excess is a refund owed, with an alert to the administrator. The link's e-mail is sent only on a click, never on its own.
