- An unpaid card order does not exist: it reserves nothing (no offered unit, no slot, no stock, no headcount), the team does not see it and is not notified until it is paid, and the guest can place another order in the meantime. On payment, it is sold at the current price; if the sale is no longer possible (slot taken, offered unit consumed by another order, price gone up), the money stays owed to the guest in the refunds register.
