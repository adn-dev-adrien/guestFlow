- **A converted devis keeps the deadline it promised** (specs `deposit-blocks-the-dates.md` rules 21 to 23, `payment-schedule-and-cancellation.md` rule 57; audits R34-RES-L1, R34-RES-L2). A direct devis with no acompte owes one payment on its validity date; once converted, the stay lost that deadline at its first priced save when the stay was more than `30 + validity` days out (start − 30 replaced it, with no message), and deleting the converted devis did the same for any stay (an acompte the guest was never asked for appeared). The stay now keeps the promised deadline for the dates quoted, and a converted devis cannot be deleted while its stay exists: « Ce devis porte l'échéance de la réservation qui en est issue : il ne peut pas être supprimé. ». The devis of a cancelled stay holds no deadline any more and can be deleted as before. Decision D27: rows stored before the fix keep the promised deadline too, so a converted stay already overdue stays overdue.
