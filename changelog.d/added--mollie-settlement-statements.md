- **Mollie settlement statement joined to its Qonto credit** (spec `mollie-settlement-receipts.md`, 2026-11-17). With Qonto connected and a Mollie token saved, a new setting « Joindre le relevé de chaque versement Mollie dans Qonto » (`mollieStatementsSince`, empty = off) makes GuestFlow write, for each Mollie credit settled on or after that date, a PDF « Relevé de versement Mollie » built from Mollie's own balance transactions (one line per payment or released reserve with its guest and reservation, gross, reserve held, fees, net) and attach it to the credit in Qonto. It is uploaded only when the lines add up to the credit to the cent, once (idempotent), hourly, 20 per run, retried up to 24 times; a Qonto connection that predates `attachment.write` asks to be reconnected. New endpoints `GET /api/payments/reconciliation/mollie-statements/status`, `GET …/mollie-statements/:transactionId.pdf` and `POST …/mollie-statements/:transactionId/retry`; the reconciliation line of a Mollie credit shows the statement state, « Voir le relevé » and « Réessayer ». Plugin `online-payment` 1.9.0.
