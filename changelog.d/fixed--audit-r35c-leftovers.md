- **Cancellation after a full refund of a solde paid with several methods books no phantom indemnity** (specs/mixed-method-payments.md rule 57): the cancellation reads the solde at its book share, less its geste, so its caisse-interne money and its geste no longer turn into a 7588 « acompte conservé », and the caisse lines still offer the caisse money afterwards.
- **Accounting export: a stay paid with one method and refunded in full also leaves every account at 0** (specs/mixed-method-payments.md rule 58): the closing refund applies to every stay from the month after the plugin version is installed (`stayClosingFrom`, stored once like `ttcSplitFrom`), so months already exported keep their cents. accounting-export 1.2.2.
- **A book refund dated before the stay's last book payment is refused** (specs/reservation-refunds.md rule 53): 400 `REFUND_BEFORE_PAYMENT` « Date antérieure au dernier paiement (JJ/MM/AAAA). », shown in the refund dialog; caisse-interne refunds and payments are not judged.
