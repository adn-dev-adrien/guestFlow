- **Articles suggested at the arrival** (`specs/outside-booking-sas.md` rules 35 to 38). A list of suggested articles per sales context (an hourly resource, a lodging), 12 at most, in the order the operator chooses and filtered by client (outside a stay: articles « vendables hors séjour » only). The options and resources catalogue gains the fields « Vendable hors séjour », VAT class and outside customer's rate, validated by the server. SDK 2.31.0: `ctx.suggestionContexts`.
