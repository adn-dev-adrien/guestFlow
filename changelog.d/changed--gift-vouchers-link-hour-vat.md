- **Gift vouchers — the payment link lives one hour, and the VAT is configurable.** The online payment link of a voucher now lives 60 minutes (it was 30): the buyer who comes back before the end gets the same link, and past the hour the link is lapsed, deactivated at the provider by the payment poll, and the voucher page says « Le lien de paiement a expiré. Aucun paiement n'a été pris. » with « Recommencer ». A payment made in time but confirmed by the bank after the withdrawal still issues the voucher; an order never paid is kept 7 days, invisible, then purged. A new setting « TVA des bons cadeaux » (10 % by default, 0 to 100, two decimals at most) is frozen on each voucher when it is sold; the receipt e-mail gains the `{vat}` variable (« dont TVA 10 % : 13,64 € »), the operator's detail shows HT and VAT, and each voucher sale enters the accounting export as HT + VAT at its rate. Generic core: `core.payments.saleLink` takes an optional `validityMinutes` (1 to 1440; a sale that states it holds the end as a hard bound) and `refreshBasketLink` lapses a sale link read past its end. A stay paid with a voucher books only the collected part, so the voucher's VAT is never counted twice (`specs/mixed-method-payments.md` rule 64, to be confirmed by the accountant).
