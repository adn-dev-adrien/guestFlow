- **Paiement en ligne (Qonto) leaves the core and becomes the first payment provider.** The core keeps what a payment means (the amount asked, the link record, the request email, the paid bucket, the devis conversion) and talks to a provider through one interface; Qonto's connection, webhook, settings page and poll live in the plugin, at the same URLs. Without a connected provider, the fiche shows no payment request button and the dashboard no « Relancer » (`specs/plugins-phase-3a-online-payment.md`).
- **« Adresse du site public » moves to Paramètres › Conditions générales**: it builds the CGV link of the emails and outlives the payment plugin.
