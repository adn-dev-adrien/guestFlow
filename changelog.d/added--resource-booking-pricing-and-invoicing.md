- Hourly resources (plugin): a booking outside a stay shows its price line by line (day, evening, offered by the property, offered time, VAT), offers time by steps of 15 minutes, and is paid at booking or at a one-screen arrival SAS with the fiches' payment lines (cash to the caisse interne by default). Paid on the books, it gets a Qonto invoice joined to the day's on-site card batch, and enters the accounting export with the invoice number as « Pièce ». Setting « TVA des ventes hors séjour », 20 % by default. See `specs/resource-booking-pricing-and-invoicing.md`.
