- **A sale on a stay no longer recalculates the nights** (`specs/pricing-components.md` rules 8 and 9). Adding, offering, removing, discounting or moving an extra from the fiche, the SAS or the stay app reads the nights as they were sold, without reading the tariff again, and no longer refuses the sale because the stay no longer meets a minimum or maximum length or an arrival day changed since then. Editing the stay itself keeps these checks. No amount changes.
