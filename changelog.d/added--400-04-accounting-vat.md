- **« Sans TVA »: a dated VAT regime**: Paramètres › TVA & exercice › « Changer de régime… » switches an installation to or from « Sans TVA » (mention art. 293 B or art. 261 D 4°) from a date, with the history of regimes kept; the legal mention replaces VAT lines and prices already paid do not move. A preview announces the effect; confirming **recalculates and stores the proportional tourist tax of the stays concerned** (frozen or declared tax, installment plans and stays the preview lists as keeping their tax excepted) and abandons open payment links whose amount changes; a change touching collected money or accounting months always asks confirmation; a future-dated change can be cancelled until its date, unless a stay it re-priced has since been paid.
- **Franchise threshold**: under « Sans TVA » with the art. 293 B mention, a « Seuil de franchise (€) » field (empty: GuestFlow ships no legal amount) feeds a card in Suivi financier with the turnover collected during the year, its projection to 31 December and a gauge, and a « Seuil de franchise » line in « À traiter » from 80 % of the threshold, or as soon as the projection goes beyond it.
- **Accounting synthesis** (plugin `accounting-export`): for a month, a quarter, the exercise or a custom window, the revenue, collected, tourist tax and VAT to declare by rate, with the last-year comparison.
