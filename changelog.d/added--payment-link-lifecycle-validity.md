- **Payment links now expire** (spec `payment-link-lifecycle.md` rules 6-9, 13-24, 2026-10-30). Every link minted from this version is valid 36 hours from its creation (`expiresAt`, whatever Qonto answers), and a request reuses an open link only while it has 24 hours left — otherwise the old link lapses and a fresh one is minted. From 00:00 Paris on the arrival day, the acompte / solde / séjour / échéance links of the stay lapse too (a link minted that day keeps its 36 hours; complement links are untouched); links of a cancelled or missing stay are withdrawn. A lifecycle pass moves them out of `open` without a provider call, then deactivates each at the provider first and reads its payment after: a payment made before the deactivation is booked and confirmed like any other, and one last look an hour later catches a payment completed after that read. It runs at the start of every poll pass and from the new online-payment job `payment-link-lifecycle` every 15 minutes (online-payment 1.5.0, SDK 2.18.0: `paymentPollRunner.runLinkLifecycle`). A refused deactivation still reads the payment (a provider refuses to deactivate a link already paid), so a payment whose webhook was lost is booked — at once when it surfaces on a new request, which is then refused; a lapsed link whose deactivation keeps failing alerts the admin once after 24 hours. The ledger row says « valable jusqu'au … à … », « Lien expiré le … à … », « Lien expiré · séjour commencé », and « · désactivation en attente chez Qonto ». A paid link no longer keeps an owed-deactivation marker, which could block the plugin's uninstall. +74 server tests.
