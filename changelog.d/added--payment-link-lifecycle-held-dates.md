- **Paid dates are held, and a request whose dates were taken loses its link** (spec `payment-link-lifecycle.md` rules 10-12, 31-33, 37-39, 46, 48, 2026-10-31). A reservation holds its nights once it carries money (an acompte paid is enough, the solde open or not; a cash payment, a paid installment or a paid link count) or when it comes from a platform; a devis never holds them, an unpaid direct reservation neither, and only a cancellation frees them. As soon as such a stay covers a night of an unpaid devis or reservation — a platform booking imported by iCal or moved by its feed, the first of two devis paid, an acompte recorded, a stay created or re-dated by hand, « Rétablir », an approved iCal date change — that request's payment links are withdrawn (« Lien retiré · dates prises »), read at the provider then deactivated, and no new link is created on it (409 « Dates déjà prises par une autre réservation : aucun lien créé. »). Two unpaid requests on the same nights both keep their links: the first paid wins. A link paid in flight is still booked, in date conflict, without the guest confirmation, and the admin is told « Paiement reçu sur des dates déjà prises — … Action requise : déplacer le séjour ou le rembourser. ». A platform booking that lands on held nights is recorded and called an over-booking (« Surbooking : Booking sur des dates réservées », naming the stay that holds them), and approving an iCal date change onto them asks first, with the stays to open. A devis cannot be created or moved onto held nights (409 « Dates déjà réservées : devis non enregistré. », with the stays listed in the dialog; the devis fiche no longer refuses a save on the nights of an unpaid reservation by itself); a devis or an unpaid reservation whose nights were taken since stays as it is, marked « Dates prises » in its action bar and in the devis list. A duplicate set aside withdraws its links too. No e-mail is sent to the guest by this change. New generic component `DatesTakenBadge`. +44 server tests, +12 client tests.
