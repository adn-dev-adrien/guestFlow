- **Gift vouchers — online purchase** (`specs/gift-vouchers.md` rules 12-23). The website sells a voucher: the order is validated again by the server (amount bounds, texts, e-mail, terms version recorded with the date and a visitor hash, 5 orders per visitor per hour, proof of work), paid through a payment link with no reservation, and the voucher is issued only when the bank confirms the link is paid — once, whatever the replays. The buyer reads and prints it from a personal link (24-byte token, stored hashed) and gets a receipt e-mail with the amount, the expiry and the link, never the code; a failed send is retried and flagged « Mail non parti ». Orders never paid are deleted after 7 days. Core, generic: `core.payments.saleLink` (SDK 2.33.0) mints the link of a plugin's basket with no stay and `ctx.paymentBasket` settles it. The gift-voucher receipt is the one guest e-mail exempt from `specs/no-automatic-email-without-approval.md`.
