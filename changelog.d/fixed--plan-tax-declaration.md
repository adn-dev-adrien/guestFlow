- **A stay paid through an installment plan now reaches « Suivi taxe de séjour »** (specs/tourist-tax-declaration-month-stay-end.md rules 7-8, D14): the page waited for `balancePaid`, which paying an installment never sets, so such a stay was on no month and its tax was never offered for declaration. A stay with a plan is now listed at the month of its last night, for its whole tax, whatever is paid, so a tax not yet collected can be declared. Other stays keep their payment gate.
