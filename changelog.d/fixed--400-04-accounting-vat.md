- **Accounting export — money already collected never moves**: a tourist tax that rises after the solde no longer rewrites the exported solde entry; the arrival complement created by a tax increase books on 46710000 in its own month; a collected installment books its stored split; a stay paid then reduced is booked pro-rata instead of putting a negative credit on 46710000; re-pricing a paid stay (free-text line, « Utiliser les tarifs actuels », change of logement) no longer moves booked entries. **The VAT of some entries changes** (e.g. an October 2026 stay 265,38 → 82,47 €, a September 2026 stay 37,13 → 19,47 €): **a month already imported by the accountant needs a corrective entry on their side.** **An acompte no longer vanishes from its month's export** when the solde of an older stay is recorded. A stay paid with several methods and refunded in full leaves every account at 0; one paid with a single method does too from the month after 4.0.0 is installed (fallback 1 Dec 2026; months already exported keep their cents); the TTC-share split also applies from the month after 4.0.0 is installed (fallback 1 Nov 2026).
- **Added guests**: the added-guests supplement is accommodation revenue from its first entry and its tax is out of pre-arrival money (46710000 no longer books it twice); it is declared with the right amount; a tourist-tax plan pins the tax it collected so a rate move no longer asks more; « Taxe de séjour en complément » cannot be set over a collected tax (409 `TOURIST_TAX_ALREADY_COLLECTED`); an added tax grown after its tick gets its own row, « Complément de taxe de séjour ».
- **Tourist tax**: a stay paid through an installment plan reaches « Suivi taxe de séjour » at the month of its last night; « Déclarée » refuses a tick (409) unless the stay is paid and its end date is past (unticking stays possible); no year or month past the current one can be picked.
- A trop-perçu refunded after a paid stay is reduced is counted once (journal, « Encaissé », « Total perçu ») and the tourist-tax account never goes negative.
