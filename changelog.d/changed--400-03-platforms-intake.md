- **Booking, Airbnb and Gîtes de France intakes join the plugin catalogue** and run on the full plugin engine (master v3.10.0 to v3.21.0 merged, including the Airbnb checkout-chores rules, the platform cleaning fee and the tourist tax of guests added on a reversing platform). On a « Sans corvées de départ » platform the SAS stops reminding dishes and bins and unfinished cleaning is no longer billed at departure.
- **Paramètres › Plateformes**: « E-mails aux voyageurs » per platform (relay address — no season e-mail goes to a relay address —, where the review is asked) and « Compté comme vente directe ». The guest e-mail sequence no longer recognises Airbnb, Booking or Gîtes de France by name. Platform names `BookingCom`, « Booking.com » or `gites-de-france` merge into the existing platform and source instead of creating duplicates; a new platform appears in Paramètres at once.
- **Public booking form — a tightening**: first and last names accept only letters of any script with marks, space, hyphen, both apostrophes and dot; any other character is refused 422 `nameInvalid` (website-booking 1.8.10). The back office, the intakes and stored clients are not judged.
- **Neat insurance** leaves the core and becomes the `neat` plugin; without an active Neat, cancellation insurance is offered nowhere (existing lines stay read-only).
