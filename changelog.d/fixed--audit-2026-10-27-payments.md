- A full payment link (admin, website and the e-mail announcing it) now charges exactly what its payment records: the acompte plus the solde, tourist tax included and arrival-complement lines excluded, with the tax on its own 0 % VAT line. It used to charge `finalPrice` (tax left out, complement lines kept in) while ticking the whole solde paid (specs/deposit-blocks-the-dates.md rule 18, specs/payment-links-vat.md rule 10).
- Stays paid through an installment plan count at their full value in Finance (revenue, per property and channel, booking pace, projection): the plan's collected and still-due totals join the « total de séjour », and « Encaissé » lists one row per collected installment (specs/direct-installments.md rule 45).
- The « Reste » of a stay (`remainingDue`) is the unpaid buckets, no longer `finalPrice − paid buckets` (specs/finance-operational-remaining-to-pay.md rule 7).
- Cancelling a stay paid through an installment plan books the requalification of what was collected: an avoir (stay at the sales VAT rate, tourist-tax shares at 0 %) and a VAT-free indemnity (specs/direct-installments.md rule 46).
- plugin:online-payment The bank reconciliation no longer inflates a platform's commission by the acompte the platform took: the solde credit is expected and written as the solde, the fiche payout being credit + acompte net (specs/qonto-bank-reconciliation.md rule 44).
- plugin:online-payment The installments are read through the core installments model (SDK 2.6.0); a failing read surfaces instead of expecting nothing; the manifest changelog is in version order (rule 45).
