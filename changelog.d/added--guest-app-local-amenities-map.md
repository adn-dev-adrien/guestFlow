- Guest app: new « Pratique » tile (« Essentials ») opening a map of the bakeries, pharmacy, butchers, grocery shops, tobacconist, post office, cash machine, fuel stations and waste points around the estate (Satillieu, Saint-Romain-d'Ay, Saint-Alban-d'Ay). Chips filter by category, a list view sits next to the map, each place opens a sheet with its hours (open / closed computed by the server at Paris time), its drive time from the estate, what can be dropped or which fuels are sold, and « Itinéraire » to Plans (Apple devices), Google Maps or Waze, remembered per device. « Me localiser » shows the phone's position without sending it anywhere. The places list is cached by the service worker, so the list and sheets work offline; only the OpenStreetMap tiles need the network (`img-src` of the guest app now allows `tile.openstreetmap.org`). Booklet buttons can target the page, on a given category (`{ kind: 'places', category? }`). Studio: a « Pratique » page to enter, publish and re-verify (12 months) the places, with the estate position as a setting. New table `guest_app_places` and setting `estatePosition`; guest-app plugin 1.6.0, `leaflet` added to the guest app. Spec: `specs/guest-app/guest-app-local-amenities.md`.
