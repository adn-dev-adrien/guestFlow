- **The VAT rate is the rate of the regime period in force**: changing it in Paramètres › TVA & exercice recalculates that period's reservations, not earlier ones; with a single period nothing moves, to the cent.
- **Plan comptable, one « Comptes » card**: the default commission account and the cancellation-indemnity account join the other accounts.
- **A payment on a stay whose amounts drifted** is split on its stored amounts in the export (also « Encaisser en une fois » and the arrival payment), never booked whole on the tourist-tax account; the Comptabilité page warns « À vérifier avant l'envoi au cabinet ». The tourist tax of guests added on a reversing platform is booked with the complement.
- **Tourist tax**: a stay whose tax is frozen at 0 € no longer appears « à déclarer »; « Déclarée » refuses a stay whose platform remits the tax itself. The tax report refuses a repeated query key or a year before 1000 or 9999; an unknown `fiscalYear` is refused `UNKNOWN_EXERCISE`.
