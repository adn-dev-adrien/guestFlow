- **Assurance annulation Neat leaves the core and becomes the `neat` plugin.** The core keeps the quote and its engine; a plugin may set the insurance unit price through a small, closed interface (the quote post-processor). Neat's pricing, subscription pass, settings, tables, Intégrations card and the chip of the insurance line live in the plugin, at the same URLs (`specs/plugins-phase-3b-neat.md`).
- **Without an active Neat, the cancellation insurance is offered nowhere**: no tile on the fiche or the devis, no block on the website, no line in Paramètres › Options. A line already on a stay or a devis stays visible at its price, read-only. Reactivating Neat brings the option back with its settings.
- Uninstalling Neat with « Effacer aussi ses données » first warns about the subscriptions still active at Neat, which stay in force there.
