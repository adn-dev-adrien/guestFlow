- Payments: the end-of-stay complement can be collected only once the check-out is done (the departure SAS committed, or the stay's last day reached without the SAS plugin). Before, « Marquer perçu », the merged card of a complement deferred to the departure, « Encaisser en une fois », a bank reconciliation and « Tout solder » could mark it during the stay or before arrival. They now answer « Complément de fin de séjour encaissable après le départ. » (409 `END_OF_STAY_BEFORE_CHECKOUT`); the fiche shows the mark closed with « Après le départ. » and « Tout solder » leaves it out. The departure SAS settles it as before; notes during the stay are still collected by card as they come. Stored payments are not changed.
