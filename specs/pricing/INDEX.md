# Pricing — nightly price engine, seasons, tariff recipes, closures, school holidays

Start with: `pricing-components` (unit pricing components and sale lines, source of every total). See also: `devis/`, `tourist-tax/`, `accounting/`.

- `pricing-components` — unit pricing components (nights, extra guests, tourist tax, articles, totals, VAT, stay clock, settlement, refunds), sale lines and one extraSale service for fiche, shop and SAS; price non-regression gate. Code: `server/src/utils/pricing/`, `server/src/models/ports/`. **[In progress]**
- `pricing-engine-thin-client` — the server computes the whole quote (`/reservations/calculate-price`); the client renders it, with manual-price override and offered lines. Code: `server/src/utils/pricing.js`, `client/src/components/PricingSummary.jsx`.
- `pricing-min-nights-per-range` — minimum nights resolved per season date range; the seasons calendar paints and splits ranges. Code: `client/src/pages/PropertyPricingSeasonsPage.jsx`, `server/src/models/propertiesModel.js`.
- `timed-options-proportional-pricing` — early check-in / late check-out surcharge always follows the current time, even on a locked saved reservation. Code: `server/src/utils/pricing/`, `server/src/utils/devisHelpers.js`.
- `tariff-recipes` — declarative recipe documents (seasons, calendar language, prices, discount curves, min stay, changeover) previewed and applied transactionally per property; horizon extended daily; Aventura Lodge recipe, extra-guest per night, whole-euro channel grid. Code: `server/src/plugins/tariff-recipes/`, `server/src/utils/changeover.js`, `client/src/plugins/tariff-recipes/`.
- `tariff-events-and-extra-guest-tiers` — recipes may declare per-night extra-guest tier tables (no ratio stacking) and named recurring events with per-year declared dates; missing years flagged on card and Dashboard. Code: `server/src/plugins/tariff-recipes/seasonPlan.js`, `server/src/utils/extraGuestTiers.js`, `client/src/plugins/tariff-recipes/TariffRecipeCard.jsx`.
- `tariff-recipe-gite` — the Gîte's whole tariff as recipe `gite-2027`: net floor pivot, week-based degressivity, six-rule calendar, festive premium seasons, 2-night minimum, no changeover. Code: `server/src/plugins/tariff-recipes/recipes/gite-2027.json`, `server/src/plugins/tariff-recipes/tests/gite-recipe-end-to-end.unit.test.js`.
- `tariff-recipe-platform-cleaning-fee` — a recipe's `cleaning` block stores per-channel net-to-recover cleaning fees; platform grid shows the grossed-up «Ménage / séjour» column; unknown channels skipped. Code: `server/src/plugins/tariff-recipes/platformCleaningFee.js`, `client/src/components/PlatformPriceCard.jsx`.
- `tariff-change-journal` — dated journal of tariff changes (recipe applications and manual entries) to measure their effect on bookings. Code: `server/src/models/tariffChangeJournalModel.js`, `server/src/plugins/tariff-recipes/controller.js`, `client/src/plugins/tariff-recipes/TariffChangeJournal.jsx`.
- `establishment-closures` — global or per-property closures blocking bookings; server rejects overlaps and conflicts with existing reservations. Code: `server/src/models/establishmentClosuresModel.js`, `server/src/utils/closureConflicts.js`, `client/src/pages/EstablishmentClosuresPage.jsx`.
- `school-holidays` — school-holiday periods for zones A/B/C synced from data.education.gouv.fr, editable (edits lock them), shown on Gantt timeline and calendars. Code: `server/src/plugins/school-holidays/`, `client/src/plugins/school-holidays/`.
- `booking-pace` — on the finance dashboard, bookings today versus the same date last year for the next twelve months. Code: `server/src/models/bookingPaceModel.js`, `server/src/utils/bookingPace.js`.
