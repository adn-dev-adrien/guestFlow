# Tourist tax — calculation base, platform handling, freeze, declaration and report

Start with: `tourist-tax-matches-the-office-calculation` (current base, freeze and declaration arithmetic). See also: accounting, pricing, platforms, reservations.

- `per-platform-tourist-tax-three-way` — global per-platform mode on `platforms`: platform remits (offered), platform reverses to us (charged in the balance, we remit), owner collects at arrival; drives quote, Suivi and compta. Code: `server/src/models/pricingInputModel.js`, `server/src/utils/pricing/touristTax.js`.
- `tourist-tax-base-accommodation-only` — percentage-mode tax base is the accommodation actually charged; adding, offering or re-routing an extra never moves it. Code: `server/src/utils/pricing/steps/touristTax.js`.
- `tourist-tax-included-services-deduction` — the tax is computed on the « dry night »: accommodation minus the fixed reference value of services included in the rate, over nights. Code: `server/src/utils/pricing/touristTax.js`, `server/src/utils/pricing/steps/touristTax.js`.
- `tourist-tax-matches-the-office-calculation` — amount equals the office of tourism's form to the cent; tax freezes on collection or « Déclarée », frozen base kept, « Recalculer » thaws it. Code: `server/src/utils/touristTaxFreeze.js`, `server/src/utils/pricing/touristTax.js`.
- `tourist-tax-freeze-past-with-refresh` — refresh button next to the tax line recomputes a frozen tax; saving persists it. Freeze trigger now defined by the office-calculation spec. Code: `client/src/components/PricingSummary.jsx`, `server/src/utils/reservationQuoteContext.js`.
- `tourist-tax-on-solde` — with an acompte, the whole tax rides the solde and the acompte covers the accommodation only. Code: `server/src/utils/pricing/schedule.js`, `server/src/utils/accounting/legacyBucketSplit.js`.
- `platform-payment-tourist-tax-as-option` — on a platform that reverses the tax, the entered brut includes it: the accommodation back-solve subtracts it, so no phantom écart. Code: `server/src/utils/pricing/steps/touristTax.js`.
- `platform-offered-tax-passthrough-and-cascade` — offered-tax platform stay: tax never revenue, no compta change, fiche bottom block as a running-subtotal cascade ending on what the operator earns. Code: `client/src/components/PricingSummary.jsx`.
- `platform-brut-excludes-offered-tourist-tax` — for a platform that remits the tax itself, the entered brut is the platform's own tax-excluded figure; full amount is revenue with full commission. Code: `server/src/utils/pricing/steps/touristTax.js`, `client/src/components/reservation/PlatformStatementEditor.jsx`.
- `platform-tourist-tax-out-of-the-commission` — operator types what the guest paid and the tax withheld; commission, revenue and VAT base follow, tax shown struck through on the summary. Code: `server/src/utils/pricing/steps/touristTax.js`, `client/src/utils/platformTouristTaxPrefill.js`.
- `platform-added-guests-tourist-tax` — tax of guests added after a platform booking is collected in the arrival complement on its own line and declared to the commune. Code: `server/src/utils/pricing/steps/touristTax.js`, `server/src/plugins/accounting-export/accountingModel.js`.
- `tourist-tax-declaration-month-stay-end` — a stay is declared in the month of its last night, or of the payment when later; unpaid stays never appear; plans enter at stay month. Code: `server/src/models/financeModel.js`, `server/src/utils/touristTaxReport.js`.
- `tourist-tax-declared-checkbox` — « Déclarée » tick per row stores when it was declared; a new tick waits for a stay paid and over; platform-remitted stays refused. Code: `client/src/pages/TouristTaxPage.jsx`, `server/src/controllers/financeController.js`.
- `tourist-tax-report-v2` — Suivi taxe de séjour: any period, exempt persons, platform share, per-night register, per-logement cards and « Marquer déclaré », CSV, bottom dock. Code: `server/src/controllers/touristTaxReportController.js`, `server/src/utils/touristTaxReport.js`, `client/src/pages/TouristTaxPage.jsx`.
