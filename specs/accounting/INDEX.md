# Accounting — journal export, VAT, bank reconciliation and the Suivi financier

Start with: `accountant-accounting-export` (journal-entry engine every other accounting spec amends). See also: tourist-tax, payments, platforms, pricing, settings.

- `accountant-accounting-export` — accountant login, read-only Comptabilité page, monthly balanced double-entry CSV driven by real payment dates; exported months freeze. Code: `server/src/plugins/accounting-export/accountingExport.js`, `server/src/plugins/accounting-export/accountingModel.js`, `client/src/plugins/accounting-export/AccountingPage.jsx`.
- `accounting-platform-commission-and-no-deposit` — platform turnover booked on the gross, commission as real debit lines on per-platform 6226xx accounts; no acompte unless the platform takes one. Code: `server/src/plugins/accounting-export/platformAccountsModel.js`, `server/src/plugins/accounting-export/accountingExport.js`.
- `accounting-encaissement-effective-percent` — each encaissement shows the money that moved: CA = amount paid, net = CA minus commission, credit lines split over the postes that encaissement collects. Code: `server/src/utils/accounting/accountingOutput.js`, `server/src/plugins/accounting-export/accountingModel.js`.
- `accounting-books-the-money-collected` — entries book exactly the collected money, on the postes each encaissement covers; gross-up ratio only for the legacy « solde = net » shape. Code: `server/src/utils/accounting/accountingOutput.js`, `server/src/utils/accounting/legacyBucketSplit.js`.
- `accounting-synthesis` — period screen: VAT to declare by rate and account, money collected, tourist tax, stay revenue, each against last year, plus consistency flags. Code: `server/src/plugins/accounting-export/synthesisModel.js`, `client/src/plugins/accounting-export/AccountingPage.jsx`.
- `single-vat-rate` — one editable global VAT rate (default 10 %) for every revenue stream, tourist tax untouched; the rate now belongs to a VAT period. Code: `server/src/utils/pricing.js`, `server/src/database.js`.
- `vat-optional-mode` — VAT regimes by period (subject or not subject, legal mention, no VAT on screens, documents, links and accounting), dated switch with history, franchise threshold tracking. Code: `server/src/models/vatRegimeModel.js`, `server/src/utils/vatRegime.js`, `client/src/pages/settings/VatFiscalSettingsPage.jsx`.
- `fiscal-year-and-nights-sold` — closing month setting, exercise selector (`exercice` URL param), accounting attribution date for every money window, nights sold per property. Code: `server/src/utils/fiscalYear.js`, `server/src/utils/reservationSettlement.js`.
- `finance-overview-rework` — money definitions still live: « total de séjour » per reservation, settled definition, element-by-element HT; its page layout is superseded by the dashboard. Code: `server/src/models/financeModel.js`, `server/src/utils/reservationSettlement.js`.
- `finance-operational-remaining-to-pay` — « reste à payer » is the sum of still-owed buckets only (acompte, solde, complément, fin de séjour); `remainingDue` equals it. Code: `server/src/utils/reservationSettlement.js`, `client/src/components/OperationalPaymentsTable.jsx`.
- `finance-pending-global-remaining` — `getOperational().pending`: finished unsettled stays, period-free, counted at the restant dû. Code: `server/src/models/financeModel.js`.
- `finance-per-property-revenue-chart` — server aggregates `revenueByProperty` / `yearToDateByProperty`: zero-seeded from properties, TTC and HT, revenue-descending; the chart itself is gone. Code: `server/src/models/financeModel.js`.
- `finance-dashboard-redesign` — the Suivi financier page: exercise/month/custom windows, hero, tiles with detail panels, charts, projection, composed server-side from financeModel. Code: `server/src/models/financeDashboardModel.js`, `client/src/pages/FinancePage.jsx`, `client/src/components/FinanceHero.jsx`.
- `finance-revpar-year-over-year` — every headline figure (revenue, collected, stays, nights, occupancy, per-night revenue, RevPAR) compared with the same window a year earlier, ready from the server. Code: `server/src/utils/financeComparison.js`, `server/src/utils/financeInsights.js`.
- `finance-data-checks` — « À vérifier » panel of the exercise: missing tourist tax, undeclared months, missing platform payment, missing guest name, each opening its record. Code: `server/src/utils/financeDataChecks.js`, `server/src/controllers/financeDataChecksController.js`, `client/src/components/FinanceDataChecksCard.jsx`.
- `qonto-bank-reconciliation` — per reservation, whether each expected or recorded payment reached the Qonto account, with amount and date, and a short list of gaps. Code: `server/src/plugins/online-payment/reconciliation/reconciliationModel.js`, `client/src/plugins/online-payment/ReconciliationPage.jsx`.
- `reconciliation-assisted-matching` — certain matches self-confirm, others confirmed in batch or picked from candidates (one or several transfers, groups of stays); platform penalties declared once. Code: `server/src/plugins/online-payment/reconciliation/autoConfirm.js`, `server/src/plugins/online-payment/reconciliation/matcher.js`.
- `mollie-settlement-receipts` — every Mollie credit on Qonto gets an automatic PDF statement built from Mollie's data, one line per payment or released reserve. Code: `server/src/plugins/online-payment/reconciliation/mollieStatementsModel.js`, `server/src/plugins/online-payment/reconciliation/mollieStatementPdf.js`.
- `legacy-net-solde-schedule-repair` — boot-time repair of « solde = net » reservations so the fiche and the books agree on what a stay collected. Code: `server/src/utils/legacyNetSoldeRepair.js`, `server/src/database.js`.
- `stay-profile` — finance page « Profil des séjours »: stays split by channel, length, lead time and price band, plus average basket per month versus last year. Code: `server/src/utils/stayProfile.js`, `client/src/pages/StayProfilePage.jsx`.
