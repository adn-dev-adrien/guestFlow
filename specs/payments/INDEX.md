# Payments — how a stay is paid, collected, refunded and cancelled for non-payment

Start with: `reservation-payment-ledger` (the status ledger every payment rule reads from). See also: `accounting/`, `platforms/`, `sas/`, `pricing/`, `settings/`.

- `reservation-payment-ledger` — payments as a status ledger: one row per due (acompte, solde, complement, caution, refunds), server-shaped, with amount editors and refusals. Code: `server/src/utils/reservationLedger.js`, `client/src/components/LedgerList.jsx`.
- `payment-schedule-and-cancellation` — direct-stay schedule (acompte at booking, solde at J-30), dashboard payment alerts, operator-sent money e-mails only, one-click cancellation for non-payment, delete guards. Code: `server/src/utils/paymentSchedule.js`, `server/src/controllers/reservationCancellationController.js`.
- `online-payments-qonto` — Qonto OAuth payment links for acompte, solde or full payment, paid detection by polling, confirmation e-mail, last-minute full payment. Automatic abandonment/dunning never built. Code: `server/src/utils/qonto/qontoClient.js`, `server/src/utils/paymentRequestService.js`, `server/src/controllers/paymentsController.js`.
- `payment-link-lifecycle` — no link outlives its purpose: validity, retirement reasons, remote deactivation, orphan handling, paid-after-retired booking, resend/withdraw actions. Code: `server/src/utils/paymentLinkLifecycle.js`, `server/src/models/paymentLinksModel.js`.
- `payment-polling-fair-use` — poll cadence decaying with link age, expiry retirement, rate-limit abort, idempotency keys, webhook acceptance of expired links. Code: `server/src/utils/paymentPollRunner.js`, `server/src/plugins/online-payment/paymentPollSchedule.js`.
- `payment-links-vat` — Qonto link items basket split by VAT rate, exact to the cent, with money guard on every link-creation flow. Code: `server/src/utils/paymentLinkItems.js`.
- `qonto-payment-link-reference` — every link's stay line carries property, reservation number and guest name in title and description, amount untouched. Code: `server/src/utils/paymentLinkLabel.js`.
- `payment-link-quote-parity` — a payment link asks exactly the amount the devis shows, replayed from the same engine. Code: `server/src/utils/paymentRequestService.js`, `server/src/models/devisModel.js`.
- `property-deposit-switch` — the property « Acompte » switch (`depositEnabled`) governs the whole deposit, on every channel. Code: `server/src/utils/pricing.js`, `client/src/components/property/PropertyPaymentTab.jsx`.
- `disable-deposit-per-reservation` — per-reservation « Sans acompte » flag: deposit zero, balance absorbs all, locks and audit. **[Approved]** Code: `server/src/utils/pricing.js`, `client/src/components/reservation/FinanceSection.jsx`.
- `editable-deposit-amount` — operator may freeze the acompte at a manual amount (`depositAmountOverride`); solde absorbs changes; refused above accommodation. Code: `server/src/utils/pricing.js`, `client/src/components/reservation/FinanceSection.jsx`.
- `deposit-blocks-the-dates` — the acompte is what blocks dates: quote wording, full payment when stay is too close, open links re-stored on rule change. Code: `server/src/utils/pricing.js`, `server/src/models/devisModel.js`.
- `caution-live-from-property` — security deposit (caution) read live from the property setting, not copied onto the reservation. Code: `client/src/pages/PropertyDetail.jsx`, `client/src/components/PricingSummary.jsx`.
- `direct-installments` — installment plan for direct stays: per-installment links, receipts, resizing, cancellation and accounting of paid installments. Code: `server/src/controllers/installmentsController.js`, `server/src/models/installmentsModel.js`, `client/src/components/reservation/InstallmentPlanCard.jsx`.
- `mixed-method-payments` — one payment settled by several methods (settlements with lines, caisse share, refunds and accounting by share). Code: `server/src/models/paymentSettlementsModel.js`, `server/src/utils/paymentSettlementService.js`.
- `single-payment-at-check-in` — stay and complement settled in one gesture at check-in, grouped as one arrival payment. Code: `server/src/utils/arrivalPaymentGroup.js`, `server/src/plugins/sas/`.
- `single-payment-from-the-fiche` — record the single arrival payment from the reservation fiche (« Encaisser en une fois »). Code: `client/src/components/reservation/FinanceSection.jsx`, `server/src/utils/arrivalPaymentAdjustment.js`.
- `reservation-refunds` — returning money without touching the sale: refund bases, caps, overpayment, cancellation refunds, accounting entries. Code: `server/src/controllers/refundsController.js`, `server/src/utils/pricing/refunds/`.
- `cancellation-compensation` — platform-paid cancellation indemnity booked as a compensation entry (account 75xx) and in the accounting export. Code: `server/src/controllers/cancellationCompensationsController.js`, `server/src/models/cancellationCompensationsModel.js`.
- `cancellation-insurance` — guest-side cancellation insurance option priced per night (or % of stay), offered only while a live plugin prices it (see `plugins/` Neat). Code: `server/src/utils/insuranceOffer.js`, `server/src/utils/cancellationInsuranceSeed.js`.
- `mollie-rolling-reserve-and-incoming-payments` — Mollie card payments, rolling reserve, settlements and statement reconciliation with the bank. Code: `server/src/plugins/online-payment/reconciliation/mollieSettlements.js`, `server/src/plugins/online-payment/mollieClient.js`.
