# SAS — arrival/departure wizard and the complements it collects (check-in, stay, check-out)

Start with: `arrival-departure-sas` (the wizard itself, its pages, commit and audit). See also: `payments/`, `reservations/`, `accounting/`, `pricing/`, `plugins/`, `resources/`, `planning/`.

- `arrival-departure-sas` — guided arrival/departure wizard: pages, caution, options, linen, cleaning, single commit, history audit. The `sas` plugin owns routes; core commits. Code: `server/src/plugins/sas/controller.js`, `client/src/plugins/sas/ReservationSasDialog.jsx`.
- `complement-buckets-by-moment` — an amount shows under the heading of the moment it is collected (arrival, stay, end of stay), same in summary and Finance cards. Code: `server/src/utils/operationalCollection.js`, `client/src/components/reservation/FinanceSection.jsx`.
- `arrival-moment-is-the-check-in` — nothing counts as sold during the stay before the guest actually arrived (check-in or arrival time). Code: `server/src/controllers/reservationsController.js`, `client/src/constants/reservationLock.js`.
- `lock-at-arrival-time-and-arrival-complement-lines` — fiche locks only once the stay started (arrival time or SAS validated); arrival complement detail lists its lines. Code: `server/src/controllers/reservationsController.js`, `client/src/constants/reservationLock.js`.
- `reception-role-checkin-only` — "Accueil" role: finance-free home, Planning and SAS only, no financial reads. Code: `server/src/utils/receptionView.js`, `client/src/constants/receptionSasLock.js`.
- `reception-sas-today-only` — reception commits a SAS only on its day and only once; server refuses the rest, admins unaffected. Code: `server/src/plugins/sas/controller.js`, `client/src/constants/receptionSasLock.js`.
- `reopen-completed-sas` — green check reopens the wizard pre-filled; re-commit is idempotent (no duplicate lines, no doubled amount). Code: `server/src/plugins/sas/controller.js`, `client/src/plugins/sas/ReservationSasDialog.jsx`.
- `sas-hide-settled-steps` — caution page hidden once received, ménage page hidden when cleaning already included; reminder moves to recap. Code: `client/src/plugins/sas/ReservationSasDialog.jsx`, `server/src/plugins/sas/controller.js`.
- `sas-departure-mode-param` — SAS read carries `?mode=arrival|departure` so a ménage already sold is not re-asked at check-out. Code: `server/src/plugins/sas/controller.js`, `client/src/api.js`.
- `sas-arrival-total-server-side` — arrival recap lines, total and one-gesture-or-two decision come from the server preview, same code as the commit. Code: `server/src/plugins/sas/arrivalRecap.js`, `server/src/utils/arrivalSasPlan.js`.
- `sas-departure-recap-server-side` — check-out recap lines, totals and settlement ask computed by the server, same code as the commit. Code: `server/src/plugins/sas/departureRecap.js`, `server/src/utils/departureSasPlan.js`.
- `sas-recap-payment-buttons` — recap settles through single-select buttons: CB/Chèque, Payé en liquide (caisse interne), En fin de séjour (arrival only). Code: `client/src/plugins/sas/ReservationSasDialog.jsx`.
- `sas-offer-complement-lines` — operator offers any billable recap line (geste commercial): 0 €, real price struck through, one input. Code: `client/src/plugins/sas/OfferableLine.jsx`, `server/src/plugins/sas/controller.js`.
- `sas-upsells-activate-catalogue-option` — ménage and linge de toilette added in the SAS become the real catalogue option, still collected in the arrival complement. Code: `server/src/utils/sasOptionSale.js`, `server/src/utils/sasUpsellOptionMigration.js`.
- `sas-breakfast-and-catering-upsell` — sell breakfast (chosen mornings) and catering at end of check-in; becomes a real reservation line in the complement. Code: `client/src/plugins/sas/ReservationSasDialog.jsx`, `server/src/utils/sasOptionSale.js`.
- `sas-breakfast-and-handover-note` — breakfast counts, hour and note recorded in arrival SAS; free handover note resurfaces in departure SAS. Code: `client/src/plugins/sas/ReservationSasDialog.jsx`, `client/src/components/OptionDayCard.jsx`.
- `sas-breakfast-milk-and-food` — breakfast step adds milk, viennoiseries and cereal counts; time shown large; counts on planning breakfast card. Code: `client/src/plugins/sas/ReservationSasDialog.jsx`, `server/src/models/breakfastModel.js`.
- `sas-breakfast-bread-and-push` — breakfast defaults (viennoiseries = persons, bread half-baguettes) and a push before serving time opening the prep popup. Code: `server/src/utils/breakfastPushRunner.js`, `client/src/components/BreakfastPrepDialog.jsx`.
- `sas-breakfast-time-applies` — breakfast hour changed in the SAS applies to planning cards, push notice and reopening. Code: `server/src/models/breakfastModel.js`, `client/src/components/BreakfastPrepDialog.jsx`.
- `collect-stay-payment-at-check-in` — arrival SAS collects the stay's acompte and solde (direct channel only); the 2026-08-30 unified one-payment settlement is in `single-payment-at-check-in`. Code: `client/src/plugins/sas/SasStayPaymentPage.jsx`, `server/src/models/reservationsModel.js`.
- `arrival-payment-detail-and-adjustment` — the single arrival payment is itemised on the fiche; real amount handed over books a Réduction accordée or a Pourboire. Code: `server/src/utils/arrivalPaymentDetail.js`, `server/src/utils/arrivalPaymentAdjustment.js`.
- `defer-arrival-complement-to-checkout` — "En fin de séjour" merges arrival and end-of-stay complement, tourist tax included, into one check-out complement; sold cleaning never rebilled. Code: `server/src/plugins/sas/arrivalRecap.js`, `server/src/models/reservationsModel.js`.
- `adjustable-complement-amounts` — operator fixes each complement's amount by hand, even after collection; the gap is absorbed, books follow the adjusted amount. Code: `client/src/components/reservation/FinanceSection.jsx`, `server/src/models/reservationsModel.js`.
- `cash-complement-and-endofstay-finance` — end-of-stay complement behaves like arrival's; either can go to "caisse interne" (counted in finance, excluded from compta). Code: `server/src/plugins/accounting-export/accountingModel.js`, `client/src/components/reservation/FinanceSection.jsx`.
- `frozen-complement-trusts-client` — once the arrival complement is collected its amount is frozen server-side; browser values never change it. Code: `server/src/controllers/reservationsController.js`, `server/src/utils/frozenComplementRepair.js`.
- `force-item-to-complement` — per-item toggle routing an option/resource/line to the complement; per-bucket contribution snapshots keep accounting entries exact. Code: `client/src/components/reservation/ExtrasSection.jsx`, `server/src/utils/pricing.js`.
- `force-extras-complement-on-platform` — platform reservations default extras to the complement (revised by rule 1bis; reversible). Code: `client/src/components/reservation/ExtrasSection.jsx`, `client/src/components/PricingSummary.jsx`.
- `mid-stay-extras-to-end-of-stay-complement` — anything sold after the guest arrived is billed in the end-of-stay complement, line by line, collected at check-out. Code: `server/src/models/reservationsModel.js`, `server/src/plugins/sas/departureRecap.js`.
- `mid-stay-extras-on-locked-fiche` — any operator sells and corrects extras on a locked fiche until the end-of-stay complement is collected; arrival lines stay frozen. Code: `server/src/controllers/reservationsController.js`, `server/src/utils/paidLineJudgment.js`.
- `mid-stay-notes` — "Nouvelle note" groups stay sales, settled with one payment choice into the "Encaissements en séjour" block; server prices. Code: `server/src/utils/midStayNotePlan.js`, `client/src/components/reservation/MidStayNoteDialog.jsx`.
- `outside-booking-sas` — mini SAS for an outside client's session: extras, offered lines, several payments, amount left, all computed by the server. Code: `client/src/plugins/hourly-resources/OutsideBookingPage.jsx`, `server/src/plugins/hourly-resources/controller.js`.
- `recall-unpaid-arrival-complement-at-checkout` — arrival SAS « Complément encaissé »; departure SAS recalls an unsettled arrival complement and collects both, amounts kept separate. Code: `server/src/plugins/sas/controller.js`, `client/src/plugins/sas/ReservationSasDialog.jsx`.
