# Platforms — OTA channels (Airbnb, Booking, GdF…), platform money, iCal import/export

Start with: `normalize-platform-names` (canonical platform names every other spec relies on). See also: `settings/` (Paramètres › Plateformes), `pricing/`, `payments/`, `plugins/`, `accounting/`. Direct reservations are filed as `direct` or `Lodgify`: test them with `isDirectChannel()`, never `platform === 'direct'`.

- `normalize-platform-names` — one canonical UpperCamelCase platform name across `platforms`, `ical_sources` and `reservations`; boot migration merges duplicates; write sites normalise. Code: `server/src/utils/platformNameFormat.js`, `server/src/models/platformsModel.js`.
- `platforms-and-ical-rework` — per-property « Plateformes & iCal » list: every platform listed, optional iCal URL, global colour, per-property disable; platform editors now on Paramètres › Plateformes. Code: `server/src/models/propertyIcalModel.js`, `client/src/pages/PropertyDetail.jsx`, `client/src/components/PlatformColorPicker.jsx`.
- `ical-platforms-in-dropdowns` — `GET /api/platforms` returns built-ins plus DB platforms, deduped, `direct` first; both platform selects consume it. Code: `server/src/routes/platforms.js`, `server/src/models/platformsModel.js`.
- `platform-settings-relay-and-review` — per-platform guest-e-mail settings (relay address, review wording) so the core names no Solio platform. Code: `server/src/utils/platformMail.js`, `client/src/pages/settings/PlatformsSettingsPage.jsx`.
- `airbnb-no-checkout-chores` — platform setting « Sans corvées de départ »: the arrival SAS never offers a checkout chore on such a platform. Code: `server/src/controllers/platformsController.js`, `client/src/plugins/sas/ReservationSasDialog.jsx`.
- `ical-import-hardening` — import never shares a client it does not own, stopped calendars stay stopped, overlaps flagged, one slow platform costs one timeout, damaged feed changes nothing. Code: `server/src/models/propertyIcalModel.js`, `server/src/utils/icalParser.js`, `server/src/utils/icalSyncStatus.js`.
- `ical-sync-mapping-resilience` — empty or garbage feeds are refused; UID→reservation mapping can be rebuilt from stored UIDs. Code: `server/src/models/propertyIcalModel.js`, `server/src/utils/icalParser.js`.
- `ical-summary-fallback-cross-uid` — an unknown UID whose summary uniquely matches an existing mapping re-claims the reservation instead of duplicating it. Code: `server/src/models/propertyIcalModel.js`.
- `ical-sync-override-locked-dates` — date change on an `icalSyncLocked` reservation becomes a pending approval (Dashboard alert: approve, open, ignore); never applied silently. Code: `server/src/models/propertyIcalModel.js`, `client/src/components/IcalDateDriftAlert.jsx`.
- `ical-cancellation-approval` — a reservation vanishing from its feed is never auto-deleted: one pending cancellation approval with Dashboard alert and three actions. Code: `server/src/models/icalCancellationModel.js`, `client/src/components/IcalCancellationAlert.jsx`.
- `ical-import-empty-notes` — iCal import never writes the reservation or client `notes`; re-sync never overwrites them. Code: `server/src/models/propertyIcalModel.js`.
- `ical-export-guest-privacy` — the public iCal feed says when the property is taken, never who takes it. Code: `server/src/routes/ical.js`.
- `ical-export-closures` — establishment closures block their dates in the public iCal export (always on, anti-overbooking); incoming events covered by a closure are dropped. Code: `server/src/routes/ical.js`, `server/src/utils/icalClosedFeed.js`.
- `lodgify-decommission` — Booking, Airbnb, Abritel back on native iCal; echo filter, Booking tombstones, Lodgify row kept (`isDirectChannel`), runbook and audit rules. Code: `server/src/utils/icalBookingEcho.js`, `server/src/models/propertyIcalModel.js`.
- `platform-payment-entry` — « Paiement plateforme » block: operator types the platform's brut, taxe retenue, commissions and transfer; the brut pins the total stay. Code: `client/src/components/reservation/FinanceSection.jsx`, `server/src/utils/pricing/platformPayment.js`.
- `platform-payment-calculer-button` — « Calculer la commission » fills the solde commission on demand (client amount minus transfer); value stays editable. Code: `client/src/components/reservation/PlatformStatementEditor.jsx`, `server/src/utils/platformCommission.js`.
- `platform-commission-line` — operator-entered commission on non-direct platforms, net computed by the engine; summary shown as a cascade. Code: `server/src/utils/pricing/platformPayment.js`, `client/src/components/PricingSummary.jsx`.
- `platform-per-echeance-commission` — commission typed per échéance (acompte, solde), booked on the platform commission account; solde stores the gross. Code: `server/src/utils/platformCommission.js`, `server/src/utils/pricing/platformPayment.js`.
- `fiche-total-sejour-net-of-commission` — `sejourNetTotal` (net of commission plus complements) feeds « Total perçu sur le séjour » and finance totals. Code: `server/src/utils/pricing/compose.js`, `client/src/components/PricingSummary.jsx`.
- `platform-deposit-toggle` — per-platform « Acompte » Oui/Non (default Non): platform reservations use the normal acompte/solde split when Oui. Code: `server/src/models/platformsModel.js`, `server/src/utils/pricing/platformPayment.js`.
- `platform-payout-due-date` — OTA solde due `payoutDueDays` (default 10) after departure from booking time; Dashboard « Virement plateforme en retard » row. Code: `server/src/utils/platformPayout.js`, `server/src/models/platformsModel.js`.
- `platform-statement-after-payout` — platform statement (brut, taxe retenue, commissions) stays correctable after payout, as a reclassification; mismatch warns, never blocks. Code: `server/src/utils/platformStatementCorrection.js`, `client/src/components/reservation/PlatformStatementEditor.jsx`.
- `platform-extra-guest-complement` — guests added after platform amounts were entered add a supplement to the arrival complement, shown in the SAS recap, offerable. Code: `server/src/utils/platformPaidGuests.js`, `server/src/utils/pricing/extraGuests.js`.
- `platform-price-from-commission` — property tariff page shows the price to list per platform and season so the owner nets the season price after commission. Code: `server/src/utils/platformPriceCheck.js`, `client/src/components/PlatformPriceCard.jsx`.
- `booking-extranet-intake` — Booking intake plugin: userscript on the extranet page fills the fiche (guest, party, booking number, payment block). Code: `server/src/plugins/booking-intake/intakeService.js`, `server/src/plugins/booking-intake/pageParser.js`.
- `gites-de-france-mail-intake` — Gîtes de France intake plugin fills the fiche from the booking e-mail; says which figure it could not settle. Code: `server/src/plugins/gites-de-france-intake/intakeService.js`, `server/src/plugins/gites-de-france-intake/mailParser.js`.
- `airbnb-mail-intake` — Airbnb intake plugin fills the iCal placeholder fiche from the booking e-mail and reconciles the payout. Code: `server/src/plugins/airbnb-intake/intakeService.js`, `server/src/plugins/airbnb-intake/mailParser.js`.
