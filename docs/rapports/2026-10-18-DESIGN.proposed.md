# Design System: GuestFlow

> Proposed by the 2026-10-18 shell audit; not committed. The normative contract stays
> `specs/design-system-reference.md` and `client/src/theme.js`; this file is the portable summary that
> design tools (impeccable, Stitch) read. Qualitative wording below is inferred from `specs/design-system.md`
> (direction C « Maison », chosen 2026-07-06) and must be confirmed by Adrien.

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "La maison tenue"** — the calm of a well-kept guest house: everything in its place,
nothing raised above its importance.

An operations tool used by one operator, on a phone in the field as much as on a laptop. Restrained colour,
serif only for titles, sans for everything a user reads quickly or compares (amounts, dates, counts). Density
is medium: lists stay scannable, cards exist only when they group a task.

**Key characteristics:** fir-green as the single action colour · warm paper ground, white cards · serif titles,
tabular sans figures · one bar per page for every page-level action · soft-background status chips · motion
only to confirm a state change.

## Colors

### Primary
- **Fir** (#2F5D46): primary action (Save), selection, focus, today. Never decorative.

### Secondary
- **Honey** (#C99038): surfaces and decorative pictograms only. **2.8:1 on white: never text.**

### Neutral
- **Paper** (#F8F5EF): app ground. **Card** (#FFFFFF): cards, dialogs. **Ink** (#27251F) / **Ink muted** (#6E6A5E,
  4.97:1 on paper): text. **Hairline** (rgba(60,54,36,.1)): dividers and bar edges.

### Semantic
Each of success / warning / error / info has `.main` (icons, borders), `.soft` (chip and pastille background)
and, where `.main` fails 4.5:1 on `.soft`, a `.text` value (warning #6F5112, success #2F6A43).

**The One Action Colour Rule.** Fir marks what the user can do next. A screen with three fir elements has
two too many.

## Typography

**Display/titles:** Source Serif 4 (self-hosted). **Body, controls, figures:** Inter (self-hosted).

- **Page title** (600, 1.35rem / 1.2rem xs, -0.01em): one per page, rendered only by `PageActionBar`.
- **Section header** (600, 1.05rem, -0.005em): sections inside a page.
- **Body** (400, 1rem, 1.5).
- **KPI value** (700, 1.6rem, tabular figures). **KPI label** (600, 0.75rem, +0.01em, muted).

**The Figures Never Serif Rule.** Amounts, dates and counts are Inter with `tabular-nums`.

## Layout

Permanent 240 px drawer from `md` (900 px); temporary drawer below. Content max-widths 900 (forms) and 1240
(wide). Spacing multipliers 0.5, 1, 1.5, 2, 3 only. Page actions in the sticky `PageActionBar` under the
AppBar. Every screen verified at 390 / 900 / 1280. Coarse pointers get 44 px targets from the theme.

## Elevation & Depth

Flat chrome, lifted content. AppBar, drawer and the page bar carry a hairline, never a shadow; cards carry the
warm shadow `0 3px 16px rgba(60,54,36,.09)`. The page bar is a translucent material over the paper with a
hairline that appears only once content scrolls under it; opaque under `prefers-reduced-transparency`.

**The Flat Chrome Rule.** Shadows belong to content, not to navigation.

## Shapes

One radius, 14 px, for cards, inputs and bar buttons; pills for status chips. No square bands between rounded
cards.

## Components

`PageActionBar` (title, Save filled fir, Cancel bordered, extras bordered, destructive last), `PageTabs`,
`StatusBadge` (soft + text), `AttentionCard` (proposed: one recipe for every dashboard alert),
`LoadingState` (skeleton in lists), `EmptyState`, `ErrorAlert`, `useToast`, `ResponsiveTable`. Catalogue and
usage rules: `specs/design-system-reference.md` §6.

## Do's and Don'ts

- Do put the day's work first on the dashboard; alerts are a list, not a wall.
- Do gate every hover style behind `(hover: hover) and (pointer: fine)`; give touch an `:active` state.
- Do honour `prefers-reduced-motion`: cross-fade, never slide.
- Don't use honey for text, side-stripe borders as accents, or a filled semantic chip.
- Don't add a second shadow to chrome, or a new radius.
- Don't truncate navigation labels; wrap them or shorten the label.
